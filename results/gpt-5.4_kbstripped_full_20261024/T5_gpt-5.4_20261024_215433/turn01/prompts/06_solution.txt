The user's question:
Assess sea surface salinity (SSS) in three CMIP6 models: their mean-state bias against observations, their historical trend, and their end-of-century change under the ssp245 scenario.

Data to use:
- CMIP6 historical `so` from CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, monthly, 1950-01 to 2014-12.

- WOA23 salinity, the 1971-2000 climate normal, annual, objectively analysed.

SSS at each grid cell is the mean salinity of its levels whose depths lie within 0-10 m (WOA23: 0, 5 and 10 m; one or two levels per model), each level weighted by its full cell volume rather than by its overlap with 0-10 m, defined the same way for the models and for WOA23, reported in psu.

models = ["CESM2", "GFDL-ESM4", "MPI-ESM1-2-LR"]

def _get_hdims(da):
    lat_name = da.attrs.get("lat_coord", "lat")
    lon_name = da.attrs.get("lon_coord", "lon")
    if lat_name in da.dims and lon_name in da.dims:
        return [lat_name, lon_name]
    if "lat" in da.dims and "lon" in da.dims:
        return ["lat", "lon"]
    return [d for d in da.dims if d not in ("time", "year", "depth", "month", "season")]

def _to_psu(da):
    units = str(da.attrs.get("units", "")).lower()
    if units in ["psu", "1", "", "pss-78"]:
        out = da.copy()
        out.attrs["units"] = "psu"
        return out
    return transform_unit(da, from_unit=da.attrs.get("units", ""), to_unit="psu")

def _surface_from_fullcell_volume(data_3d, vol_3d):
    sss = compute_basic_stat(data_3d * vol_3d, op="sum", dim="depth") / compute_basic_stat(vol_3d, op="sum", dim="depth")
    sss = sss.assign_attrs(data_3d.attrs)
    sss.attrs["units"] = "psu"
    return sss
