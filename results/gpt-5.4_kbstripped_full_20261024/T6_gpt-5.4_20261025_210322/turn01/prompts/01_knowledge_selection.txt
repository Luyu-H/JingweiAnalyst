===== USER =====
User question: Assess the nitracline depth simulated by three CMIP6 models against the WOA23 observed nitrate climatology, globally and by ocean basin.

Data to use:
- CMIP6 historical `no3` from CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, monthly, 1971-01 to 2000-12, each on its own native grid with its own areacello.
- WOA23 nitrate, the all-time-periods annual climatology, objectively analysed, on the 1-degree grid.
- The WOA23 land-sea mask (landsea_01, ocean is value > 1) and basin mask (basinmask_01).

The nitracline is the depth of the maximum vertical gradient of nitrate concentration, searched over the levels within the top 1000 m only (the gradient at the deepest of these still uses the level just below 1000 m, so load the profile below 1000 m).

Expected numerical results:
- The global mean nitracline depth of each model (1971-2000 climatology) and of WOA23, in metres to the nearest whole metre, and each model's difference from WOA23.
- The mean nitracline depth in the Pacific, Atlantic and Indian Oceans (basins as defined by the WOA23 basin mask, marginal seas folded into their parent ocean; the Southern and Arctic Oceans are basins of their own and excluded) for each of the four sources, in metres to the nearest whole metre, and each model's difference from WOA23 in each basin.
- For each model, the spatial Pearson correlation with WOA23 to 3 decimal places and the RMSE in metres to 1 decimal place, on the WOA23 1-degree grid.
- Which model reproduces the observed nitracline best, and whether each model reproduces the observed ordering of nitracline depth across the three basins.

Expected visualizations:
- Figure 1, a 2-by-2 panel of global maps of nitracline depth for the three models and WOA23, all four on one shared sequential colour scale on which deeper is visually heavier.
- Figure 2, a 4-panel longitude-depth section of nitrate concentration through the equatorial Pacific averaged over 2S-2N, in the order WOA23, CESM2, GFDL-ESM4, MPI-ESM1-2-LR, covering 120E to 80W and the top 1000 m with depth increasing downward, all four panels on one shared colour scale in the same concentration unit, and each panel's own nitracline depth overlaid as a line.

Now output the fragments you choose.
