# Plan

1. Load the analysis inputs with `load_regular_data` for `cmip6_historical` (`o2`, `thetao`, `so`; GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR; monthly 1971-01 to 2000-12) and `cmip6_ssp245` (`o2`, `thetao`, `so`; CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR; monthly 2071-01 to 2100-12). Load WOA23 `oxygen`, `percent_oxygen_saturation` and `apparent_oxygen_utilization` with `load_climatology_data`; do not load CESM2 historical `o2`, which is not published.

2. Load the static geometry and masks: each model’s `volcello` with `load_volumes_for_cmip6`, each model’s `areacello` and WOA23 cell areas with `load_areas`, each model’s `deptho` and vertical levels with `load_regular_data`/`load_depth_levels` as appropriate, WOA23 `landsea_01` with `load_landsea_mask`, and WOA23 `basinmask_01` with `load_basin_mask`.

3. For CMIP6, compute monthly in-situ density from `thetao` and `so` using `compute_potential_density_anomaly` with `ref_pressure=None`, then convert `o2` from mol m-3 to µmol kg-1 before applying the 20 and 60 thresholds. Form period means with `temporal_aggregate(freq='mean')` over 1971-2000 for GFDL-ESM4/MPI-ESM1-2-LR historical and 2071-2100 for CESM2/GFDL-ESM4/MPI-ESM1-2-LR ssp245; apply OMZ thresholds to the period-mean converted oxygen fields, not to monthly thresholded fields. Also form period means of the raw `o2` for the inventory.
   - Note [cmip6-bgc-units-per-volume]: Convert model `o2` from mol m-3 to µmol kg-1 before applying the 20 and 60 thresholds: µmol kg-1 = mmol m-3 × 1000 / ρ, with in-situ ρ from `compute_potential_density_anomaly` with `ref_pressure=None` (not the `transform_unit` constant default); report `attrs['density_assumption']`. The oxygen inventory in mol needs no density: use Σ `o2` · `volcello` on the volume basis via `convert_concentration_to_content`, not a µmol kg-1 field.
   - Note [monthly-means-are-time-averages]: Threshold exceedance is nonlinear; state the order of operations used. For like-for-like comparison with the observational climatology, average the monthly fields over each period with `temporal_aggregate(freq='mean')`, which weights by calendar month length; `compute_basic_stat(op='mean', dim='time')` weights every month equally and is not a time average.

4. For each of the six source-and-experiment combinations (WOA23; GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR historical; CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR ssp245), use `identify_oxygen_minimum_zone` on the period-mean oxygen field to derive OMZ column thickness at the 20 and 60 thresholds, and use `compute_exceedance_fraction` with volume weights for the total volume strictly below `<20` and `<60` µmol kg-1, converting m3 to 1e15 m3. At the 60 threshold, apply `locate_extreme` to the OMZ thickness field to report the maximum thickness and its latitude/longitude. For CMIP6 use `volcello/areacello` cell thickness; for WOA23 use its depth-level geometry.
   - Note [cmip6-static-grid-fields]: OMZ volume = Σ `volcello` over cells below the threshold; column OMZ thickness = Σ `cell_thickness_like` (= `volcello / areacello`) over those cells. Do not take thickness from `lev_bnds` differences, which fail at partial bottom cells. `deptho` is model bathymetry (missing over land), not true topography; use it, not an external data set, to decide what is above the sea floor.
   - Note [omip-weighted-means-and-integrals]: Volume-weight every 3D mean and integral, restricting weights to cells with valid data; `compute_exceedance_fraction` returns the volume (m3) behind the percentage. State the volume-weighted (Boussinesq) assumption per model; in-situ density varies only ~2 % about 1035 kg m-3.
   - Note [cmip6-o2sat-and-derived-oxygen]: `identify_oxygen_minimum_zone` compares the field with the threshold in the field’s own units, so model `o2` must be converted to µmol kg-1 first. WOA23 AOU and percent saturation rest on a solubility/pressure convention the model OMIP values may not share: state that observed-vs-model AOU/saturation differences include a definitional component, and give AOU in µmol kg-1.

Use the raw volume-basis field, not the converted µmol kg-1 field, per [cmip6-bgc-units-per-volume].

Apply the basin selection before `compute_exceedance_fraction` and report 1e15 m3 to 3 significant figures.
   - Note [basin-and-region-definitions]: Basin volumes use the WOA23 basin mask via `select_basin`/`load_basin_mask` with `include_marginal_seas=True` and surface labels applied through the column (`depth_dependent=False`); state that Southern and Arctic are separate basins and excluded, and that the Ofx `basin` field is absent locally so OMIP’s 10-region labels cannot be used.
