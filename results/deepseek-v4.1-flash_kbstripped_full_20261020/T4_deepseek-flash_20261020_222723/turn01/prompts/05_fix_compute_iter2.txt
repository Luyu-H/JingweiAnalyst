The problem: `op_mm` has been pre-subset to a **single calendar month**, so it contains only one distinct month. `temporal_aggregate(freq='climatology')` builds a `month` dimension of size 1 but then tries to attach the full 12-element `month_names` coordinate → `AlignmentError: conflicting dimension sizes {1, 12}`.
