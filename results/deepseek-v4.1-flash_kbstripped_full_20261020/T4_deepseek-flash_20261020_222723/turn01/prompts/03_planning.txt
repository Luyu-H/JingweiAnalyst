[cmip6-mlotst-definition] (must)
- Operator criterion: displaced surface parcel compared at local pressure, δB = −g(ρ_displaced − ρ_local)/ρ_local; MLD = first depth where δB > ΔB_crit = 0.0003 m s-2 (Δρ_crit = 0.03 kg m-3), interpolated between the bracketing levels; not the KPP boundary-layer depth.
- Pass `ref_depth` = the model's shallowest level to `compute_mixed_layer_depth`; exclude columns where the threshold is never reached (GFDL/MPI fill roughly full column, CESM2 often 0 m) and report that excluded area; the operator is expected shallower than `mlotst`, so state the three differences (reference level, displaced-parcel vs σ0, time averaging) instead of calling the residual model error.

[monthly-means-are-time-averages] (must)
- f(monthly mean) ≠ monthly mean of f: compute the MLD of each March/September profile first, then average over the 30 instances; the MLD of an averaged profile is systematically shallower.
- Averaging the 30 monthly means of one calendar month is a plain mean over years, with no day weights.

[common-comparison-mask] (must)
