# Plan

1. Load the sources. Use `load_regular_data` for `cmip6_historical` with models CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR: `dissic`, `talk`, `ph`, `thetao`, `so` monthly for 2002, and `dissic`, `thetao`, `so` monthly for 1971-2000. Use `load_climatology_data` for `glodap` `TCO2`, `TAlk` and `pH` (`pHtsinsitutp`). Load each model’s `areacello` with `load_areas`, `volcello` with `load_volumes_for_cmip6`, and depth levels/layer boundaries with `load_depth_levels`.
   - Note [missing-values-and-land-cells]: read through xarray so the 1e20 fill becomes NaN; build each field’s valid mask from the field itself and apply that same mask to its weights; a value near 1e20 is an unmasked fill, not a result.
   - Note [cmip6-carbonate-system-definitions]: `dissic` is total dissolved inorganic carbon, `talk` is total alkalinity with the OMIP terms, and `ph` is in-situ pH on the TOTAL scale; GLODAP `pHtsinsitutp` is also total-scale in-situ pH.

2. Aggregate time and convert units. Use `temporal_aggregate` to form the 2002 annual means of `dissic`, `talk`, `ph`, `thetao`, `so`, and the 1971-2000 day-weighted long-term means of `dissic`, `thetao`, `so` for each model. For model `dissic` and `talk`, compute in-situ density from `thetao`/`so` with `compute_potential_density_anomaly(ref_pressure=None)`, then use `transform_unit` to convert to micromoles per kilogram with that density; record `attrs['density_assumption']`. GLODAP `TCO2`/`TAlk` are already micromoles per kilogram; pH needs no unit conversion.
   - Note [cmip6-bgc-units-per-volume]: convert model `dissic`/`talk` as µmol kg-1 = mmol m-3 × 1000 / rho using in-situ density from `compute_potential_density_anomaly` with `ref_pressure=None`; do not treat GLODAP values converted by mol m-3 × 1e3 as µmol kg-1 without dividing by density.

3. Compute the 0-50 m global means. For each model and for GLODAP, use `compute_basic_stat` with `op='volume_weighted_mean'` over all 0-50 m cells for DIC/TCO2, TAlk and pH, using each source’s own cell measures (CMIP6 `areacello`/`volcello`; GLODAP area and layer thickness). Form each model-minus-GLODAP difference for DIC, TAlk and pH.
   - Note [omip-weighted-means-and-integrals]: use `volume_weighted_mean` for means whose dimensions include depth and `area_weighted_mean` for horizontal means; `compute_difference_metrics` weights by area by default and `analyze_correlation` needs `weighting='area'`; a plain lat-lon mean over-weights polar cells.
   - Note [omip-native-grid-vs-regridded]: compute the global means and the 2002-minus-(1971-2000) DIC change on each model’s own grid with its own cell measures; regrid only for the map comparison using `'conservative'`, state the method, and interpolate the five sampled depths with `regrid_vertical`, not by nearest-level selection.

4. Build the 0-50 m DIC map comparison. For each model and GLODAP, compute the per-cell 0-50 m mean of DIC/TCO2 with `compute_basic_stat(op='volume_weighted_mean')` over depth only. Use `regrid_horizontal` to put each model field on the GLODAP 1-degree grid with `'conservative'`. Use `build_comparison_mask` across the model and GLODAP maps with `ocean_only=False`, and report the mask cell counts. On that common mask, use `compute_difference_metrics` for model-minus-GLODAP mean bias and RMSE, and `analyze_correlation` with `weighting='area'` for spatial correlation.
   - Note [common-comparison-mask]: put all fields on the GLODAP 1-degree grid and apply one shared mask from `build_comparison_mask` before the map bias/RMSE/correlation; the reference is GLODAP, so pass `ocean_only=False`; report the mask cell counts; the difference of the separately taken per-source 0-50 m means is not this shared-mask map bias; for the vertical profile the stated pairwise depth mask applies and those numbers must not be ranked.

5. Compute the vertical DIC bias profile. For each model 2002 annual mean DIC and GLODAP TCO2, use `regrid_vertical` to interpolate both products to 0, 100, 500, 1000 and 2000 m, following [omip-native-grid-vs-regridded]. Use `regrid_horizontal` to put the model profiles on the GLODAP 1-degree grid with `'conservative'`. At each depth, build the pairwise valid mask where model and GLODAP are both defined, and use `compute_difference_metrics` (area-weighted by default) for the global mean model-minus-GLODAP bias over those cells; retain the values for Figure 2.

6. Compute the DIC period change and ratio. On each model’s native grid with its own cell measures ([omip-native-grid-vs-regridded]), compute the global 0-50 m volume-weighted mean DIC for the 2002 annual mean and for the 1971-2000 long-term mean, then take 2002 minus 1971-2000. Use `compute_ratio` to divide this change by that model’s 0-50 m DIC difference from GLODAP in step 3. Interpret the ratio: a value of order one or larger means the temporal change is comparable to or larger than the bias, so the 2002 bias cannot be read simply as a static model error; a small ratio leaves the bias as the more likely model-error signal.

7. Synthesize. Compare the absolute DIC bias/RMSE/correlation and the TAlk and pH 0-50 m differences across CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR; identify the model with the smallest overall carbon-system errors and state whether the signs of the model-minus-GLODAP differences in DIC, TAlk and pH agree across the three models. Keep unrounded intermediate values and round only at reporting.

8. Make Figure 1 from the step 4 bias maps: three panels, one per model, shared diverging colour scale centred on zero, labelled in µmol kg-1. Make Figure 2 from the step 5 profiles: depth downward, bias on the horizontal axis, one line per model, a vertical zero line, and the five sampled depths marked. Plotting is own code because no listed tool covers figures.

Other notes:
- [cmip6-grid-labels] GFDL-ESM4 is `gr`: its “native” values are already remapped to a 180×360 1-degree archive grid with a regridded land–sea mask, so its budgets are only approximately conservative.
- The pH comparison is on the total scale in situ; no pH unit conversion is applied.
- The step 6 ratio contrasts a 2002-minus-1971-2000 temporal change with a 2002-minus-GLODAP bias; it is diagnostic of whether the bias could be period/background related, not proof of cause.
- Report the common-mask cell counts with Figure 1 and state that the step 3 global 0-50 m means use each source’s own valid cells.
