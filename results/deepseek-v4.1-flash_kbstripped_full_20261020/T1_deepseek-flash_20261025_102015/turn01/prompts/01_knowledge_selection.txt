===== USER =====
User question: Assess the ENSO amplitude simulated by three CMIP6 historical runs against ERSSTv5 observations over 1950-01 to 2014-12.

Data to use:
- CMIP6 historical variable `tos` from CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, monthly, 1950-01 to 2014-12, each on its own native grid with its own published cell areas.
- ERSSTv5 monthly SST over the same window, on its native 2-degree grid.

Expected numerical results, based on the monthly Nino 3.4 index of each of the four sources over 1950-01 to 2014-12: the SST anomaly over 5S-5N, 170W-120W, each source relative to a 1950-2014 monthly climatology. Standard deviations are population standard deviations (ddof = 0).
- For each source, the standard deviation of its Nino 3.4 anomaly over all 780 months, in degrees Celsius to 2 decimal places.
- For each model, the amplitude bias (model minus ERSSTv5 standard deviation) in degrees Celsius to 2 decimal places and the amplitude ratio (model divided by ERSSTv5) to 2 decimal places, and the ranking of the three models from smallest to largest absolute amplitude bias.
- For each source, the standard deviation of the anomaly for each of the 12 calendar months, in degrees Celsius to 2 decimal places (48 values).
- For each source, the calendar month of largest standard deviation and its value, the calendar month of smallest standard deviation, and the largest-to-smallest ratio to 2 decimal places; and whether each model reproduces the observed peak month and strength of seasonal phase locking.
- For each source, the number and the percentage (to 1 decimal place) of months whose Nino 3.4 anomaly strictly exceeds 1.0 degrees Celsius in absolute value.

Expected visualizations:
- Figure 1, a line plot of the seasonal cycle of amplitude: calendar month (January to December) on the x axis, standard deviation in degrees Celsius on the y axis, four curves on one axis, the observations visually distinct from the three models, and each curve's peak month marked.
- Figure 2, four stacked panels sharing one time axis over 1950-2014, in the order ERSSTv5, CESM2, GFDL-ESM4, MPI-ESM1-2-LR, each showing that source's Nino 3.4 anomaly series on an identical y-axis range that contains every series, with the +1.0 and -1.0 degrees Celsius levels marked and each panel annotated with its standard deviation and exceedance percentage.
