**Approach.** Monthly data were used over **1950-01 to 2014-12**: ERSSTv5 `sst`, CMIP6 historical `tos` for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, and the published `cpc_indices` `pdo` series. Each SST/`tos` field was regridded to the 1° grid with `regrid_horizontal(..., target="1deg", method="bilinear")`. Monthly anomalies were computed with `compute_anomaly_vs_own_climatology(..., freq="month", base_period=1950-01–2014-12, method="subtract")`, i.e. relative to each source’s own 1950–2014 monthly climatology. The PDO mode was derived with `compute_pdo_index(..., depth_level=None, standardize=True)`. Amplitude is the area-weighted RMS of each regression pattern, computed by squaring the pattern, applying `compute_basic_stat(op="area_weighted_mean")` over lat/lon, and taking the square root. Persistence is the lag-12 autocorrelation from `analyze_correlation(var1=index, var2=index, dim="time", lag=12)`. Pattern correlations are Pearson correlations over shared ocean cells from `build_comparison_mask(..., ocean_only=True)`, with no spatial weighting. The 9-year running mean used `temporal_aggregate(freq="rolling", op="mean", window=108, center=True)`. Bilinear regridding was prescribed; regridded explained variance and pattern correlations depend on that choice. CMIP6 `tos` is a bulk top-cell temperature with model-dependent thickness (CESM2 0–10 m, GFDL-ESM4 0–2 m, MPI-ESM1-2-LR 0–12 m), and sea-ice-covered cells are not missing, limiting exact amplitude comparability. GFDL-ESM4 is on a 180×360 1° archive grid, whereas CESM2 (~100 km) and MPI (~250 km) differ in native resolution.

The ERSSTv5-derived standardized PDO index correlates with the published PDO series at **r = 0.962** over 1950–2014 (n = 780). This demonstrates strong agreement in temporal variability and phase, including the shared trend. It does **not** demonstrate absolute amplitude agreement, independent validation, or causality; the published PDO is dimensionless and independently baselined, so only variability/phase is directly comparable. No detrending was applied.

The 108-month centred running mean of the ERSSTv5 index is valid only from **1954-07 to 2010-07**, because a full window is required; sign-change detection is therefore limited near the 1950 and 2014 ends. Applying the sign-change rule and merging crossings less than 3 years after the last counted transition gives detected transition years **1961, 1976, and 2002**. Of the documented examples, **1976 corresponds to the 1976/77 Pacific climate shift**; **1988/89 and 1998/99 are not identified** as sign changes by this smoothed-index procedure. The additional detected years are 1961 and 2002.

**Model assessment.** **CESM2 reproduces the observed spatial PDO pattern best** (pattern r = 0.928), followed by GFDL-ESM4 (0.893) and MPI-ESM1-2-LR (0.767). Amplitude and persistence are not uniformly consistent with observations: CESM2 has slightly higher amplitude (0.364 vs 0.330 °C s.d.⁻¹) but lower lag-12 persistence (0.223 vs 0.342); GFDL-ESM4 has lower amplitude (0.291) and near-observed persistence (0.305); MPI-ESM1-2-LR has higher amplitude (0.409) and higher persistence (0.425). Thus the model with the best spatial pattern is not the best persistence match, and the models do not all reproduce observed amplitude and persistence.
