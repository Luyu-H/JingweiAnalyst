===== USER =====
User question: Assess the Pacific Decadal Oscillation (PDO) simulated by three CMIP6 models against ERSSTv5 observations, over 1950-01 to 2014-12.

Data to use:
- CMIP6 historical `tos` from CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, monthly, 1950-01 to 2014-12.
- ERSSTv5 monthly SST over the same window.
- The published PDO index (product `pdo` in the index registry).

The PDO is the leading EOF of monthly North Pacific SST anomalies over 20N-70N, 110E-100W, relative to a 1950-2014 monthly climatology.

Expected numerical results, for each of the four sources over 1950-2014, with any regridding done by bilinear interpolation onto the 1-degree grid:
- The percentage of variance explained by the leading mode, to 1 decimal place.
- The amplitude of the mode, in degrees Celsius per standard deviation, to 3 decimal places.
- The lag-1-year (12-month) autocorrelation of the monthly standardized index, to 3 decimal places.
- The Pearson correlation of the ERSSTv5-derived index with the published PDO series, to 3 decimal places, and what that correlation does and does not demonstrate.
- For each model, the spatial Pearson correlation between its regression pattern and the ERSSTv5 regression pattern, to 3 decimal places.
- The observed PDO phase-transition years over 1950-2014, defined as the years in which the 9-year (108-month) centred running mean of the ERSSTv5 index changes sign (a crossing less than 3 years after the last counted transition is merged into it, keeping the earlier year), and which of them correspond to documented Pacific climate shifts.
- Which model reproduces the observed pattern best, and whether the models' amplitude and persistence are consistent with observations.

Expected visualizations:
- Figure 1, a 2-by-2 panel of the regression pattern over the PDO domain, in the order ERSSTv5, CESM2, GFDL-ESM4, MPI-ESM1-2-LR, on one diverging colour scale centred on zero labelled in degrees Celsius per standard deviation, each panel annotated with its explained variance and, for the models, its pattern correlation with observations.
- Figure 2, a single time-series panel of the standardized index of all four sources plus the published PDO series as a distinguishable fifth line, with the 9-year smoothed ERSSTv5 curve and the transition years marked, and the y axis labelled as a standardized index.
