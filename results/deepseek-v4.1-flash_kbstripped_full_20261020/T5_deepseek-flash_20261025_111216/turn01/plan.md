# Plan

1. **WOA23 SSS (observed reference).** Load WOA23 `salinity` for the 1971-2000 climate normal with `load_climatology_data` (annual, 1° grid); load its depth levels and layer boundaries with `load_depth_levels`; load the 1° cell areas with `load_areas`; load the WOA23 1° land-sea mask (`landsea_01`) with `load_landsea_mask` to keep only ocean cells. Build WOA23 SSS at each cell as the volume-weighted mean of the levels whose depths lie within 0-10 m (0, 5 and 10 m), each selected level weighted by its *full* cell volume (= cell area × that level's layer thickness from the level boundaries), not by its overlap with the 0-10 m band. No listed tool performs this level selection, so it is coded directly. Keep the result on the native 1° grid.
   - Note [cmip6-so-practical-salinity]: `so` and WOA23 `salinity` are both practical salinity (stored units "0.001" vs "1" denote the same magnitude ~35); difference them directly with no ×0.001 scaling and label all results psu.

2. **Model SSS, historical.** For CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR: load monthly `so` for 1950-01 to 2014-12 with `load_regular_data`; load each model's depth levels/boundaries with `load_depth_levels`, its `volcello` with `load_volumes_for_cmip6`, and its `areacello` with `load_areas`. Compute SSS at every cell and month exactly as in step 1: mean of the model levels whose depths lie within 0-10 m (one or two levels), each weighted by that level's full `volcello`. Then form annual means of the SSS field with `temporal_aggregate` (annual), on each model's own grid.

3. **Model SSS, ssp245.** Same as step 2 for the ssp245 `so` over 2071-01 to 2100-12 (same three models, same own grids, same `volcello`/`areacello`/level definitions), producing annual-mean SSS fields for 2071-2100.

4. **1971-2000 mean state on native grids.** From the annual historical SSS series of each model, take the 1971-2000 long-term mean with `temporal_aggregate` (day-weighted long-term mean). Compute the global mean 1971-2000 SSS for each model and for WOA23 on its own grid, using `compute_basic_stat` with `op='area_weighted_mean'` (NaN-aware weights over valid cells only) — never a plain mean. Report each model's and WOA23's value to 2 dp and each model's difference from WOA23 to 2 dp.
   - Note [omip-weighted-means-and-integrals]: global and basin mean SSS = Σ SSS·areacello / Σ areacello over valid cells only, via `compute_basic_stat` with `op='area_weighted_mean'`.
   - Note [omip-native-grid-vs-regridded]: compute all reported numbers (global and basin mean SSS, ΔSSS, global-mean trends) on each model's own grid with its own `areacello`; use the WOA 1° grid only for the bias/RMSE maps and the pattern correlation, and state that the regridding method is 'conservative'.

5. **Bias, RMSE and pattern correlation vs WOA23.** Regrid each model's 1971-2000 SSS climatology to the WOA23 1° grid with `regrid_horizontal` (method `conservative`). Build one common scoring set with `build_comparison_mask` over the three regridded climatologies plus WOA23 (default `ocean_only=True`, WOA23 as reference) and apply it with `.where(mask)` to every field before differencing. Compute the three bias maps (model − WOA23) for Figure 1, the RMSE and largest departure with `compute_difference_metrics` (area-weighted by default; RMSE reported to 2 dp) and the spatial Pearson correlation with `analyze_correlation` using `weighting='area'` (reported to 3 dp). Report the cell count recorded by the mask alongside these metrics.
   - Note [common-comparison-mask]: all three models and WOA23 go on the one 1° grid, one `build_comparison_mask` mask applied with `.where(mask)` to every field before the bias maps, RMSE and pattern correlation; report the mask's cell count, since a mask much smaller than the individual fields means the products disagree about where the ocean is, not that the models are poor.

6. **Historical trend of the global mean SSS.** From the annual global-mean SSS series (area-weighted, step 4 operator) of each model over 1950-2014, fit a linear trend with `fit_1D_trend`, reporting psu per decade to 3 significant figures with its 95 % confidence interval, its R², and whether the interval excludes zero.
   - Note [serial-correlation-and-significance]: `fit_1D_trend` and `fit_trend_map` apply an effective sample size along time by default — quote the corrected confidence interval and p, and call a trend significant only if the interval excludes zero.

7. **ssp245 trend of the global mean SSS.** Fit the same trend with `fit_1D_trend` to each model's annual global-mean SSS over 2071-2100, reported identically (psu per decade to 3 s.f., 95 % CI, R², whether the CI excludes zero), with the same effective-sample-size correction as in step 6 [serial-correlation-and-significance].

8. **Slope ratio.** For each model, form the ratio of the ssp245 slope (step 7) to the historical slope (step 6), unrounded inputs, reported to 1 dp, and state whether that ratio is meaningful given that it compares a 65-year historical rate with a 30-year late-century scenario rate over different periods, forcings and (for CESM2) different ensemble members; the two segments are never fitted as one series across the 2014/2015 join.

9. **Cell-by-cell historical trend (Figure 2).** Apply `fit_trend_map` to each model's annual SSS field over 1950-2014 on its own native grid; regrid the trend field with `regrid_horizontal` (method `conservative`) onto a common regular longitude-latitude frame for display only, use one shared diverging scale centred on zero, and stipple the cells whose corrected p < 0.05 [serial-correlation-and-significance]. The stippling decision is made on each model's own grid before the display regrid.

10. **End-of-century change ΔSSS.** From the annual SSS fields, take the historical 1995-2014 mean and the ssp245 2071-2100 mean with `temporal_aggregate`, and difference them (ssp245 minus historical) on each model's own grid. Compute the global area-weighted mean ΔSSS (`compute_basic_stat`, `op='area_weighted_mean'`) and the Atlantic, Indian and Pacific basin means with `select_basin`, using the WOA23 basin mask (`load_basin_mask`; regrid the numeric basin codes to each model grid with `regrid_horizontal`, nearest neighbour, for the mask only so that the selection matches the native field). Report ΔSSS globally and per basin to 2 dp.
   - Note [historical-ssp245-member-continuity]: CESM2 ssp245 is r10i1p1f1 (parent historical r10i1p1f1) but only historical r1 is local, so the CESM2 ΔSSS contains forced change plus the internal-variability difference between two members — state this caveat; GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR are r1/r1 and continuous. Do not fit a trend across the 2014/2015 join for CESM2.
   - Note [basin-and-region-definitions]: basin means use the WOA23 mask via `select_basin`/`load_basin_mask` with marginal seas folded into their parent ocean; state that this follows WOA labelling (not the OMIP 10-region one) and that the Southern and Arctic Oceans are separate basins and are excluded here; note that marginal seas in the WOA mask may have no model counterpart, so the cells behind a basin mean can differ between sources.

11. **Figures.** Figure 1: three global maps in one row (one per model) of the step-5 bias field on the step-5 comparison cells, one shared diverging colour scale centred on zero, colour bar labelled psu. Figure 2: three global maps in one row of the step-9 trend field on the common display grid, one shared diverging scale centred on zero, psu per decade, with the p < 0.05 cells stippled. Figure 3: one time-series panel of the global-mean SSS of all three models, historical 1950-2014 and ssp245 2071-2100 drawn as separate segments with the 2015-2070 gap left empty, each segment overlaid with its own fitted line from steps 6-7, y axis in psu; each model keeps one colour across all three figures.

Other notes:
- [cmip6-so-practical-salinity] All SSS values, biases and ΔSSS are practical salinity reported in psu; no unit rescaling is applied between `so` and WOA23 `salinity`.
- [omip-native-grid-vs-regridded] A regridded RMSE or pattern correlation depends on the conservative regridding used; the cell-by-cell trend is computed on each model's own grid and the Figure 2 map is a regridded copy for display only.
- [serial-correlation-and-significance] There is no such correction for the spatial pattern correlation — read its coefficient, not its p-value.
- The 1971-2000 normal of WOA23 is the reference period throughout; no 1991-2020 normal is used.
