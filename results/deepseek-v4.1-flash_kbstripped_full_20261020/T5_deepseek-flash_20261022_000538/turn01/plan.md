# Plan

1. Load the model fields. `load_regular_data` on cmip6_historical, variable `so`, models CESM2, GFDL-ESM4, MPI-ESM1-2-LR, monthly, 1950-01 to 2014-12 (surface levels only need to be retained, but load the full column as the tool returns it); `load_volumes_for_cmip6` and `load_areas` for each of the three models (Ofx `volcello`/`areacello`) to supply each model's own cell volumes and areas.
2. Load the future fields. `load_regular_data` on cmip6_ssp245, variable `so`, same three models, monthly, 2071-01 to 2100-12, with each model's own `volcello`/`areacello` via `load_volumes_for_cmip6` and `load_areas` from the ssp245 grid definitions.
3. Load the observations and masks. `load_climatology_data` on woa23 for `salinity`, climate normal 1971-2000, annual; `load_landsea_mask` for WOA23 `landsea_01` (ocean where value > 1); `load_basin_mask` for WOA23 `basinmask_01`; and `load_depth_levels` for both woa23 and each CMIP6 model so the 0–10 m level selection uses each product's own depths and layer boundaries.
4. Build SSS for every product and model: at each cell take the mean of the salinity of the levels whose depths lie within 0–10 m (WOA23: 0, 5, 10 m; models: the one or two levels that fall in that range), weighting each retained level by that level's full cell volume (never by its overlap with 0–10 m), and apply this identical rule to WOA23 and to all three models. Volumes come from each model's `volcello`; for WOA23 the level volume is the cell area from `load_areas` times the layer thickness between that level's boundaries from `load_depth_levels` (the area factor cancels in a within-cell weighted mean). Report SSS in psu.
   - Note [cmip6-static-grid-fields]: weight levels by `volcello`/`areacello` rather than by `lev_bnds` differences, and record the `attrs['area_source']` and `attrs['volume_source']` strings for reporting; state the area each global mean is taken over rather than rescaling one model to another.
   - Note [cmip6-so-practical-salinity]: `so` is practical salinity with units string `0.001` and stored values of order 35; difference it directly against WOA23 salinity with no multiplication by 0.001 or 1000, and report in psu.
5. Put the mean states on a common grid and score them: time-mean the historical SSS over 1971-01 to 2000-12 with `temporal_aggregate` (day-weighted long-term mean) to give each model's 1971–2000 SSS climatology; `regrid_horizontal` each model's SSS onto the WOA23 1° grid; `build_comparison_mask` with `ocean_only=True` over the WOA23 land-sea mask cells at this level and `.where(mask)` each regridded field and the WOA23 field; then `compute_difference_metrics` for bias and RMSE (psu, 2 dp) and `analyze_correlation` with `weighting='area'` for the spatial Pearson correlation (3 dp).
   - Note [common-comparison-mask]: build one mask on the WOA23 1° grid from the fields at the 0–10 m-derived level used here, apply it to every field before bias/RMSE/correlation, and report the mask's cell counts together with `mean_data`/`mean_reference` from `compute_difference_metrics` rather than means over separately masked cells.
6. Global mean 1971–2000 SSS: apply `compute_basic_stat` with `op='area_weighted_mean'` over (y, x) to each model's 1971–2000 SSS climatology using its own `areacello`, and to the WOA23 1971–2000 SSS on its own areas; report each to 2 dp and each model minus WOA23 to 2 dp, stating the ocean area each mean was taken over.
   - Note [omip-weighted-means-and-integrals]: use Σ SSS·areacello / Σ areacello with NaN-aware weights on each product's native grid (never an unweighted mean over y, x on curvilinear grids or over latitude on regular ones), and use `weighting='area'` for the pattern correlation already requested in step 5.
7. Annual global-mean SSS series and historical trends: for each model, `temporal_aggregate` the SSS field to annual means, then `compute_basic_stat` (`area_weighted_mean`) over space for each year, giving one global series for historical 1950–2014 and one for ssp245 2071–2100; fit `fit_1D_trend` to the historical 1950–2014 series and to the ssp245 2071–2100 series separately, reporting slope in psu per decade to 3 significant figures with its 95 % confidence interval, R², and whether the interval excludes zero; also form the ratio ssp245 slope / historical slope to 1 dp.
   - Note [serial-correlation-and-significance]: quote the effective-sample-size-corrected slope with its 95 % confidence interval, call a trend significant only when that interval excludes zero, and do not fit any trend across the 2014/2015 join.
   - Note [historical-ssp245-member-continuity]: CESM2 historical is r1i1p1f1 while its ssp245 is r10i1p1f1 (parent r10i1p1f1), i.e. different realisations, whereas GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR are r1→r1 continuous trajectories; state the members used per model when discussing the ratio.
8. End-of-century change ΔSSS: for each model compute the ssp245 2071–2100 mean SSS minus the historical 1995–2014 mean SSS, on the native grid, then the area-weighted global mean ΔSSS with `compute_basic_stat`; then use `select_basin` with the WOA23 `basinmask_01` (via `load_basin_mask`) to repeat the area-weighted mean ΔSSS for the Atlantic, Indian and Pacific basins, reporting each to 2 dp.
   - Note [basin-and-region-definitions]: use the WOA23 basin mask through `select_basin`/`load_basin_mask` (not the OMIP Ofx `basin`), state that the mask was WOA23, that `include_marginal_seas=True` folds each marginal sea into its parent ocean, that the Southern and Arctic Oceans are excluded as separate basins, and whether labels were applied through the column (`depth_dependent=False`) or level-wise.
   - [historical-ssp245-member-continuity] applies to ΔSSS: for CESM2 the difference carries forced change plus an internal-variability difference between r1i1p1f1 and r10i1p1f1, and the local archive holds no CESM2 historical r10; for GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR the two experiments are the same realisation, so ΔSSS is a cleaner forced-change estimate.
9. Figure 1: three global maps in one row of SSS bias (model 1971–2000 climatology minus WOA23) drawn only on the step-5 comparison-mask cells, one diverging colour scale centred on zero shared by all three panels, colour bar labelled in psu (data: woa23 and each model's historical SSS from steps 3–5).
10. Figure 2: for each model, `fit_trend_map` on the annual-mean SSS over 1950–2014 (from step 7's annual field) to get the cell-by-cell linear trend in psu per decade; `regrid_horizontal` the trend and its significance field onto a regular longitude-latitude grid for display only, plot three panels on a common longitude-latitude frame with one shared diverging colour scale centred on zero, and stipple the p < 0.05 cells on top of the trend field.
    - [serial-correlation-and-significance] applies: `fit_trend_map` uses the effective sample size along time, so stipple p < 0.05 cells rather than blanking insignificant ones.
11. Figure 3: one time-series panel of the three models' global-mean SSS from step 7, with the historical 1950–2014 and ssp245 2071–2100 segments drawn separately and the 2015–2070 gap left empty, each segment overlaid with its own `fit_1D_trend` fitted line, y axis labelled in psu, and each model in the colour used in Figures 1 and 2.

Other notes:
- [common-comparison-mask] The bias, RMSE and correlation are all scored on the same WOA23 1° comparison cells; report the cell count and the two reference means from `compute_difference_metrics`.
- [cmip6-static-grid-fields] Report the recorded `area_source`/`volume_source` and the ocean area behind each global mean, and note that total ocean area differs by about a per cent between models.
- [basin-and-region-definitions] Marginal seas are folded into their parent ocean by the WOA23 mask; the Southern and Arctic Oceans are excluded as separate basins, and marginal seas in the WOA mask may have no model counterpart.
- [historical-ssp245-member-continuity] The ssp245/historical slope ratio mixes two different CESM2 realisations and is therefore at best indicative, while for GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR it compares one continuous trajectory; also note that the two trend windows are separated by a 56-year gap, so the ratio is a scale comparison rather than a rate of change.
- [cmip6-so-practical-salinity] All salinity numbers are practical salinity in psu; no Absolute Salinity conversion is made because no TEOS-10 routine is called.
