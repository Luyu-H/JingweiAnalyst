# Plan

1. **WOA23 SSS reference field.** Load with `load_climatology_data` the WOA23 salinity for the 1971-2000 climate normal, annual; load its layer boundaries with `load_depth_levels`, its cell areas with `load_areas`, the 1-degree land-sea mask with `load_landsea_mask` (landsea_01, ocean = value > 1) and the basin mask with `load_basin_mask` (basinmask_01). Compute WOA23 SSS per cell as the volume-weighted mean of salinity over the levels whose depths lie within 0-10 m (the 0, 5 and 10 m levels), each weighted by its full cell volume (area x full layer thickness from the level boundaries), not by its overlap with 0-10 m, in psu.

2. **Model SSS, historical.** Load with `load_regular_data` the CMIP6 historical `so` for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, monthly 1950-01 to 2014-12, with each model's `areacello` and `volcello` via `load_volumes_for_cmip6` and its levels via `load_depth_levels`. Compute each model's monthly SSS on its own native grid as the volume-weighted mean of `so` over the model levels whose depths lie within 0-10 m (one or two levels per model), each weighted by its full-cell `volcello`, in psu.
   - Note [cmip6-static-grid-fields]: take each level's thickness as `volcello / areacello` (partial bottom cells respected), not the `lev_bnds` difference, when deciding which levels fall in 0-10 m.
   - Note [cmip6-so-practical-salinity]: model `so` and WOA23 salinity are both practical salinity on the same scale; no conversion before the level averaging, the differencing or the psu labelling.

3. **Model SSS, ssp245.** Same as step 2 with `load_regular_data` on cmip6_ssp245 `so` for the three models, monthly 2071-01 to 2100-12, using each model's own `areacello`/`volcello` and the same 0-10 m volume-weighted definition.

4. **Global mean 1971-2000 SSS.** Build each model's 1971-2000 SSS climatology with `temporal_aggregate` (day-weighted long-term mean over 1971-01 to 2000-12 of the monthly SSS). Compute the global mean with `compute_basic_stat` as the area-weighted mean over (y, x) using `areacello` (models) or `load_areas` (WOA23), NaN-aware and restricted to the same valid, ocean-mask cells as the data (never a plain mean over y, x). Report each model's value and WOA23's to 2 dp and each model-minus-WOA23 difference to 2 dp.
   - Note [omip-weighted-means-and-integrals]: global mean SSS = sum(SSS*areacello)/sum(areacello) over valid cells, weights NaN-aware and matching the data cells.
   - Note [omip-native-grid-vs-regridded]: every number (global mean, trends, basin means, delta SSS) is computed on each model's own grid with its own `areacello`; regridding is used only for the bias map, RMSE and pattern correlation, conservatively toward the coarser 1-degree WOA23 grid.

5. **Bias, RMSE and spatial correlation against WOA23.** Put the three model SSS climatologies and WOA23 SSS on the WOA23 1-degree grid with `regrid_horizontal` (conservative). Build one common set of cells with `build_comparison_mask` over all four fields and apply `.where(mask)` to every field; compute the area-weighted bias and RMSE with `compute_difference_metrics` and the spatial Pearson correlation with `analyze_correlation(weighting='area')` on that same mask, reporting RMSE to 2 dp and r to 3 dp. Report the mask's cell count alongside the metrics.
   - Note [common-comparison-mask]: one mask over all three models and WOA23 (ocean_only=True, the default, is correct because WOA23 is in the comparison); per-pair masks would score each model on a different piece of ocean. A mask much smaller than the fields means the products disagree about where the ocean is.

6. **Figure 1.** Three global maps in one row, one per model, of the SSS bias (model 1971-2000 climatology minus WOA23) on the step-5 comparison cells, one diverging colour scale centred on zero shared by all three panels, colour bar labelled in psu.

7. **Historical trend of the global mean.** From the monthly SSS series, form each model's annual global mean SSS over 1950-2014 with `temporal_aggregate` (annual) followed by `compute_basic_stat` (area-weighted over y, x), then fit `fit_1D_trend` with `method='linear'` on each model's own grid. Report slope in psu per decade to 3 significant figures with its 95 % confidence interval, R-squared, and whether the interval excludes zero.
   - Note [serial-correlation-and-significance]: fit the straight line explicitly with `method='linear'`; quote the effective-sample-size-corrected 95 % interval and p-value (not the naive monthly one), call the trend significant only if the interval excludes zero, and give R-squared.

8. **ssp245 trend of the global mean, and the slope ratio.** Same construction and `fit_1D_trend` (`method='linear'`) for ssp245 2071-2100, fitted separately (no single trend across the 2014/2015 join). Report slope, corrected 95 % interval, R-squared and significance, and the ratio ssp245 slope / historical slope to 1 dp.
   - Note [historical-ssp245-member-continuity]: CESM2's historical here is r1i1p1f1 while its ssp245 is r10i1p1f1, so its ssp245 slope and its delta SSS mix forced change with a difference in internal variability between two members — this caveat must be stated; GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR use r1 in both experiments.

9. **Delta SSS (global and basins).** Per model on its native grid, take the ssp245 2071-2100 mean minus the historical 1995-2014 mean of monthly SSS (using the unrounded series from steps 2-3); then compute the area-weighted mean with `compute_basic_stat` globally and per basin using `select_basin` with the WOA23 basin mask (Atlantic, Indian and Pacific only; the Southern and Arctic Oceans, being basins of their own, are excluded). Report each in psu to 2 dp, together with the CESM2 ensemble-member caveat from [historical-ssp245-member-continuity].
   - Note [basin-and-region-definitions]: use the WOA23 basin mask (`select_basin`, `load_basin_mask`), not any model OMIP basin field, with `include_marginal_seas=True` so marginal seas are folded into their parent ocean, and surface labels applied through the whole column (`depth_dependent=False`); each level's own labels are for water-mass work only.

10. **Figure 2.** Cell-by-cell linear trend of annual-mean SSS over 1950-2014 with `fit_trend_map` (annual means from `temporal_aggregate`), slope in psu per decade; stipple cells significant at p < 0.05 on top of the full slope field, per [serial-correlation-and-significance] (seasonal cycle removed before the fit; for the WOA23 pattern correlation read the coefficient, not its p-value). Each panel a true geographic map on a common longitude-latitude frame, curvilinear model fields put on a regular longitude-latitude grid for display only, one shared diverging colour scale centred on zero, per [omip-native-grid-vs-regridded] (the trend itself is computed before that display regridding).

11. **Figure 3.** One time-series panel of the global mean SSS of all three models, historical 1950-2014 and ssp245 2071-2100 drawn as separate segments with the 2015-2070 gap left empty, each segment overlaid with its own fitted line from steps 7-8, y axis labelled in psu, the same model colours used in Figures 1-3.

Other notes:
- The two trend segments are disjoint in time and driven by different forcing (historical all-forcing vs SSP2-4.5), so the ssp245/historical slope ratio is a descriptive comparison of two independently fitted slopes, not a physical sensitivity; it is reported only because it was requested.
- [historical-ssp245-member-continuity] the CESM2 historical/ssp245 member mismatch applies equally to its delta SSS and to its slope ratio, so those numbers carry forced change plus internal-variability differences between two members.
