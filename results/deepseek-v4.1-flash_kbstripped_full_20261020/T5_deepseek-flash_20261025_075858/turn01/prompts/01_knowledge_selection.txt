===== USER =====
User question: Assess sea surface salinity (SSS) in three CMIP6 models: their mean-state bias against observations, their historical trend, and their end-of-century change under the ssp245 scenario.

Data to use:
- CMIP6 historical `so` from CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, monthly, 1950-01 to 2014-12.
- CMIP6 ssp245 `so` from the same three models, monthly, 2071-01 to 2100-12.
- Each model's own areacello and volcello.
- WOA23 salinity, the 1971-2000 climate normal, annual, objectively analysed.
- The WOA23 1-degree land-sea mask (landsea_01, ocean is value > 1) and basin mask (basinmask_01).

SSS at each grid cell is the mean salinity of its levels whose depths lie within 0-10 m (WOA23: 0, 5 and 10 m; one or two levels per model), each level weighted by its full cell volume rather than by its overlap with 0-10 m, defined the same way for the models and for WOA23, reported in psu.

Expected numerical results:
- The global mean 1971-2000 SSS of each model and of WOA23, to 2 decimal places, and each model's difference from WOA23 to 2 decimal places.
- For each model, the RMSE against WOA23 in psu to 2 decimal places and the spatial Pearson correlation with WOA23 to 3 decimal places, on the WOA23 1-degree grid.
- For each model, the linear trend of the annual global mean SSS over historical 1950-2014 and over ssp245 2071-2100, each in psu per decade to 3 significant figures with its 95 per cent confidence interval, its R-squared, and whether the interval excludes zero; and the ratio of the ssp245 slope to the historical slope to 1 decimal place, with a statement of whether that ratio is meaningful.
- For each model, the mean change delta SSS (ssp245 2071-2100 mean minus historical 1995-2014 mean) in psu to 2 decimal places, for the global ocean and for the Atlantic, Indian and Pacific basins (basins as defined by the WOA23 basin mask, marginal seas folded into their parent ocean; the Southern and Arctic Oceans are basins of their own and excluded), together with any caveat arising from the ensemble members used for each model's two experiments.

Expected visualizations:
- Figure 1, three global maps in one row, one per model, of the SSS bias (model 1971-2000 climatology minus WOA23) on the cells of that comparison, one diverging colour scale centred on zero shared by all three panels, with the colour bar labelled in psu.
- Figure 2, three global maps in one row, one per model, of the cell-by-cell linear trend of annual-mean SSS over 1950-2014 in psu per decade, drawn as maps on a common longitude-latitude frame (each panel a true geographic map, with curvilinear model fields put on a regular longitude-latitude grid for display only) with one shared diverging colour scale centred on zero, and cells significant at p < 0.05 stippled on top of the trend field.
- Figure 3, a single time-series panel of the global mean SSS of all three models, with the historical 1950-2014 and ssp245 2071-2100 segments drawn separately (the 2015-2070 gap left empty), each segment overlaid with its own fitted line, the y axis labelled in psu, and each model in a colour used consistently across all three figures.
