# Plan

1. Load monthly data for 1950-01 to 2014-12 with `load_regular_data`: `ersst` variable `sst`; `cmip6_historical` variable `tos` for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR; and `cpc_indices` product `pdo`. Retain at least the PDO domain 20°N–70°N, 110°E–100°W, treating the longitudes continuously across the dateline as 110°E–260°E. Keep the published PDO as the published dimensionless monthly index and record its documented base period.
2. Regrid each SST field, including ERSSTv5, bilinearly onto the common 1° grid with `regrid_horizontal`; then subset to the exact PDO domain. For CMIP6 `tos`, use the model’s surface/top level (the PDO calculation will use `depth_level=None`).
   - Note [omip-native-grid-vs-regridded]: perform the EOF on this common 1° grid and compute explained variance and regression patterns there; state that bilinear is the question’s prescribed method, while the protocol’s conservative choice would be preferred when coarsening before an area-weighted step.
   - Note [cmip6-grid-labels]: GFDL-ESM4 `tos` is already `gr` on 1° (conserve_order1), so its “native” mask is the regridded one; CESM2 (~100 km) and MPI (~250 km) differ about 2.5× in resolution, so amplitudes of narrow features are not directly comparable.
3. Remove the seasonal cycle from each regridded SST field by subtracting its own 1950–2014 monthly climatology with `compute_anomaly_vs_own_climatology`; do not subtract an observed climatology from a model. Use the published PDO series as published.
   - Note [anomaly-baselines-and-climate-indices]: each model index must come from its own climatology; comparing with the published PDO requires its `base_period`, and the answer must state the climatology and baseline used.
4. For each of the four SST sources, call `compute_pdo_index` on the monthly anomaly field over 20°N–70°N, 110°E–100°W with `depth_level=None` to obtain the leading EOF mode, its variance-explained percentage, the standardized monthly index, and the regression pattern of SST anomalies on the standardized index.
   - Note [eof-modes-and-pdo-index]: the mode amplitude is the area-weighted RMS of the regression pattern in °C per standard deviation, not an EOF loading; explained variance is comparable only on this one common grid and period; `compute_pdo_index` fixes the arbitrary sign by convention (positive = cool central/western North Pacific), and patterns are compared with `analyze_correlation(weighting='area')`.
   - Note [cmip6-tos-bulk-top-cell]: pass `depth_level=None` so the top level reproduces each model’s own SST definition; do not use a 10 m level.
5. Using [eof-modes-and-pdo-index], compute each mode amplitude by applying `compute_basic_stat` to its regression pattern with area-weighted RMS over the domain; report °C per standard deviation to 3 decimals. Take the variance-explained percentages from step 4 to 1 decimal.
6. Standardize each monthly index over 1950–2014 to zero mean and unit standard deviation, and obtain its lag-12 (lag-1-year) autocorrelation by using `analyze_correlation` in lead-lag mode with the standardized monthly index against itself at 12 months. Report to 3 decimals.
7. Correlate the ERSSTv5-derived standardized index with the published PDO series using `analyze_correlation` on the two series as provided (no detrending); report Pearson r to 3 decimals and its significance from the effective sample size.
   - Note [serial-correlation-and-significance]: monthly ocean series are strongly autocorrelated, so quote significance from `analyze_correlation`’s effective sample size, not the number of months; the ERSSTv5-derived index and published PDO are two estimates of one quantity, correlated as they are, so the correlation shows agreement in time only, not equal variance or amplitude.
8. Correlate each model regression pattern with the ERSSTv5 regression pattern using `analyze_correlation(weighting='area')` [eof-modes-and-pdo-index]; report each spatial Pearson r to 3 decimals over the cells the two fields share. When reporting these correlations, state the bilinear regridding and the difference from the conservative recommendation [omip-native-grid-vs-regridded].
   - Note [common-comparison-mask]: regression patterns of a variability mode are the documented exception to a shared mask; correlate each model pattern with the ERSSTv5 pattern over shared cells and let `analyze_correlation` drop cells either lacks rather than masking all patterns first; report shared cell counts. If any shared mask is built, use `ocean_only=False` because the reference is not WOA23.
9. Build the ERSSTv5 9-year (108-month) centred running mean of the standardized monthly index with `temporal_aggregate`; identify sign changes of the smoothed series over 1950–2014, merging a crossing less than 3 years after the last counted transition into that earlier transition. List the resulting transition years and compare them to documented Pacific climate shifts, including the 1976/77 regime shift and other accepted shifts in the record.
10. Determine the best model by highest spatial pattern correlation from step 8; compare the models’ amplitudes from step 5 and lag-12 autocorrelations from step 6 with the ERSSTv5 values to assess amplitude and persistence, noting the resolution caveat from [cmip6-grid-labels].
11. Make Figure 1: a 2×2 panel of the regression pattern over the PDO domain in the order ERSSTv5, CESM2, GFDL-ESM4, MPI-ESM1-2-LR, using one diverging colour scale centred on zero labelled in °C per standard deviation, with each panel annotated with its explained variance and, for models, its pattern correlation with observations. Make Figure 2: a single time-series panel with the standardized indices of all four SST sources and the published PDO as a distinguishable fifth line, plus the 9-year smoothed ERSSTv5 curve and transition-year markers; label the y axis as standardized index.
