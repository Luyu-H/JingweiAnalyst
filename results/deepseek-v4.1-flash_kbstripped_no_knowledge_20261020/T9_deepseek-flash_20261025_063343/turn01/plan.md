# Plan

1. Load data with load_regular_data: CMIP6 historical `tos` for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, monthly 1950-01 to 2014-12; ERSSTv5 `sst` over the same window; and the published PDO series from cpc_indices, product `pdo`, over the same window. Also load the 1-degree land/sea mask with load_landsea_mask for the PDO domain 20N-70N, 110E-100W, handling the dateline crossing.
2. Use regrid_horizontal with bilinear interpolation to put each CMIP6 `tos` field and the ERSSTv5 `sst` field on the 1-degree grid. Apply the 1-degree land/sea mask to keep ocean cells only and retain a common monthly time axis.
3. For each source separately (ERSSTv5, CESM2, GFDL-ESM4, MPI-ESM1-2-LR), compute monthly anomalies over 1950-2014 relative to that source's own 1950-2014 monthly climatology using compute_anomaly_vs_own_climatology.
4. For each source's anomaly field in the PDO domain, use compute_pdo_index to obtain the leading EOF/PC, regression pattern and standardized PDO index. Compute and record the leading-mode variance explained as the leading singular value squared divided by the sum of all singular values squared, expressed as a percentage and reported to 1 decimal place. Compute the regression pattern as the covariance of the anomaly field with the standardized PC1, giving units of degrees Celsius per standard deviation; use compute_basic_stat to compute the spatial standard deviation of each regression pattern as the mode amplitude and report it to 3 decimal places.
5. Use analyze_correlation on each standardized monthly index series with itself at a lead-lag of 12 months to compute the lag-1-year autocorrelation; report each to 3 decimal places.
6. Use analyze_correlation between the ERSSTv5-derived standardized index and the published `pdo` series over 1950-2014 to obtain their Pearson correlation; report to 3 decimal places. Record that this correlation measures temporal phase/amplitude agreement between the two series, but does not by itself demonstrate causality, validate the published index, or establish CMIP6 model skill.
7. Use analyze_correlation between each model's regression pattern and the ERSSTv5 regression pattern over the same ocean cells and domain to compute the spatial Pearson pattern correlation; report each to 3 decimal places and identify the model with the highest pattern correlation as the best reproduction of the observed PDO pattern.
8. For the ERSSTv5 standardized index, use temporal_aggregate to compute the 108-month (9-year) centred running mean. Use detect_threshold_events with a zero threshold, or custom sign-change detection on the smoothed series, to locate zero crossings; apply the stated merging rule: if a crossing is less than 3 years after the last counted transition, merge it into the previous transition and keep the earlier year. Report the resulting transition years over 1950-2014 and compare them with documented Pacific climate shifts such as 1976/77, 1988/89, 1998/99 and 2014.
9. Compare the four sources' variance explained, amplitude, lag-12 autocorrelation and pattern correlations. Use these to judge which model best reproduces the observed PDO pattern and whether the models' amplitude and persistence are consistent with ERSSTv5.
10. Generate Figure 1 as a 2-by-2 panel of regression patterns in the order ERSSTv5, CESM2, GFDL-ESM4, MPI-ESM1-2-LR, using one diverging colour scale centred on zero and labelled in degrees Celsius per standard deviation. Annotate each panel with its explained variance and, for models, its pattern correlation with ERSSTv5. Generate Figure 2 as a single time-series panel showing the standardized index of all four sources plus the published PDO as a distinguishable fifth line, with the 9-year smoothed ERSSTv5 curve and transition years marked, and label the y axis as a standardized index.

Other notes:
- The sign of an EOF/PC is arbitrary; before pattern correlations and plotting, orient each leading mode with a fixed convention, such as positive regression loadings in the eastern North Pacific, so that pattern correlations are meaningful.
- The ERSSTv5-derived index versus published PDO correlation demonstrates temporal agreement only; it does not prove causality or independently establish model skill.
- All sources are bilinearly regridded to the 1-degree grid and masked only by the 1-degree land/sea mask; no additional detrending, screening or filtering is applied beyond the specified monthly climatology and 108-month running mean.
- The transition-year result follows the question's 9-year smoothing and less-than-3-year merging rule and can be sensitive to smoothing and EOF sign convention.
