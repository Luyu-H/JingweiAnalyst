The user's question:
Check whether a mixed layer depth (MLD) computed from each CMIP6 model's own temperature and salinity reproduces the mlotst field that the same model publishes. This is an internal consistency test: both quantities come from the same simulation, so any disagreement is a property of the diagnostic, not of the model's physics.

Data to use:
- CMIP6 historical `thetao`, `so` and `mlotst` from CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, monthly, 1971-01 to 2000-12.
- Each model's own published cell areas (areacello).

Expected numerical results, comparing the 1971-2000 March and September climatologies of the operator MLD with those of the published mlotst, for each of the six model-and-month combinations. The operator MLD is the mixed layer depth computed offline from the model's own thetao and so with the density criterion that defines mlotst, the crossing depth interpolated between the bracketing levels. For each model and calendar month, both climatologies cover only the cells where both fields are defined in all 30 instances of that month (the 30 Marches, or the 30 Septembers):
- The mean difference, operator MLD minus mlotst, in metres to 1 decimal place.
- The root-mean-square difference, in metres to 1 decimal place.
- The spatial Pearson correlation between the two fields, to 3 decimal places.
- The percentage of the area where both fields are defined on which the absolute difference exceeds 20 m, to 1 decimal place, and the area where both fields are defined as a percentage of the model's total ocean area, to 1 decimal place.
- The ranking of the three models from most to least internally consistent, with the criterion used, the regions where the disagreement concentrates, and why the operator and mlotst are not expected to agree exactly.

Expected visualizations:
- Figure 1, a 3-by-2 panel of global maps of operator MLD minus mlotst, rows CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, columns March and September, all six panels drawn as maps on a common longitude-latitude frame (each panel a true geographic map, with curvilinear model fields put on a regular longitude-latitude grid for display only) with one shared diverging colour scale centred on zero.
- Figure 2, a 3-by-2 panel of scatter plots in the same layout, operator MLD on the x axis against mlotst on the y axis, one point per ocean cell, with logarithmic axes of identical range and ticks in every panel, a dashed y = x reference line, and each panel annotated with its bias, root-mean-square difference and correlation.

The analysis plan:
1. Load from cmip6_historical, for each model CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, the monthly 1971-01 to 2000-12 fields thetao, so, mlotst, and the fixed areacello on each model's native ocean grid using load_regular_data.
2. For each model, compute potential density anomaly from thetao and so with compute_potential_density_anomaly (TEOS-10), then compute the operator MLD with compute_mixed_layer_depth using the density criterion that defines mlotst (0.03 kg m-3 density increase relative to the reference near-surface level), with the crossing depth interpolated between bracketing levels.
3. For each model and separately for March and September, build the common validity mask over 1971-2000 requiring both monthly operator MLD and monthly published mlotst to be defined in every one of the 30 instances of that calendar month, using build_comparison_mask. Apply this mask to both monthly fields.
4. For each model and each of March and September, use temporal_aggregate to form the climatological-monthly means of the masked operator MLD and masked mlotst over 1971-2000 (climatological-monthly aggregation; only the 30 selected months contribute), yielding six operator-vs-mlotst climatology pairs.
5. For each of the six model/month pairs, compute the difference field operator MLD minus mlotst. Use compute_difference_metrics on the common valid cells for the mean difference (bias) and root-mean-square difference; use analyze_correlation for the spatial Pearson correlation between the two climatology fields on the same cells; use locate_extreme on the difference to identify where the largest departures occur.
6. For each pair, compute the element-wise absolute difference and use compute_exceedance_fraction with a 20 m threshold, by area, to obtain the percentage of the common valid area where |operator MLD - mlotst| > 20 m. Use compute_basic_stat with areacello weights to obtain the common valid area and the model's total ocean area from areacello, then express the common valid area as a percentage of total ocean area.
7. Rank the three models from most to least internally consistent using as primary criterion the lower mean RMSE of the March and September comparisons (averaging the two monthly RMSEs), with lower area fraction exceeding 20 m as tie-breaker and correlation/bias values as supporting diagnostics. Use the difference maps and locate_extreme to report the regions where disagreement concentrates.
8. For Figure 1, use regrid_horizontal only for display to put each of the six difference maps on a common regular longitude-latitude grid, then plot a 3-by-2 panel (rows CESM2, GFDL-ESM4, MPI-ESM1-2-LR; columns March, September) with one shared diverging colour scale centred on zero.

Other notes:
- Metrics are computed on each model's native grid and on the common mask requiring both fields to be defined in all 30 March or all 30 September instances; maps are regridded for display only.
- The area percentages use each model's own areacello; the denominator for "percentage of model total ocean area" is the sum of areacello over the model's ocean cells.
- Operator and published mlotst are not expected to agree exactly because mlotst is an online model diagnostic while the operator is computed offline from monthly mean thetao/so; differences can arise from threshold/reference-depth implementation, vertical discretization and interpolation, sea-ice/brine effects, short-lived convection and temporal averaging before MLD calculation, and missing-value/masking details.
- The spatial Pearson correlation is computed across the common valid cells; it is not an area-weighted correlation.

# Internal consistency of CMIP6 mlotst vs an offline MLD diagnostic
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
models = ['CESM2', 'GFDL-ESM4', 'MPI-ESM1-2-LR']
month_names = ['March', 'September']
month_nums = {'March': 3, 'September': 9}
time_range = ['1971-01', '2000-12']


def _num(x):
    """Materialise a scalar (float / 0-d numpy- or dask-backed DataArray)."""
    if hasattr(x, 'compute'):
        x = x.compute()
    return float(x)


results = {}        # results[(model, month)] -> dict of diagnostics
map_fields = {}     # map_fields[(model, month)] -> regridded difference field (display only)
scatter_vals = {}   # scatter_vals[(model, month)] -> (operator MLD, mlotst) on common cells

for model in models:
    print('=' * 78)
    print('Model:', model)

    thetao = load_regular_data(dataset='cmip6_historical', variable='thetao', model=model,
                               field='total', time_range=time_range,
                               depth_range=[0.0, 1000.0])
    so = load_regular_data(dataset='cmip6_historical', variable='so', model=model,
                           field='total', time_range=time_range,
                           depth_range=[0.0, 1000.0])
    mlotst = load_regular_data(dataset='cmip6_historical', variable='mlotst', model=model,
                               field='total', time_range=time_range)
    areacello = load_regular_data(dataset='cmip6_historical', variable='areacello', model=model)

    # --- offline MLD from the model's own T and S, density criterion (0.03 kg m-3
    #     relative to the 10 m reference level), crossing depth interpolated ------
    mld_op = compute_mixed_layer_depth(temp_data=thetao, sal_data=so,
                                       ref_depth=10.0, method='density',
                                       density_threshold=0.03)

    total_area = _num(compute_basic_stat(data=areacello, op='sum', dim=None))

    for mname in month_names:
        mnum = month_nums[mname]
        op_m = mld_op.sel(time=(mld_op.time.dt.month == mnum))
        pub_m = mlotst.sel(time=(mlotst.time.dt.month == mnum))
        n_months = int(op_m.sizes['time'])

        # both fields must be defined in EVERY instance of this calendar month
        mask = (op_m.notnull() & pub_m.notnull()).all('time')

        op_m = op_m.where(mask)
        pub_m = pub_m.where(mask)

        # Day-weighted long-term mean over the 30 instances of this calendar month.
        # freq='mean' collapses the time axis to a single field; freq='climatology'
        # would try to build a 12-entry 'month' axis and fails on a one-month input.
        clim_op = temporal_aggregate(data=op_m, freq='mean')
        clim_pub = temporal_aggregate(data=pub_m, freq='mean')

        # keep the loader's grid provenance on the climatologies
        clim_op.attrs = {**clim_op.attrs,
                         **{k: v for k, v in mld_op.attrs.items() if k != 'units'}}
        clim_op.attrs['units'] = 'm'
        clim_pub.attrs = {**clim_pub.attrs,
                          **{k: v for k, v in mlotst.attrs.items() if k != 'units'}}
        clim_pub.attrs['units'] = 'm'

        # --- bias / RMSE (area weighted over the common valid cells) -------------
        metrics = compute_difference_metrics(data=clim_op, reference=clim_pub)
        bias = _num(metrics['bias'])
        rmse = _num(metrics['rmse'])
        crmse = _num(metrics['centered_rmse'])
        n_valid = int(metrics['n_valid'])

        # --- spatial Pearson correlation over the common cells ------------------
        corr = analyze_correlation(var1=clim_op, var2=clim_pub, dim=['lat', 'lon'])
        r = _num(corr['correlation'])
        pval = _num(corr['pvalue'])

        # --- difference field, extreme, exceedance ------------------------------
        diff_field = metrics['difference'].compute()
        diff_field.attrs = dict(metrics['difference'].attrs)
        diff_field.attrs['units'] = 'm'

        extreme = locate_extreme(data=diff_field, op='abs_max')

        abs_diff = abs(diff_field)
        abs_diff.attrs = dict(diff_field.attrs)
        abs_diff.attrs['units'] = 'm'
        exc = compute_exceedance_fraction(data=abs_diff, threshold=20.0,
                                          comparison='greater', weighting='area', dim=None)
        frac20 = _num(exc['fraction_percent'])

        common_area = _num(compute_basic_stat(data=areacello.where(mask), op='sum', dim=None))
        area_pct = 100.0 * common_area / total_area

        results[(model, mname)] = dict(bias=bias, rmse=rmse, centered_rmse=crmse,
                                       corr=r, pvalue=pval, frac20=frac20,
                                       common_area=common_area, total_area=total_area,
                                       area_pct=area_pct, extreme=extreme,
                                       n_months=n_months, n_valid=n_valid)

        # --- native-grid values for the scatter panel ---------------------------
        scatter_vals[(model, mname)] = (clim_op.compute().values.ravel(),
                                        clim_pub.compute().values.ravel())

        # --- regrid the difference for DISPLAY only -----------------------------
        map_fields[(model, mname)] = regrid_horizontal(data=diff_field, target='1deg',
                                                       method='bilinear').compute()

        print(f'  {mname:9s} ({n_months} fields)  bias={bias:+7.1f} m  rmse={rmse:6.1f} m  '
              f'r={r:+.3f}  |d|>20 m: {frac20:5.1f} %  common area: {area_pct:5.1f} %')
        print('           extreme:', extreme['description'])

    del thetao, so, mlotst, areacello, mld_op

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Ranking: most -> least internally consistent (mean of March & September RMSE)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
model_score = {}
for model in models:
    rmses = [results[(model, mn)]['rmse'] for mn in month_names]
    fracs = [results[(model, mn)]['frac20'] for mn in month_names]
    model_score[model] = (float(np.mean(rmses)), float(np.mean(fracs)))

ranking = sorted(models, key=lambda m: (model_score[m][0], model_score[m][1]))

print()
print('=' * 78)
print('RANKING (most -> least internally consistent)')
print('primary criterion: mean of the March and September RMSE (operator MLD - mlotst)')
for i, m in enumerate(ranking, start=1):
    mr, mf = model_score[m]
    print(f'  {i}. {m:16s} mean RMSE = {mr:6.1f} m | mean |d|>20 m fraction = {mf:5.1f} %')

print()
print('=' * 78)
print('SUMMARY TABLE')
for model in models:
    for mname in month_names:
        d = results[(model, mname)]
        print(f'{model:16s} {mname:9s} '
              f'bias={d["bias"]:+7.1f}  rmse={d["rmse"]:6.1f}  r={d["corr"]:+.3f}  '
              f'|d|>20m={d["frac20"]:5.1f}%  valid area={d["area_pct"]:5.1f}%')

summary_table = pd.DataFrame(
    [{'model': m, 'month': mn,
      'bias_m': round(results[(m, mn)]['bias'], 1),
      'rmse_m': round(results[(m, mn)]['rmse'], 1),
      'corr': round(results[(m, mn)]['corr'], 3),
      'pct_absdiff_gt20m': round(results[(m, mn)]['frac20'], 1),
      'pct_of_ocean_area': round(results[(m, mn)]['area_pct'], 1)}
     for m in models for mn in month_names])
print(summary_table.to_string(index=False))

fig1, axes1 = plt.subplots(3, 2, figsize=(14, 10), constrained_layout=True)

pooled = np.concatenate([np.abs(map_fields[(m, mn)].values).ravel()

vmax = float(np.nanpercentile(pooled[np.isfinite(pooled)], 98))

mesh = None
for i, model in enumerate(models):
    for j, mname in enumerate(month_names):
        ax = axes1[i, j]
        d = map_fields[(model, mname)]
        mesh = d.plot(ax=ax, cmap='RdBu_r', vmin=-vmax, vmax=vmax,
                      add_colorbar=False, extend='both')
        ax.set_title(f'{model} — {mname}', fontsize=10)
        ax.set_xlabel('')
        ax.set_ylabel('')
        ax.set_xlim(float(d['lon'].min()), float(d['lon'].max()))
        ax.set_ylim(float(d['lat'].min()), float(d['lat'].max()))
        ax.tick_params(labelsize=8)

for ax in axes1[:, 0]:
    ax.set_ylabel('Latitude', fontsize=9)
for ax in axes1[-1, :]:
    ax.set_xlabel('Longitude', fontsize=9)

cb1 = fig1.colorbar(mesh, ax=axes1, shrink=0.6, pad=0.02,
                    label='Operator MLD - mlotst (m)')
fig1.suptitle('Figure 1. Operator MLD minus published mlotst, 1971-2000 climatologies '
              '(shared diverging scale, clipped at pooled 98th percentile '
              f'|$\\Delta$| = {vmax:.0f} m)', fontsize=12)

pos_all = []
