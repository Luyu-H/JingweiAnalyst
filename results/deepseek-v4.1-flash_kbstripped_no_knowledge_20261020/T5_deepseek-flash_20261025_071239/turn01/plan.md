# Plan

1. Load CMIP6 data with load_regular_data: cmip6_historical `so` for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, monthly 1950-01 to 2014-12; cmip6_ssp245 `so` for the same models, monthly 2071-01 to 2100-12. For each model load areacello with load_areas, volcello with load_volumes_for_cmip6, and level depths/boundaries with load_depth_levels. Convert `so` to psu with transform_unit if it is stored as 1e-3. Record the variant/ensemble label for each historical and ssp245 load.
2. Load WOA23 with load_climatology_data: salinity, 1971-2000 climate normal, annual. Load its depth levels/boundaries with load_depth_levels, 1-degree cell areas with load_areas, landsea_01 with load_landsea_mask (ocean where value > 1), and basinmask_01 with load_basin_mask.
3. Define SSS at each cell for models and WOA23: keep only levels whose depths lie within 0-10 m inclusive (WOA23 0, 5, 10 m; one or two model levels). Use compute_basic_stat along depth with full-cell-volume weights: CMIP6 weights are volcello for each selected level; WOA23 weights are cell area times layer thickness from load_areas and load_depth_levels. Do not weight by overlap with 0-10 m. Apply the same definition to monthly model fields and to WOA23, in psu.
4. For the 1971-2000 mean state, use temporal_aggregate to make each model's historical SSS 1971-2000 long-term mean per cell, then compute_basic_stat area-weighted global mean with areacello. For WOA23, compute_basic_stat area-weighted global mean over the landsea_01 ocean cells with 1-degree areas. Report each model and WOA23 global mean to 2 dp, and model minus WOA23 to 2 dp.
5. For spatial bias metrics, use each model's 1971-2000 SSS climatology from step 4, regrid_horizontal to the WOA23 1-degree grid, and build_comparison_mask from the WOA23 ocean mask and valid model/WOA23 SSS. On that mask use compute_difference_metrics for bias and RMSE and analyze_correlation for spatial Pearson correlation. Form the bias map (model climatology minus WOA23) with compute_anomaly_vs_woa23. Report RMSE to 2 dp and correlation to 3 dp.
6. For historical trends, compute each model's monthly global mean SSS by area-weighted compute_basic_stat over valid ocean cells with areacello, temporal_aggregate those monthly means to annual values, and fit_1D_trend over 1950-2014. Report slope in psu/decade to 3 significant figures, 95% CI, R-squared, and whether the interval excludes zero. Retain the annual global mean series for Figure 3.
7. Repeat step 6 for ssp245 over 2071-2100. Compute the ratio ssp245 slope / historical slope to 1 dp from the fitted slopes. Assess whether that ratio is meaningful given the different periods, mean states, forcing and separate uncertainties.
8. For the cell-by-cell historical trend maps, temporal_aggregate each model's monthly SSS to annual-mean SSS per cell over 1950-2014, then fit_trend_map to obtain the linear slope and significance at every cell. For display only, regrid_horizontal the slope and significance fields to a regular longitude-latitude grid. Stipple cells with p < 0.05 on top of the trend field.
9. For delta SSS, use temporal_aggregate to compute each model's ssp245 2071-2100 mean SSS and historical 1995-2014 mean SSS. Delta is ssp245 mean minus historical mean. Compute the global ocean mean with area weighting (areacello on the model grid). For basin means, regrid_horizontal the period-mean fields to the WOA23 1-degree grid, apply the WOA23 basinmask_01 using select_basin for Atlantic, Indian and Pacific (marginal seas folded into parent; Southern and Arctic excluded), and compute area-weighted means with WOA23 1-degree areas. Report global and basin deltas to 2 dp.
10. Build Figure 1 as three bias maps (one per model) on the step-5 comparison cells, one shared diverging colour scale centred on zero, colour bar in psu. Build Figure 2 as three cell-trend maps on a common regular longitude-latitude display grid, one shared diverging scale centred on zero in psu/decade, with p < 0.05 stippling. Build Figure 3 as the global mean SSS annual series for all three models, historical 1950-2014 and ssp245 2071-2100 drawn as separate segments with the 2015-2070 gap empty, each segment overlaid with its fitted line, y axis in psu, and consistent model colours across all figures.

Other notes:
- WOA23 is a 1971-2000 climatology, so the bias is against that normal, not a concurrent observational time series.
- SSS is the full-cell-volume-weighted mean of the selected levels within 0-10 m, not an overlap-weighted 0-10 m integral, and the same definition is used for models and WOA23.
- CMIP6 salinity is converted to psu if the native unit is 1e-3.
- Spatial RMSE and correlation are computed only on the common WOA23 1-degree ocean/valid cells.
- Basin means use the WOA23 basinmask_01; marginal seas are folded into parent oceans and Southern and Arctic Oceans are excluded.
- Historical and ssp245 results are single-member estimates; if the variant labels differ between a model's two experiments, the delta and slope ratio also include member-to-member differences.
- The ssp245/historical slope ratio is descriptive only because the two slopes cover different periods and mean states and are not a formal ratio test; it is especially weak if either slope is near zero.
