Evaluate the ocean carbon system of three CMIP6 models against the GLODAPv2.2016b mapped climatology: dissolved inorganic carbon, total alkalinity and pH.

Data to use:
- CMIP6 historical `dissic`, `talk`, `ph`, `thetao` and `so` from CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, monthly, for the calendar year 2002, plus `dissic`, `thetao` and `so` for 1971-2000.
- Each model's own areacello and volcello.
- GLODAPv2.2016b mapped climatology: TCO2, TAlk and pH.

Expected numerical results, with the models taken as their 2002 annual mean unless stated otherwise, and model DIC and alkalinity compared in micromoles per kilogram:
- The global mean over 0-50 m of DIC and of total alkalinity for each model and for GLODAP (for every source one mean over all its 0-50 m cells), in micromoles per kilogram to the nearest whole unit, and each model's difference from GLODAP.
- The same 0-50 m mean for pH on the total scale, to 3 decimal places, and each model's difference from GLODAP.
- For the map of 0-50 m mean DIC (each cell's mean over its own 0-50 m levels), compared on the GLODAP 1-degree grid: each model's mean bias and RMSE in micromoles per kilogram to the nearest whole unit, and the spatial correlation to 3 decimal places.
- For each model, the global mean DIC bias (model minus GLODAP) at 0, 100, 500, 1000 and 2000 m, compared on the GLODAP 1-degree grid and averaged, at each depth, over every cell where that model and GLODAP are both defined at that depth, in micromoles per kilogram to the nearest whole unit.
- For each model, the period change of the global 0-50 m mean DIC, 2002 minus the 1971-2000 average, in micromoles per kilogram to the nearest whole unit, and its ratio to that model's 0-50 m DIC difference from GLODAP as defined in the first item to 2 decimal places, with what that implies for reading the bias as a model error.
- Which model reproduces the observed carbon system best, and whether the three models' errors in DIC, alkalinity and pH are consistent in sign.

Expected visualizations:
- Figure 1, three global maps in one row, one per model, of the 0-50 m DIC bias against GLODAP on the cells of the map comparison, with one diverging colour scale centred on zero shared by all three panels and labelled in micromoles per kilogram.
- Figure 2, a single panel of the vertical profile of the global mean DIC bias: depth on the vertical axis increasing downward, bias in micromoles per kilogram on the horizontal axis, one line per model, a vertical zero reference line, and the five sampled depths marked.
