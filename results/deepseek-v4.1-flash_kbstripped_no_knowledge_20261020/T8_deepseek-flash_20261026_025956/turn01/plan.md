# Plan

1. Load CMIP6 historical with `load_regular_data` for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR: `dissic`, `talk`, `ph`, `thetao`, `so` monthly for 2002; `dissic`, `thetao`, `so` monthly for 1971-2000. Load each model's `areacello` and `volcello` (`load_volumes_for_cmip6` for volume) and model depth levels/layer boundaries with `load_depth_levels`.
2. Load GLODAP with `load_climatology_data`: `TCO2`, `TAlk`, `pHtsinsitutp`; load its depth levels/boundaries with `load_depth_levels` and its 1-degree cell areas with `load_areas`.
3. Use `temporal_aggregate` to form each model's 2002 annual mean for `dissic`, `talk`, `ph`, `thetao`, `so`, and each model's 1971-2000 long-term mean for `dissic`, `thetao`, `so`.
4. Convert model `dissic` and `talk` from mol m-3 to µmol kg-1: compute density from `thetao` and `so` with `compute_potential_density_anomaly` (add 1000 kg m-3 to sigma), use `transform_unit` for the amount-concentration unit part, and divide by density. Keep GLODAP `TCO2`/`TAlk` in µmol kg-1 and use `pHtsinsitutp` for pH.
5. Using `load_depth_levels`, select each source's levels wholly within 0-50 m, with no partial-layer weighting. For each model and GLODAP, compute one 0-50 m global mean for DIC/TCO2, alkalinity/TAlk and pH with `compute_basic_stat` and its spatial weighting operators (model `areacello`/`volcello`; GLODAP area/depth-level weights). Also compute, for each model and GLODAP, the 2-D 0-50 m mean DIC field as each cell's mean over its own selected 0-50 m levels with `compute_basic_stat`.
6. Compare the scalar 0-50 m means: model minus GLODAP for DIC, alkalinity and pH; report DIC/alkalinity to whole µmol kg-1 and pH to 3 decimals. Use `compute_difference_metrics` for field comparisons where applicable and plain arithmetic for these scalar differences.
7. For the DIC map comparison, use `regrid_horizontal` to put each model's 0-50 m mean DIC field on the GLODAP 1-degree grid; use `build_comparison_mask` with the model and GLODAP fields; compute mean bias and RMSE with `compute_difference_metrics` and spatial correlation with `analyze_correlation` on the common mask. Plot Figure 1 as three model bias maps (model minus GLODAP) with one shared zero-centred diverging colour scale labelled µmol kg-1.
8. For the vertical DIC-bias profile, take each model's 2002 annual mean DIC in µmol kg-1, use `regrid_horizontal` to the GLODAP 1-degree grid, and use `regrid_vertical`/GLODAP levels to evaluate model and GLODAP at 0, 100, 500, 1000 and 2000 m. At each depth, use `build_comparison_mask` for cells where both are defined, then use `compute_difference_metrics`/`compute_basic_stat` to average model minus GLODAP in µmol kg-1. Plot Figure 2 with depth downward, bias on the horizontal axis, one line per model, a vertical zero line and the five sampled depths marked.
9. For the period change, use the converted DIC fields to compute each model's global 0-50 m mean DIC for 2002 and for the 1971-2000 long-term mean with the same level selection and `compute_basic_stat` weighting; subtract to get 2002 minus 1971-2000. Divide this period change by that model's 2002 0-50 m DIC difference from GLODAP from step 6, report to 2 decimals, and interpret whether the model-GLODAP bias is comparable to the recent period change.
10. Rank the models using the DIC map bias/RMSE/correlation, vertical bias profile, and the DIC/alkalinity/pH 0-50 m differences from steps 6-8; state which model is best overall. Compare the signs of model-minus-GLODAP for DIC, alkalinity and pH across the three models and within each model, using the results from steps 6-8.

Other notes:
- GLODAPv2.2016b is a 1972-2013 climatology, while the model fields are a 2002 annual mean and a 1971-2000 model average, so part of any difference is temporal/climatological mismatch.
- Model DIC and alkalinity are converted from mol m-3 to µmol kg-1 using each model's `thetao` and `so` to estimate density; GLODAP `TCO2` and `TAlk` are already in µmol kg-1.
- pH comparison uses GLODAP `pHtsinsitutp` and CMIP6 `ph` as provided; the total-scale convention should be confirmed for the model output.
- 0-50 m means use only levels wholly within 0-50 m, with no partial-layer weighting, and map/vertical comparisons use only cells defined in both fields at the relevant depth.
- The period-change-to-bias ratio is a diagnostic, not a formal attribution: a ratio comparable to or larger than one means the apparent model-GLODAP DIC bias should not be read purely as a structural model error.
- Model ranking depends on the combined DIC, alkalinity and pH metrics; sign consistency should be assessed separately for each variable across models and for the three variables within each model.
