# Plan

1. Load CMIP6 historical data from cmip6_historical for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR using load_regular_data: monthly `dissic`, `talk`, `ph`, `thetao`, `so` for calendar year 2002; monthly `dissic`, `thetao`, `so` for 1971-2000; and each model's fixed `areacello` and `volcello`. Use load_volumes_for_cmip6 for the model cell volumes. Load GLODAP climatology from glodap using load_climatology_data: `TCO2`, `TAlk`, `pHtsinsitutp`.
2. Use temporal_aggregate to form, for each model, the 2002 annual means of `dissic`, `talk`, `ph`, `thetao`, `so`, and the day-weighted long-term mean over 1971-2000 of `dissic`, `thetao`, `so`.
3. For each model, convert `dissic` and `talk` from mol m-3 to micromoles per kilogram: compute density from `thetao` and `so` with compute_potential_density_anomaly (density = 1000 + sigma), convert mol m-3 to micromol m-3 with transform_unit, then divide by density using compute_ratio. Apply this to the 2002 `dissic`, 2002 `talk`, and 1971-2000 `dissic`. Keep GLODAP `TCO2` and `TAlk` in micromoles per kilogram. Compare pH on the total scale as supplied, using model `ph` and GLODAP `pHtsinsitutp`, with no pH conversion.
4. Compute the global 0-50 m means for DIC, total alkalinity and pH from each model and from GLODAP using compute_basic_stat over all cells/levels in the 0-50 m range, weighted in space. Use model `volcello` for model weights; for GLODAP use load_areas and load_depth_levels to supply the 1-degree cell volume weights to compute_basic_stat. Then compute each model's 0-50 m mean minus the corresponding GLODAP mean for DIC, total alkalinity and pH, using unrounded intermediates and rounding only for reporting.
5. Build the 0-50 m mean DIC map for each model and for GLODAP: for each horizontal cell, use compute_basic_stat along depth over that source's own levels in the 0-50 m range to get the cell's 0-50 m mean DIC. Regrid each model map to the GLODAP 1-degree grid with regrid_horizontal. Use build_comparison_mask to retain cells where model and GLODAP are both defined, then use compute_difference_metrics for mean bias and RMSE and analyze_correlation for the spatial correlation.
6. For each model and each requested depth (0, 100, 500, 1000, 2000 m), use regrid_vertical to put model DIC and GLODAP `TCO2` on that depth, then regrid_horizontal the model field to the GLODAP 1-degree grid. At each depth, use build_comparison_mask for cells where both are defined at that depth, and use compute_difference_metrics to obtain the global mean DIC bias, model minus GLODAP, over the common cells.
7. For each model, compute the global 0-50 m mean DIC for the 2002 annual mean and for the 1971-2000 long-term mean with compute_basic_stat using model volume weights, after the model DIC conversion to micromoles per kilogram. Subtract the 1971-2000 mean from the 2002 mean to get the period change. Use compute_ratio to divide this period change by the model's 0-50 m DIC difference from GLODAP obtained in step 4, reporting the ratio to two decimal places.
8. Use the global mean biases for DIC, total alkalinity and pH, together with the DIC map bias, RMSE and spatial correlation and the vertical DIC bias profile, to identify which model best reproduces the observed carbon system. Check the signs of each model's DIC, total alkalinity and pH differences from GLODAP to state whether the three errors are consistent in sign.
9. Make Figure 1 from the masked 0-50 m DIC bias maps: three global maps in one row, one per model, with a single shared diverging colour scale centred on zero and labelled in micromoles per kilogram. Make Figure 2 from the vertical DIC bias profiles: depth on the vertical axis increasing downward, DIC bias in micromoles per kilogram on the horizontal axis, one line per model, a vertical zero reference line, and the five sampled depths marked.

Other notes:
- Model DIC and total alkalinity are converted from mol m-3 to micromoles per kilogram using model `thetao`/`so` density; GLODAP `TCO2` and `TAlk` are used in micromoles per kilogram.
- pH is compared on the total scale as supplied, with model `ph` against GLODAP `pHtsinsitutp`, without conversion.
- The global 0-50 m means are spatial weighted means, using model `volcello` and GLODAP area-times-layer-thickness volume weights; the 1971-2000 average is day-weighted over time.
- Map and depth comparisons use only cells where both the model field and GLODAP are defined on the GLODAP 1-degree grid at that map/depth.
- The ratio of the 2002-minus-1971-2000 DIC change to the model-GLODAP DIC bias indicates how much the apparent bias may reflect the model's transient/period change rather than a static model error; a ratio comparable to or larger than one means the bias should not be read as a pure model error.
- If a CMIP6 model does not publish a requested variable, it is reported as missing.
