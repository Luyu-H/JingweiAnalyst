# Plan

1. Load WOA23 climatology oxygen, percent_oxygen_saturation and apparent_oxygen_utilization using the requested 1971-2000 normal where available; load WOA23 landsea_01, basinmask_01, grid-cell areas and depth levels with load_climatology_data, load_landsea_mask, load_basin_mask, load_areas and load_depth_levels. Build WOA23 ocean-cell volumes from area times layer thickness, restricted by landsea_01.
2. Load CMIP6 historical o2, thetao and so for GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, monthly 1971-01 to 2000-12, with load_regular_data. Load CMIP6 ssp245 o2, thetao and so for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, monthly 2071-01 to 2100-12. Load each model’s volcello with load_volumes_for_cmip6, areacello with load_areas, and depth levels with load_depth_levels.
3. For each CMIP6 model, compute density anomaly from thetao and so with compute_potential_density_anomaly, convert o2 from mol m-3 to micromoles per kilogram using that density and transform_unit, then use temporal_aggregate to form the day-weighted long-term mean for historical 1971-2000 and ssp245 2071-2100. Keep the original o2 concentration for inventory calculations.
4. For each source-and-experiment combination where applicable, compute the total ocean volume with oxygen strictly below 20 and strictly below 60 micromoles per kilogram using compute_exceedance_fraction by volume with each product’s cell volumes, and multiply by the domain total volume from compute_basic_stat. Report volumes in 1e15 m3 to 3 significant figures for WOA23, GFDL-ESM4 historical, MPI-ESM1-2-LR historical, CESM2 ssp245, GFDL-ESM4 ssp245 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR ssp245.
5. For the same six combinations, compute the OMZ thickness at the 60 threshold as the summed thickness of water below the threshold in each column using identify_oxygen_minimum_zone with the relevant depth-layer boundaries. Use locate_extreme to find the maximum thickness and its latitude and longitude, reported with the requested precision.
6. For GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, compute the percentage change in OMZ volume from historical to ssp245 at both the 20 and 60 thresholds as 100 times (ssp245 volume minus historical volume) divided by historical volume, using unrounded volumes and reporting positive values for expansion.
7. Compute the total dissolved oxygen inventory for GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR historical, and for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR ssp245, by applying convert_concentration_to_content to the model oxygen concentration and each model’s volcello, then summing with compute_basic_stat. Report inventories in moles to 3 significant figures.
8. For WOA23 and the GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR historical simulations, compute OMZ volume at the 60 threshold in the Pacific, Indian and Atlantic basins only. Use basinmask_01 with select_basin for WOA23, and regrid the WOA23 basin mask to each model grid with regrid_horizontal before select_basin for the models. For each basin, use compute_exceedance_fraction by volume with the basin-restricted volumes and multiply by the basin total volume from compute_basic_stat; exclude Southern and Arctic, and report in 1e15 m3 to 3 significant figures.
9. For WOA23, compute the volume-weighted mean apparent oxygen utilization and percent oxygen saturation within the 60-threshold OMZ using the O2<60 mask and compute_basic_stat. Report each to 1 decimal place and interpret whether the combination of high AOU and low saturation indicates respiration-driven oxygen consumption or poor ventilation.
10. Compare the historical GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR OMZ volumes with the WOA23 volumes at both thresholds to determine which model reproduces the observed volume better. Compare the sign and magnitude of the two models’ historical-to-ssp245 percentage changes at both thresholds.
11. Plot Figure 1 as three global maps of historical OMZ thickness at the 60 threshold for GFDL-ESM4, MPI-ESM1-2-LR and WOA23 on one shared sequential colour scale in metres. Plot Figure 2 as the ssp245-minus-historical OMZ thickness change for GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR on one shared diverging scale centred on zero, plus CESM2 ssp245 OMZ thickness on its own sequential scale labelled to show that no historical change can be formed for CESM2.

Other notes:
- WOA23 oxygen, percent oxygen saturation and AOU are used as the observational baseline; WOA23 metadata describes oxygen and nutrients as an all-period composite, so the requested 1971-2000 oxygen normal is not separately available and the all-period oxygen climatology is used.
- CMIP6 o2 is converted from mol m-3 to micromoles per kilogram using in-situ density from thetao and so before applying the thresholds; inventories use the original oxygen concentration and volcello.
- CESM2 has no historical o2, so no historical-to-ssp245 change can be formed for CESM2; only its ssp245 OMZ metrics and map are reported.
- Basin calculations use the WOA23 basin mask regridded to each model grid, with Pacific, Indian and Atlantic only; Southern and Arctic are excluded and marginal seas follow their parent basin.
- OMZ thresholds are strict (<20 and <60 micromoles per kilogram); values are rounded only at reporting, and percentage changes use unrounded volumes.
- WOA23 volumes are built from grid-cell area and depth-layer thickness; CMIP6 volumes use each model’s own volcello.
- Figure 2’s CESM2 panel is ssp245 OMZ thickness alone because no historical counterpart exists.
