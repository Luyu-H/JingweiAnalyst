# Plan

1. Load WOA23 1971-2000 climatology oxygen, percent_oxygen_saturation and apparent_oxygen_utilization; load landsea_01 and basinmask_01; load the 1-degree cell areas and depth-level boundaries. Build WOA23 ocean-cell volumes and layer thicknesses from areas and level boundaries, restricted by the land-sea mask.
2. Load CMIP6 historical o2, thetao and so for GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, monthly 1971-01 to 2000-12, with each model's volcello, areacello, deptho and depth-level boundaries. Aggregate each variable to day-weighted 1971-2000 means with temporal_aggregate.
3. Load CMIP6 ssp245 o2, thetao and so for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, monthly 2071-01 to 2100-12, with each model's volcello, areacello, deptho and depth-level boundaries. Aggregate each variable to day-weighted 2071-2100 means with temporal_aggregate.
4. For each source/experiment combination (WOA23; GFDL-ESM4 historical; MPI-ESM1-2-LR historical; CESM2 ssp245; GFDL-ESM4 ssp245; MPI-ESM1-2-LR ssp245), use identify_oxygen_minimum_zone on the oxygen field at thresholds 20 and 60 micromoles per kilogram to obtain the strict-below-threshold OMZ masks and column-thickness maps, using the appropriate depth-level boundaries and ocean mask.
5. For each source/experiment and threshold, use compute_exceedance_fraction by volume with the matching cell volumes to obtain the fraction of ocean volume below the threshold, then multiply by the total ocean volume from compute_basic_stat to obtain the total OMZ volume in cubic metres; report in units of 1e15 cubic metres.
6. For each source/experiment at the 60 threshold, apply locate_extreme to the OMZ thickness map to find the maximum column thickness and its latitude and longitude.
7. For GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, compute the percentage change in OMZ volume from historical to ssp245 at both thresholds as (ssp245 minus historical) divided by historical times 100, keeping positive values for an expanding OMZ.
8. For the model oxygen inventories, use temporal-mean thetao and so with compute_potential_density_anomaly to obtain the TEOS-10 density information; use transform_unit to convert oxygen from micromoles per kilogram to moles per kilogram, combine with cell water mass from density and volcello, and use convert_concentration_to_content for the volume integration. Sum globally for GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR historical and for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR ssp245.
9. For WOA23 at the 60 threshold, build the oxygen-below-60 mask and use compute_basic_stat with volume weights to compute the mean apparent_oxygen_utilization and mean percent_oxygen_saturation within that OMZ. Interpret the pair in terms of respiration versus poor ventilation.
10. For basin totals at the 60 threshold, map the WOA23 basinmask_01 codes to Pacific, Indian and Atlantic, folding marginal seas into their parent basins and excluding the Southern and Arctic Oceans. For WOA23, compute volume-exceedance fractions within each basin and multiply by each basin's total volume. For GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR historical, regrid the WOA23 basin mask to each model grid with regrid_horizontal and repeat the basin volume-exceedance calculation using each model's own volumes.
11. Compare the WOA23 total OMZ volumes at 20 and 60 with the GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR historical totals to identify which model is closer to the observed volume; compare the GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR scenario-change percentages for sign and size.
12. Produce Figure 1 from the 60-threshold thickness maps for GFDL-ESM4 historical, MPI-ESM1-2-LR historical and WOA23 on one shared sequential colour scale in metres. Produce Figure 2 from the ssp245-minus-historical 60-threshold thickness for GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR on one shared diverging colour scale centred on zero, plus CESM2 ssp245 60-threshold thickness on its own sequential scale labelled to show that no change can be formed for CESM2.
Other notes:
- CESM2 publishes no historical o2, so no CESM2 historical OMZ, no CESM2 historical-to-ssp245 change and no CESM2 observed-volume comparison can be formed; CESM2 appears only in ssp245 results and its ssp245 map.
- Scenario-change percentages and change maps are possible only for GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR.
- WOA23 is a climatological normal, whereas the CMIP6 historical and ssp245 results are 30-year time means.
- OMZ thresholds are strict: oxygen below 20 and below 60 micromoles per kilogram.
- Basin totals use the WOA23 basin mask, with marginal seas folded into their parent oceans and the Southern and Arctic Oceans excluded.
- Model oxygen inventories require conversion from micromoles per kilogram using the models' thetao/so-derived density and volcello.
