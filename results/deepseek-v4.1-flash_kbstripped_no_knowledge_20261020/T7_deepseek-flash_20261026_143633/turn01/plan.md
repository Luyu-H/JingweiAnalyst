# Plan

1. Load WOA23 annual objectively analysed climatology nitrate and phosphate with load_climatology_data; also load the WOA23 depth levels and layer boundaries with load_depth_levels, the WOA23 basin mask (basinmask_01) with load_basin_mask, and WOA23 grid-cell areas with load_areas for volume weighting.
2. For each CMIP6 model (CESM2, GFDL-ESM4, MPI-ESM1-2-LR), load historical monthly no3 and po4 for 1971-01 to 2000-12 over 200-1000 m with load_regular_data; load each model’s own volcello with load_volumes_for_cmip6 and its depth levels/boundaries with load_depth_levels. If a model does not publish no3 or po4, record it as missing.
3. For each CMIP6 model, use temporal_aggregate to form the DAY-WEIGHTED long-term mean over 1971-2000 for no3 and po4, giving the model 1971-2000 climatology. Treat the WOA23 fields as the annual climatology without temporal aggregation.
4. Restrict all fields to the 200-1000 m layer using load_depth_levels; use each model’s volcello as its volume weights and use WOA23 area × layer thickness from load_areas and load_depth_levels as its volume weights. Use build_comparison_mask on nitrate and phosphate for each source to define the common valid cells for that source.
5. Convert CMIP6 no3 and po4 to micromoles per kilogram at the stated constant density of 1025 kg/m3 using transform_unit; WOA23 nitrate and phosphate are already in micromoles per kilogram. Keep unrounded values for subsequent calculations.
6. For each source, compute the cell-by-cell N:P ratio with compute_ratio on nitrate and phosphate over 200-1000 m. Compute the headline mean N:P as the volume-weighted mean of this ratio field over depth and horizontal space using compute_basic_stat. Separately compute the volume-weighted mean nitrate and mean phosphate with compute_basic_stat and form the second N:P value as their ratio with compute_ratio. Report both clearly labelled, to 2 decimal places.
7. For each source, use build_comparison_mask on the converted nitrate and phosphate fields over 200-1000 m and fit_multivariate_relation with nitrate as the dependent variable and phosphate as the predictor, over all valid cells, each cell weighted equally. Report the OLS slope a to 2 decimal places, intercept b in micromoles per kilogram to 2 decimal places, and R-squared to 3 decimal places.
8. For each source, use compute_exceedance_fraction on the N:P ratio field with the 200-1000 m volume weights to obtain the percentage of water volume with 14 < N:P < 18, and the percentage with N:P < 12 globally. Use select_basin to isolate the Pacific and Indian Oceans, then use compute_exceedance_fraction again for N:P < 12 in each of those basins. Report percentages to 1 decimal place.
9. For each source, use select_basin to isolate the Pacific, Atlantic and Indian Oceans according to the WOA23 basin mask, with marginal seas folded into their parent ocean; exclude Southern and Arctic Oceans from these basin means. Compute the headline mean N:P within each selected basin using compute_basic_stat with the same 200-1000 m volume weights, and report to 2 decimal places.
10. For Figure 2, use compute_basic_stat to reduce the N:P ratio field over the depth dimension only, with volume weights, producing a 200-1000 m mean N:P map for each source. Subtract 16 from each map. For the CMIP6 models only, use regrid_horizontal to put the map on a regular longitude-latitude grid for display. Plot the four maps in a 2-by-2 panel on a common longitude-latitude frame with one shared diverging colour scale centred on zero.
11. For Figure 1, use the converted nitrate and phosphate cell pairs over 200-1000 m for each source and make a 4-panel scatter in the order WOA23, CESM2, GFDL-ESM4, MPI-ESM1-2-LR. Use identical axis ranges and ticks, plot nitrate on y and phosphate on x, overlay each source’s fitted OLS regression line and the Redfield 16:1 line through the origin, make the lines visually distinguishable, and annotate a, b and R-squared.
12. Compare each source’s headline mean N:P with Redfield 16 to classify it as at, above or below Redfield. Compare each model’s 14-18% volume fraction, N:P < 12 global/Pacific/Indian percentages, and Pacific/Atlantic/Indian mean N:P with the corresponding WOA23 values to judge whether the model reproduces the observed low-N:P volume and basin distribution.

Other notes:
- The two mean N:P definitions are reported separately: the headline mean of cell-by-cell N:P, and the ratio of mean nitrate to mean phosphate.
- Regression is ordinary least squares over all valid 200-1000 m cells with each cell weighted equally; means and volume percentages use the stated volume weights.
- CMIP6 nitrate and phosphate are converted to micromoles per kilogram using the constant density 1025 kg/m3 before regression and scatter plotting; N:P ratios are dimensionless.
- WOA23 is the annual observed climatology; CMIP6 values are 1971-2000 long-term climatological means.
- Basin means include only Pacific, Atlantic and Indian Oceans; Southern and Arctic Oceans are excluded from basin means but included in the global N:P < 12 volume percentage.
- Model maps are regridded to a regular longitude-latitude grid for display only.
- If a CMIP6 model does not publish no3 or po4, that model’s result must be reported as missing.
