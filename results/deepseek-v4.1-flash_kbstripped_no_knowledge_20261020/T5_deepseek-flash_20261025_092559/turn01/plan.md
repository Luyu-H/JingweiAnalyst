# Plan

1. Load WOA23 salinity for the 1971-2000 climate normal (annual) with load_climatology_data; load WOA23 depth levels and layer boundaries with load_depth_levels, land-sea mask landsea_01 with load_landsea_mask, basin mask basinmask_01 with load_basin_mask, and WOA23 cell areas with load_areas. Convert salinity to psu with transform_unit if needed.
2. For WOA23, select the levels whose depths lie within 0-10 m (0, 5 and 10 m); compute WOA23 SSS as the full-cell-volume-weighted mean of salinity over those levels using compute_basic_stat weighted by each level's full cell volume formed from WOA23 cell area and layer thickness, with no partial-layer overlap weighting. Keep ocean cells where landsea_01 indicates ocean. Compute the WOA23 global mean 1971-2000 SSS by area-weighting with compute_basic_stat and WOA23 cell areas; report to 2 decimal places.
3. For each of CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, load CMIP6 historical monthly so for 1950-01 to 2014-12 with load_regular_data; load that model's areacello with load_regular_data, volcello with load_volumes_for_cmip6, and depth levels and layer boundaries with load_depth_levels. Convert so to psu with transform_unit if needed. Identify the model levels whose depths lie within 0-10 m and compute monthly SSS at every cell and month as the full-cell-volume-weighted mean over those levels using compute_basic_stat with volcello weights.
4. For each model, load CMIP6 ssp245 monthly so for 2071-01 to 2100-12 with load_regular_data, and its areacello, volcello and depth levels as above; compute ssp245 SSS with the identical 0-10 m full-cell-volume-weighted definition.
5. For each model, compute the 1971-2000 mean SSS from the historical monthly SSS using temporal_aggregate as a day-weighted long-term mean. Area-weight with that model's areacello via compute_basic_stat to get the model global mean 1971-2000 SSS; report to 2 decimal places and subtract the WOA23 global mean to get the model difference to 2 decimal places, using unrounded intermediates.
6. Regrid each model's 1971-2000 SSS climatology to the WOA23 1-degree grid with regrid_horizontal. Build the common comparison cells with build_comparison_mask using the WOA23 ocean mask and valid WOA23/model SSS. Use compute_difference_metrics on those common cells to get RMSE and bias, and retain the model-minus-WOA23 bias field for Figure 1; use analyze_correlation to get the spatial Pearson correlation. Report RMSE to 2 decimal places and correlation to 3 decimal places. Produce Figure 1 as three one-row global bias maps with one shared zero-centred diverging psu colour scale and consistent model colours.
7. For each model, compute the monthly global mean SSS from the monthly SSS using compute_basic_stat with model areacello weights. Use temporal_aggregate to form annual global-mean SSS series for historical 1950-2014 and ssp245 2071-2100. Fit each segment with fit_1D_trend: historical 1950-2014 and ssp245 2071-2100 slopes in psu per decade, 95 per cent confidence intervals, R-squared, and whether each interval excludes zero; report slopes to 3 significant figures. Compute the ssp245/historical slope ratio to 1 decimal place and assess meaningfulness from historical trend significance and stability.
8. For each model, compute cell-by-cell annual-mean SSS over historical 1950-2014 using temporal_aggregate; apply fit_trend_map to get the linear trend and significance at every native cell. For display only, regrid the trend and p-value/significance fields to a regular longitude-latitude grid with regrid_horizontal; produce Figure 2 as three one-row true geographic maps with one shared zero-centred diverging psu per decade colour scale and p &lt; 0.05 stippling, using the same model colours.
9. For each model, compute the ssp245 2071-2100 mean SSS and the historical 1995-2014 mean SSS using temporal_aggregate long-term means on the native grid. Form delta SSS as the ssp245 mean minus the historical mean. Compute the global-ocean delta using compute_basic_stat area-weighted with that model's areacello on common valid ocean cells; report to 2 decimal places.
10. For basin deltas, use load_basin_mask and regrid_horizontal to map the WOA23 basin mask to each model grid for basin selection; use select_basin to isolate the Atlantic, Indian and Pacific basins, folding marginal seas into their parent ocean and excluding the Southern and Arctic Oceans. Compute area-weighted basin means of the native delta SSS with compute_basic_stat and that model's areacello; report each to 2 decimal places. Record the historical and ssp245 variant/ensemble labels for each model to state the ensemble-member caveat.
11. Produce Figure 3 as a single global-mean SSS time-series panel with the historical 1950-2014 and ssp245 2071-2100 segments drawn separately, leaving the 2015-2070 gap empty, each segment overlaid with its own fitted line from fit_1D_trend, y axis labelled in psu, and model colours used consistently across Figures 1-3.
Other notes:
- The historical and ssp245 experiments may use different ensemble members or variant labels for a given model, so the delta and slope ratio can include internal-variability differences rather than only the forced response.
- SSS is defined by the specified 0-10 m levels weighted by each level's full cell volume; no partial-layer overlap weighting is applied, and the definition is identical for models and WOA23.
- Trends are fitted separately to annual global means over 1950-2014 and 2071-2100; the 2015-2070 gap is not interpolated.
- The ssp245/historical slope ratio is only meaningful if the historical slope is significantly nonzero and stable; otherwise it should be described as not meaningful or unstable.
- Basin means use the WOA23 basin mask, with marginal seas folded into their parent ocean and the Southern and Arctic Oceans excluded.
- Bias, RMSE, correlation and Figure 1 use the common WOA23 1-degree comparison cells; Figure 2 model fields are regridded to a regular longitude-latitude grid for display only.
- The WOA23 mean state is the 1971-2000 climatology, while model mean-state bias is computed from the same historical period.
