# Plan

1. Load cmip6_historical monthly fields for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR over 1971-01 to 2000-12: thetao, so, mlotst on Omon, and the fixed areacello field, using load_regular_data for each model and variable.
2. For each model and each month, compute potential density anomaly from thetao and so with compute_potential_density_anomaly.
3. For each model and each month, compute the operator MLD from that density field with compute_mixed_layer_depth using the density criterion that defines mlotst (0.03 kg m-3 sigma-theta difference from the reference/surface level), with the crossing depth interpolated between bracketing levels.
4. For each model and for March and September separately, use build_comparison_mask on the 30 monthly operator-MLD fields and the 30 monthly mlotst fields to retain only cells where both are defined in all 30 instances; apply this mask to the operator MLD and mlotst series.
5. Use temporal_aggregate (climatological-monthly mean) on the masked monthly operator MLD and mlotst series to obtain the 1971-2000 March and September climatologies for each model.
6. On each model/month common mask, compute the operator-minus-mlotst difference field and use compute_difference_metrics to obtain the mean difference/bias, RMSE, and largest absolute departure; use compute_basic_stat with areacello spatial weights to report the area-weighted mean difference; use analyze_correlation to compute the spatial Pearson correlation between operator MLD and mlotst.
7. Use compute_exceedance_fraction by area on |operator MLD - mlotst| with threshold greater than 20 m over the common mask, and use compute_basic_stat with areacello weights on the month-specific common-mask indicator over each model grid to obtain common-mask area as a fraction of the model's total ocean area.
8. Use locate_extreme on each absolute-difference field to identify the location of the largest absolute difference; inspect the difference and exceedance fields/maps to describe the regions where disagreement concentrates.
9. Rank the three models from most to least internally consistent using smaller area-weighted RMSE over the common mask, averaged over the March and September results, as the stated criterion; retain the six sets of bias/RMSD/correlation/exceedance/coverage values.
10. For Figure 1, use regrid_horizontal to place each model/month operator-minus-mlotst field on a regular longitude-latitude grid for display only; make a 3-by-2 panel with rows CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, columns March and September, using one shared diverging colour scale centred on zero.
11. For Figure 2, using the same six common-mask climatology pairs, make 3-by-2 scatter plots with operator MLD on the x axis, mlotst on the y axis, one point per ocean cell, identical-range logarithmic axes and ticks in every panel, a dashed y=x reference line, and each panel annotated with its bias, RMSD and correlation.
Other notes:
- Operator MLD and published mlotst are not expected to agree exactly because mlotst is a model diagnostic computed online on the model's native vertical grid/algorithm, whereas the operator is computed offline from archived thetao/so with interpolation and may differ in surface/reference-level treatment, vertical discretisation, density-criterion implementation and post-processing/archiving.
- The March and September climatologies are restricted to cells where both fields are defined in all 30 Marches or all 30 Septembers; coverage percentages use each model's total ocean area from its own areacello.
- Maps are regridded to a regular longitude-latitude grid for display only; all metrics are computed on each model's native cells.
