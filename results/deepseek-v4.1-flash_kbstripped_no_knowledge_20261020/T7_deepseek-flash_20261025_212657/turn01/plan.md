# Plan

1. Load CMIP6 historical `no3` and `po4` for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR monthly over 1971-01 to 2000-12 with `load_regular_data`; load each model's `volcello` with `load_volumes_for_cmip6`; load each model's depth levels and layer boundaries with `load_depth_levels`. Aggregate the monthly fields to 1971-2000 day-weighted long-term means with `temporal_aggregate`, convert `no3` and `po4` to micromoles per kilogram at constant density 1025 kg/m3 with `transform_unit`, and restrict to the 200-1000 m layer using the depth levels and layer boundaries.
2. Load WOA23 annual `nitrate` and `phosphate` climatology with `load_climatology_data`; load WOA23 depth levels and layer boundaries with `load_depth_levels`; load WOA23 1-degree cell areas with `load_areas` to provide layer volume weights to `compute_basic_stat`. Restrict to the 200-1000 m layer. WOA23 nutrients are already in micromoles per kilogram.
3. Load the WOA23 `basinmask_01` basin mask with `load_basin_mask`; identify the Pacific, Atlantic and Indian basin codes, with marginal seas folded into their parent ocean and Southern and Arctic Oceans excluded from basin statistics. For each CMIP6 model, put the basin mask on that model's native horizontal grid with `regrid_horizontal` so basin selection and basin volume fractions use the WOA23 basin definitions.
4. For each source (WOA23 and each of the three models), compute cell-by-cell N:P with `compute_ratio` from nitrate and phosphate over the 200-1000 m layer, retaining only valid cells where both nutrients are present and phosphate is nonzero. Compute the headline mean N:P as the volume-weighted mean of the cell-by-cell N:P ratio using `compute_basic_stat` with volume weights: CMIP6 `volcello`, and WOA23 layer volumes assembled from `load_areas` and `load_depth_levels`. Separately compute the volume-weighted mean nitrate and volume-weighted mean phosphate with `compute_basic_stat`, then form the ratio of mean nitrate to mean phosphate. Report both mean N:P values to 2 decimal places, clearly labelled.
5. For each source, gather all valid 200-1000 m cells with nitrate and phosphate in micromoles per kilogram, with each cell weighted equally. Fit the ordinary least-squares regression NO3 = a * PO4 + b using `fit_multivariate_relation` with phosphate as predictor and nitrate as response, including an intercept. Report slope a to 2 decimal places, intercept b in micromoles per kilogram to 2 decimal places, and R-squared to 3 decimal places. Use these fitted coefficients for Figure 1.
6. For each source, compute the percentage of water volume with N:P strictly between 14 and 18 using `compute_exceedance_fraction` with volume weights, implemented as the volume fraction above 14 minus the volume fraction at or above 18 so the upper bound is strict. Report to 1 decimal place.
7. For each source and for the Pacific, Atlantic and Indian basins, use `select_basin` with the WOA23 basin mask (marginal seas folded into parent, Southern and Arctic excluded) and compute the headline volume-weighted mean N:P with `compute_basic_stat` and volume weights. Report these basin means to 2 decimal places. Also compute the percentage of water volume with N:P below 12 globally and within the Pacific and Indian basins using `compute_exceedance_fraction` with volume weights, reporting each to 1 decimal place.
8. Compare each source's headline mean N:P with the Redfield value 16 and state whether it is at, above or below Redfield. Compare each model's global low-N:P volume fraction, Pacific and Indian low-N:P volume fractions, and basin mean N:P values against WOA23 to judge whether the model reproduces the observed low-N:P volume and basin distribution.
9. Produce Figure 1 as a 4-panel scatter of nitrate on the y-axis against phosphate on the x-axis in micromoles per kilogram for valid 200-1000 m cells, with panels ordered WOA23, CESM2, GFDL-ESM4, MPI-ESM1-2-LR. Use identical axis ranges and ticks in all panels; show each panel's fitted OLS regression line and the Redfield 16:1 line through the origin with distinguishable styles; annotate each panel with a, b and R-squared.
10. Produce Figure 2 as a 2-by-2 panel of global maps of the 200-1000 m mean N:P minus 16 for WOA23, CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR. Compute each source's 200-1000 m mean N:P per horizontal cell with `compute_basic_stat` over depth using layer/volume weights. For the CMIP6 curvilinear model fields, use `regrid_horizontal` to put the display field on a common regular longitude-latitude grid for display only; WOA23 is already regular. Draw true geographic maps on a common longitude-latitude frame with one shared diverging colour scale centred on zero.

Other notes:
- CMIP6 nitrate and phosphate are converted to micromoles per kilogram at constant density 1025 kg/m3; WOA23 nutrients are already in micromoles per kilogram.
- The headline mean N:P is the volume-weighted mean of the cell-by-cell N:P ratio; the second mean N:P is the ratio of the volume-weighted mean nitrate to the volume-weighted mean phosphate.
- The regression is ordinary least squares over valid cells with each cell weighted equally, not volume-weighted.
- Volume fractions and basin means are volume-weighted using CMIP6 `volcello` and WOA23 cell area and layer thickness; the WOA23 basin mask defines basins with marginal seas folded into their parent ocean and Southern and Arctic Oceans excluded from basin statistics.
- The N:P interval 14 to 18 and the threshold below 12 are treated as strict thresholds.
- Figure 2 model fields are regridded to a regular longitude-latitude grid for display only; all statistical calculations remain on the native grids.
- CMIP6 values are 1971-2000 day-weighted long-term means; WOA23 nutrients are annual objectively analysed climatology values.
- The Redfield reference is N:P = 16, and the Figure 1 Redfield line is drawn through the origin.
- Only final reported values are rounded; intermediate calculations use unrounded results.
