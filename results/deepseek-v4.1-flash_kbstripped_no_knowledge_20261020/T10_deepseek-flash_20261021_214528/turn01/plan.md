# Plan

1. Load WOA23 climatology on the 1971-2000 climate normal: oxygen, percent_oxygen_saturation and apparent_oxygen_utilization via load_climatology_data; load WOA23 landsea_01 and basinmask_01 via load_landsea_mask and load_basin_mask; load WOA23 depth levels and layer boundaries via load_depth_levels.
2. Load CMIP6 historical monthly o2, thetao and so for GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, 1971-01 to 2000-12, via load_regular_data. Load each model’s volcello and areacello via load_volumes_for_cmip6 and load_areas, and depth levels via load_depth_levels. Use temporal_aggregate to form each model’s day-weighted 1971-2000 long-term mean of o2, thetao and so.
3. Load CMIP6 ssp245 monthly o2, thetao and so for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, 2071-01 to 2100-12, via load_regular_data. Load each model’s volcello, areacello and depth levels as in step 2, and use temporal_aggregate to form each model’s day-weighted 2071-2100 long-term mean of o2, thetao and so.
4. For the six source-and-experiment combinations where oxygen exists (WOA23; GFDL-ESM4 historical; MPI-ESM1-2-LR historical; CESM2 ssp245; GFDL-ESM4 ssp245; MPI-ESM1-2-LR ssp245), apply identify_oxygen_minimum_zone to the oxygen field with thresholds strictly below 20 and strictly below 60 micromoles per kilogram. Use the resulting 3-D OMZ masks and column OMZ thickness fields. Use locate_extreme on the 60-threshold thickness field to obtain the maximum OMZ thickness and its latitude and longitude. Compute total OMZ volume at each threshold using compute_exceedance_fraction by volume with the native cell volumes, reporting in 1e15 cubic metres; where needed, cross-check by applying the OMZ mask to volcello/cell volume and summing with compute_basic_stat.
5. For GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, calculate the percentage change in OMZ volume from historical 1971-2000 to ssp245 2071-2100 at the 20 and 60 thresholds as 100*(ssp245 - historical)/historical, positive for an expanding OMZ. Use unrounded volumes from step 4.
6. For GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR historical, and for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR ssp245, compute potential density anomaly from thetao and so with compute_potential_density_anomaly using TEOS-10, convert to density, and use convert_concentration_to_content with o2 and each model’s native volcello to compute the global dissolved oxygen inventory in moles. Sum over the global ocean with compute_basic_stat as needed.
7. For WOA23 and the two historical models, align the WOA23 basin mask to each model grid with regrid_horizontal (nearest neighbour) and use select_basin to restrict the 60-threshold OMZ volume/mask to the Pacific, Indian and Atlantic basins, excluding the Southern and Arctic Oceans and folding marginal seas into their parent ocean as defined by the WOA23 basin mask. Compute each basin’s OMZ volume in 1e15 cubic metres using compute_exceedance_fraction by volume or compute_basic_stat on the native volume field restricted by the OMZ and basin masks.
8. For WOA23, use the 60-threshold OMZ mask from step 4 and compute_basic_stat with volume weighting to obtain the mean apparent_oxygen_utilization and mean percent_oxygen_saturation within the OMZ. Interpret high AOU with low saturation as evidence for respiration-dominated oxygen consumption, and low AOU with relatively high saturation as evidence for poor ventilation.
9. Compare the historical GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR 60-threshold OMZ volumes with the WOA23 observed value to determine which model reproduces the observed OMZ volume better. Compare the GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR percentage changes at both thresholds to assess whether they agree on the sign and approximate size of the scenario change.
10. For Figure 1, use regrid_horizontal to put the historical 60-threshold OMZ thickness fields for GFDL-ESM4, MPI-ESM1-2-LR and WOA23 on a common 1-degree grid, and plot three global maps in one row on one shared sequential colour scale in metres. For Figure 2, compute ssp245 minus historical 60-threshold OMZ thickness for GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, regrid to the common grid, and plot them in one row on a shared diverging colour scale centred on zero; regrid CESM2’s ssp245 60-threshold OMZ thickness to the same grid and plot it on its own sequential scale, labelled that no CESM2 change can be formed because CESM2 has no historical o2.

Other notes:
- CESM2 publishes no historical o2, so CESM2 cannot contribute a historical OMZ volume, an inventory for historical, a historical-to-ssp245 percentage change, or a change map; only CESM2 ssp245 OMZ thickness and inventory are computed.
- WOA23 oxygen is the 1971-2000 climate normal; CMIP6 historical is 1971-2000 and ssp245 is 2071-2100, each aggregated to a day-weighted long-term mean before OMZ identification.
- OMZ volumes are total water volumes with oxygen strictly below the stated threshold; thresholds are 20 and 60 micromoles per kilogram.
- Volume and inventory calculations use each model’s native grids and native volcello; horizontal regridding is used only for map display and for aligning the WOA23 basin mask to model grids.
- Basin totals use the WOA23 basin mask; marginal seas are folded into their parent ocean, and the Southern and Arctic Oceans are excluded from the Pacific/Indian/Atlantic totals.
- Percentage change is positive for an expanding OMZ and is computed from unrounded volumes.
- Inventory conversion uses TEOS-10 density from thetao and so to convert oxygen concentrations in micromoles per kilogram to moles per cell.
- AOU and percent oxygen saturation means within the WOA23 60-threshold OMZ are volume-weighted.
- Values are rounded only when reported: OMZ volumes and inventories to 3 significant figures, maximum thickness to 0.1 m, coordinates to 0.01 degree, percentage changes to 0.01, and AOU/percent saturation to 0.1.
