# Plan

1. Load CMIP6 historical `so`, `areacello` and `volcello` for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, monthly 1950-01 to 2014-12, using `load_regular_data` and `load_volumes_for_cmip6`; load each model’s depth levels with `load_depth_levels`. Load CMIP6 ssp245 `so` for the same three models, monthly 2071-01 to 2100-12, with matching `areacello` and `volcello`.
2. Load WOA23 salinity for the 1971-2000 climate normal, annual, with `load_climatology_data`; load the WOA23 1-degree `landsea_01` mask (ocean > 1) with `load_landsea_mask`, the `basinmask_01` basin mask with `load_basin_mask`, WOA 1-degree cell areas with `load_areas`, and WOA depth levels/boundaries with `load_depth_levels`.
3. Compute SSS at each cell for each model and for WOA23: select levels whose depths lie within 0-10 m (WOA23: 0, 5 and 10 m; models: the available one or two levels), and take a depth-weighted mean using full cell volume as weights, not overlap with 0-10 m. Use `compute_basic_stat` with its weighted operator: CMIP6 weights from `volcello`, WOA23 weights from cell area times layer thickness from `load_areas` and `load_depth_levels`. For CMIP6, apply this at each monthly time; for WOA23, to the annual climatology. Keep salinity in psu.
4. For the 1971-2000 mean state, use `temporal_aggregate` to form the 1971-2000 day-weighted long-term mean SSS at each model cell. Compute each model’s global mean with `compute_basic_stat` using native `areacello` weights and valid ocean cells; compute the WOA23 global mean with `compute_basic_stat` using WOA area weights. Report each model and WOA23 global mean to 2 dp, and each model-minus-WOA23 difference to 2 dp.
5. For bias/RMSE/correlation and Figure 1, regrid each model’s 1971-2000 SSS climatology to the WOA23 1-degree grid with `regrid_horizontal`; build the common valid comparison cells with `build_comparison_mask` using the WOA23 ocean mask and valid model/WOA23 cells. Compute bias and RMSE with `compute_difference_metrics` and spatial Pearson correlation with `analyze_correlation`; report RMSE to 2 dp and correlation to 3 dp. Retain the cell-by-cell model-minus-WOA23 bias field for Figure 1: three global maps in one row, one shared diverging colour scale centred on zero, colour bar labelled in psu.
6. For the historical global trend, use `temporal_aggregate` to produce annual-mean SSS fields for 1950-2014 for each model, then compute the annual global mean SSS with `compute_basic_stat` using `areacello` weights. Fit the annual global-mean series with `fit_1D_trend`; report the slope in psu per decade to 3 significant figures, its 95 per cent confidence interval, R-squared, and whether the interval excludes zero.
7. For the ssp245 global trend, repeat the annual-mean and global-mean calculation for 2071-2100 ssp245 SSS with `temporal_aggregate` and `compute_basic_stat`, then fit with `fit_1D_trend`; report slope, 95 per cent confidence interval, R-squared and whether the interval excludes zero. Compute the ratio of the ssp245 slope to the historical slope to 1 dp and state whether that ratio is meaningful.
8. For Figure 2, compute annual-mean SSS fields for 1950-2014 with `temporal_aggregate`, then use `fit_trend_map` at every native model cell to obtain the cell-by-cell linear trend in psu per decade and p < 0.05 significance. For display only, regrid the trend and significance fields to a common regular longitude-latitude grid with `regrid_horizontal`. Plot three true geographic maps in one row on a common longitude-latitude frame, with one shared diverging colour scale centred on zero, and stipple cells significant at p < 0.05.
9. For delta SSS, compute each model’s ssp245 2071-2100 mean SSS and historical 1995-2014 mean SSS at each cell with `temporal_aggregate`, and form the difference field. Regrid the delta to the WOA23 1-degree grid with `regrid_horizontal` and apply the common ocean/comparison mask. Use `compute_basic_stat` with area weights for the global-ocean mean change. Use `select_basin` with the WOA23 basin mask for the Atlantic, Indian and Pacific basins, excluding the Southern and Arctic Oceans, with marginal seas folded into their parent ocean, and compute area-weighted basin means with `compute_basic_stat`. Report all delta SSS values in psu to 2 dp.
10. For Figure 3, use the annual global-mean SSS series already computed for each model for historical 1950-2014 and ssp245 2071-2100. Plot one time-series panel with the two segments drawn separately and the 2015-2070 gap left empty, overlay each segment with its fitted line from `fit_1D_trend`, label the y-axis in psu, and use the same model colours as in Figures 1 and 2.
Other notes:
- The WOA23 comparison is against the 1971-2000 annual climate normal; model mean-state values are averages over the same period from monthly historical output.
- SSS is defined from levels within 0-10 m weighted by full cell volume; for CMIP6 this uses `volcello`, and for WOA23 it uses cell area times layer thickness from the WOA depth boundaries.
- Bias maps, RMSE and correlation are computed on the WOA23 1-degree grid after regridding; model global means are computed on each model’s native grid with its own `areacello`.
- Historical and ssp245 may use different ensemble members or variant labels for a model; the delta SSS combines the two experiments and should be interpreted with that ensemble-member caveat.
- Basin means use the WOA23 basin mask, with marginal seas folded into parent oceans and the Southern and Arctic Oceans excluded.
- The ratio of ssp245 slope to historical slope is meaningful only if the historical slope is distinguishable from zero and the two experiment segments are comparable; otherwise report it as not meaningful.
- If any model does not publish `so`, `areacello` or `volcello`, report that model’s result as missing rather than substituting another field.
