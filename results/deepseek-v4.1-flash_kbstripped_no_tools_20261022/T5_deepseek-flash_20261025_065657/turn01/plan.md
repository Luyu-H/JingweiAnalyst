# Plan

1. Load WOA23 annual salinity climatology for 1971-2000 on its 1-degree grid, plus `landsea_01` and `basinmask_01`. Load CMIP6 historical `so` monthly 1950-01 to 2014-12 and ssp245 `so` monthly 2071-01 to 2100-12 for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, with each experiment's `areacello`, `volcello`, grid coordinates and time coordinates.
- Note [cmip6-grid-labels]: GFDL-ESM4 is `gr`; its SSS and `areacello` are already on the 1x1 degree archive grid with a regridded land-sea mask, so its native numbers are archive-grid numbers. CESM2 (about 100 km) and MPI (about 250 km) differ in resolution, so features narrower than the coarse grid are not comparable in amplitude on the bias maps.
- Note [cmip6-so-practical-salinity]: model `so` and WOA23 salinity are both practical salinity; difference them directly in psu without any 0.001 or 1000 conversion.

2. Compute SSS for each model month by selecting salinity levels whose depths lie within 0-10 m inclusive (one or two model levels) and taking the weighted mean with the full `volcello` of each selected level as its weight; do not use the level's overlap with 0-10 m. For WOA23, select the 0, 5 and 10 m salinity levels and apply the same full-level-volume weighting, constructing the 1-degree full cell volume from the WOA cell area and the full level thickness. Restrict WOA23 fields to ocean cells where `landsea_01` is greater than 1. Keep model SSS on native grids and WOA23 SSS on the 1-degree grid.

3. For each model, form the 1971-2000 mean SSS and regrid it to the WOA23 1-degree grid for comparison. Build one shared comparison mask from the WOA23 land-sea mask with `build_comparison_mask` using `ocean_only=True`, and apply `.where(mask)` to every model and WOA field before bias, RMSE or pattern correlation. Report the mask cell count beside the metrics.
- Note [common-comparison-mask]: use the single common mask, and use the `mean_data` and `mean_reference` returned over that shared sample by `compute_difference_metrics` for the means behind the bias, not global means each computed over its own ocean.
- Note [anomaly-baselines-and-climate-indices]: the bias and WOA reference are against the WOA23 1971-2000 climate normal; state this baseline, and note that series built on different climatological baselines differ by a constant offset.

4. On the shared comparison mask, compute for each model the area-weighted mean SSS, bias versus WOA23, RMSE, and spatial Pearson correlation with WOA23. Obtain the mean values from `compute_difference_metrics` (`mean_data`, `mean_reference`) and use area weighting for the pattern correlation.
- Note [omip-weighted-means-and-integrals]: use the sum of SSS times `areacello` divided by the sum of `areacello`, with the weight NaN-masked to the same valid cells; `compute_difference_metrics` is area-weighted by default and the correlation must be called with `weighting='area'`.

5. For each model and each experiment separately, compute annual global mean SSS from the monthly SSS fields by area-weighting with that experiment's `areacello` over valid ocean cells in a NaN-aware way, then fit a linear trend to annual means over historical 1950-2014 and over ssp245 2071-2100, separately. Use `method='linear'`, effective sample size, and report the corrected slope, its 95 per cent confidence interval, R-squared, and whether the interval excludes zero. Compute the ssp245/historical slope ratio to 1 decimal place and explain its meaning.
- Note [serial-correlation-and-significance]: `fit_1D_trend` applies the effective sample size along time by default and returns the unadjusted values alongside; quote the corrected values, fit `method='linear'` explicitly, and call a trend significant only if its 95 per cent interval excludes zero.
- Note [historical-ssp245-member-continuity]: CESM2 historical is r1i1p1f1 while its ssp245 is r10i1p1f1, so its delta SSS and slope ratio include internal-variability differences between two members; GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR pair r1 with r1 and are continuous. Do not fit one trend across the 2014/2015 join; each segment gets its own fitted line and the gap is left empty.

6. Compute each model's delta SSS as the ssp245 2071-2100 mean minus the historical 1995-2014 mean. For the global ocean and for Atlantic, Indian and Pacific basins, bring the delta SSS field and the model's `areacello` to the WOA23 1-degree grid and apply the WOA23 basin mask, using area-weighted means over the same valid cells; select only the Atlantic, Indian and Pacific basins, excluding the Southern and Arctic Oceans as separate basins. Report the [historical-ssp245-member-continuity] caveat with the delta results.
- Note [basin-and-region-definitions]: use the WOA23 basin mask (`select_basin` and `load_basin_mask`) with `include_marginal_seas=True`, folding marginal seas into their parent ocean; compare basin means only under this same mask.

7. Figure 1: three panels in one row, one per model, of SSS bias (model 1971-2000 climatology minus WOA23) on the common comparison-mask cells. Use one shared diverging colour scale centred on zero with the colour bar labelled psu; use each model's consistent colour for panel labels or outlines.

8. Figure 2: for each model, compute the cell-by-cell linear trend of annual-mean SSS over 1950-2014 using `method='linear'` with the effective sample size and retain cells significant at p &lt; 0.05. For display only, put curvilinear model fields on a regular longitude-latitude grid and draw true geographic maps on a common frame. Use one shared diverging colour scale centred on zero in psu per decade and stipple cells significant at p &lt; 0.05 on top of the trend field. This step uses [serial-correlation-and-significance] for the trend and significance convention.

9. Figure 3: plot the global mean SSS time series for all three models, with historical 1950-2014 and ssp245 2071-2100 drawn as separate segments and the 2015-2070 gap left empty. Overlay each segment with its own fitted line from step 5, label the y axis in psu, and use each model's colour consistently with Figures 1 and 2.

Other notes:
- The 0-10 m SSS is defined with full cell-volume weights for the selected levels, not overlap weights, and the same full-volume convention is applied to WOA23.
