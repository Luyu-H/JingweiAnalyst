[cmip6-bgc-units-per-volume] (must)

[monthly-means-are-time-averages] (must)
- f(monthly mean) ≠ mean of f: oxygen-threshold volumes and AOU from monthly means depend on order; state whether the diagnostic was formed first or the mean state first (WOA is a climatology, so average-then-diagnose is the like-for-like choice).
- Average a period with `temporal_aggregate` (day weights in the model calendar); plain `compute_basic_stat(op='mean', dim='time')` weights months equally and is not a time average.

[cmip6-o2sat-and-derived-oxygen] (should)
- `identify_oxygen_minimum_zone` and any threshold comparison evaluate in the field's own units, so a mol m-3 `o2` field must be converted first or the whole ocean is flagged as below the threshold.
- WOA23 percent saturation and AOU rest on the WOA convention/pressure assumptions, not the OMIP (Garcia & Gordon 1992, 0.20946 O2 mole fraction, `P_a/P⁰_a`) recipe; treat model–WOA AOU or %sat differences as including a definitional component.

[missing-values-and-land-cells] (should)
- Read 1e20 as NaN (default xarray decoding); a value near 1e20 in output is an unmasked fill. Build each field's valid mask from the field itself — masks differ between variables of the same model — and mask the weights (volcello/areacello) to the same cells.
- Use `compute_exceedance_fraction` for the below-threshold volumes: it keeps missing cells out of both numerator and denominator.

[cmip6-static-grid-fields] (should)
- The archive has no `thkcello`/`masscello`: take column thickness as volcello/areacello (`cell_thickness_like`), not the `lev_bnds` difference, which fails at partial bottom cells (14% of GFDL-ESM4 cells differ >1%).
- Use `deptho` (model bathymetry) or the field's own fill pattern, not external topography, to decide whether a level is above the sea floor.

[omip-native-grid-vs-regridded] (should)
- Compute every number (OMZ volumes, basin volumes, inventory, max thickness) on each source's native grid with its own weights; regrid to the WOA 1° grid only for the Figure 1/2 maps, and build one shared mask before cell-by-cell statistics.
- Do not vertically interpolate for OMZ thickness: integrate thickness on the original levels (`regrid_vertical` would change layer-integrated quantities); interpolation to fixed depths is for comparison only.

[omip-weighted-means-and-integrals] (should)
- Volume-weight every mean and integral: OMZ volume is Σ volcello over valid cells below the threshold (a share of volume, not of cells); Pacific/Indian/Atlantic volumes apply the basin mask on the model's own grid.
- For a map-wide statistic (RMSE, bias, pattern correlation against WOA) weight by area: `compute_difference_metrics` does by default, `analyze_correlation` needs `weighting='area'`.
