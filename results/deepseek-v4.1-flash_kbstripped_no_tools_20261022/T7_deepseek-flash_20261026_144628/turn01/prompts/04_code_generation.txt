Plan from the previous step:
1. **WOA23 nitrate & phosphate, annual climatology.** Open the WOA23 raw files and read the objectively analysed annual fields of `nitrate` and `phosphate` (the single all-period nutrient composite; time record labelled "00"/annual). Decode the 1e20 land/fill value to NaN and keep each field's own valid mask. Build the 200–1000 m layer value at every horizontal grid cell as the thickness-weighted mean over the standard depth levels lying in 200–1000 m, with cell volume computed from the file's own latitude/longitude/depth bounds (cos(lat) × Δlon × Δz) — no plain average over the levels or over lat/lon.

2. **WOA23 basin mask.** Read `basinmask_01` from the WOA23 files and derive the Pacific, Atlantic and Indian labels with marginal seas folded into their parent ocean (e.g. the `select_basin(..., include_marginal_seas=True)` operation), with `depth_dependent=False` so the surface labels apply through the whole 200–1000 m layer.
   - Note [basin-and-region-definitions]: use the WOA23 mask with `include_marginal_seas=True` and `depth_dependent=False` for the named-ocean 200–1000 m basin means; this is WOA labelling, not the OMIP 10-region mask, and Southern and Arctic are separate basins that this analysis excludes.

3. **CMIP6 nitrate & phosphate time series.** For CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, open the `cmip6_historical` `Omon` files and read `no3` and `po4` for 1971-01 to 2000-12 (all members as published; use the single `r1i1p1f1`-like historical member the model provides). Decode the 1e20 fill value to NaN. Convert both tracers from mmol m-3 to µmol kg-1 with µmol kg-1 = mmol m-3 × 1000 / ρ using the question's constant ρ = 1025 kg m-3.
   - Note [cmip6-bgc-units-per-volume]: apply µmol kg-1 = mmol m-3 × 1000 / ρ with the stated ρ = 1025 kg m-3 and state this constant-density assumption in the answer (the reference procedure would use in-situ density below the surface); ratios of the two tracers are unchanged by the conversion, but a mol m-3 × 1e3 value must never be labelled µmol kg-1.

4. **CMIP6 1971–2000 climatology and 200–1000 m layer means.** For each model form the monthly climatology as the plain mean of each calendar month across the 30 years, then average the 12 monthly climatologies to the 1971–2000 annual-mean state; do not day-weight and do not equal-weight months into a period mean. Build the 200–1000 m layer value of each tracer at every horizontal cell as Σ(x·`volcello`)/Σ(`volcello`) over the model levels inside the layer, with the weights masked to the same valid cells; read each model's `volcello` (and `deptho` for ocean-point definition) from its `Ofx` fixed fields.
   - Note [monthly-means-are-time-averages]: the 1971–2000 climatology is the plain mean of each calendar month across years (no day weights), and every N:P in this plan is computed from the climatological-mean `no3`/`po4` fields, i.e. it is the stoichiometry of the mean state (like-for-like with WOA23), not the mean of instantaneous ratios — state that order.
   - Note [cmip6-static-grid-fields]: use `volcello` for the 200–1000 m layer volume and for every volume weight, not `lev_bnds` differences; `deptho` is the model bathymetry and its own valid cells/`deptho` decide the ocean points.

5. **Cell-by-cell N:P and the two global means.** At every cell where both `no3` and `po4` are valid (from each field's own mask, weights masked to those cells) compute N:P = nitrate/phosphate for WOA23 and each model. Report (i) the headline mean of the cell-by-cell N:P, volume-weighted as Σ(N:P·w)/Σw with w the WOA23 cell volume or the model `volcello` restricted to the layer, and (ii) the ratio of the volume-weighted mean nitrate to the volume-weighted mean phosphate.
   - Note [omip-weighted-means-and-integrals]: volume-weight every mean as Σ x·`volcello`/Σ `volcello` with weights masked to the valid cells (for WOA23 the cos-lat × Δlon × Δz cell volume from step 1), and report the cell-by-cell N:P mean and the ratio of volume-weighted means separately; the question's equal-cell-weight OLS departs from the area/volume-weighted advice — follow the question and state both choices.
   - Note [missing-values-and-land-cells]: decode the 1e20 fill to NaN, build each field's mask from itself, compute N:P only where both `no3` and `po4` are valid, and mask the volume weights to those cells (a near-1e20 output is an unmasked fill).

6. **Equal-cell-weight OLS regression.** Over all valid 200–1000 m cells of each of the four sources, fit NO3 = a·PO4 + b by ordinary least squares with every cell weighted equally, using the µmol kg-1 values from step 3 (WOA23 is already in µmol kg-1). Report the slope a and the intercept b in µmol kg-1, and R², to the requested precision; keep unrounded values for the figure annotations and the Redfield comparison.

7. **Volume shares of the N:P distribution.** Compute the percentage of water volume with N:P strictly between 14 and 18, and the percentage with N:P strictly below 12, for each of the four sources, globally and within the Pacific and Indian basins, using the step-5 valid cells and volume weights (the `compute_exceedance_fraction` operation: missing cells excluded from numerator and denominator, returning the volume behind the percentage along with the percentage).

8. **Basin means of the headline N:P.** For WOA23, average the cell-by-cell N:P volume-weighted within each of the Pacific, Atlantic and Indian labels from step 2 (marginal seas folded in, Southern and Arctic excluded). For each CMIP6 model, sample the WOA23 `basinmask_01` labels at the model cell centres (nearest neighbour on the curvilinear grid) and apply the same volume-weighted average with `volcello`, restricted to the valid-cell masks of step 5.

9. **Assessment against Redfield and against the observation.** Compare each source's headline mean N:P and its fitted slope with 16 to place it at, above or below Redfield, and compare the modelled low-N:P volume share (<12, global/Pacific/Indian) and its basin distribution with WOA23's to judge whether each model reproduces the denitrification signature; use the models' differing N:P formulations to interpret any cross-model spread.
   - Info [bgc-model-structure-comparison]: interpret the cross-model N:P spread through model structure (GFDL fixed N:P per plankton type, MPI prescribed Redfield, CESM2 variable C:P); "not detailed in the paper" does not mean the mechanism is absent.

Other notes:
- [cmip6-bgc-units-per-volume] The µmol kg-1 conversion uses the question's constant ρ = 1025 kg m-3 and this assumption is stated in the answer; the reference protocol would use in-situ density below the surface, which matters for the absolute µmol kg-1 values (and hence the OLS intercept) but not for N:P.
- [monthly-means-are-time-averages] All N:P values are computed from climatological-mean `no3`/`po4` (the diagnostic of the mean state, matching WOA23's objectively analysed climatology), not as a time average of instantaneous ratios; N:P is nonlinear so the two differ.
- [omip-weighted-means-and-integrals] The OLS is fitted with equal cell weights as the question prescribes, whereas the reference protocol would area/volume-weight; both choices are stated, and the headline mean N:P (cell-by-cell, volume-weighted) is reported separately from the ratio of volume-weighted means.

def get_depth_info(ds, lev_name):

def umol_kg_factor(units, rho=RHO):
    """Factor converting the declared concentration units to umol kg-1."""
    u = str(units).lower().replace(" ", "")

def read_basin_mask(path):
    rows = []
    with open(path, "r") as f:
        for line in f:
            s = line.strip()
            if not s or s.startswith("#"):
                continue
            parts = s.split()
            if len(parts) < 3:
                continue
            vals = []
            ok = True
            for p in parts:
                p2 = p.replace("*", "nan").replace("D", "E").replace("d", "e")
                try:
                    vals.append(float(p2))
                except ValueError:
                    ok = False
                    break
            if ok and len(vals) >= 3:
                rows.append(vals)
    if not rows:
        raise ValueError("No numeric rows in basin mask")
    maxlen = max(len(r) for r in rows)
    arr = np.full((len(rows), maxlen), np.nan, dtype=float)
    for i, r in enumerate(rows):
        arr[i, : len(r)] = r
    return arr


def detect_latlon_cols(arr):
    ncol = arr.shape[1]
    candidates = list(range(min(4, ncol)))

        if not np.any(np.isfinite(arr[:, i])):

        if np.nanmin(arr[:, i]) >= -90.1 and np.nanmax(arr[:, i]) <= 90.1:

def vol_exceedance(np_ratio, vol, cond, extra_mask=None):
    m = np.isfinite(np_ratio) & np.isfinite(vol) & (vol > 0.0)
    if extra_mask is not None:
        m = m & extra_mask
    total = np.nansum(vol[m])
    num = np.nansum(vol[m & cond])
    pct = 100.0 * num / total if total > 0.0 else np.nan
    return pct, total, num


def compute_stats(np_ratio, nitrate, phosphate, vol, basin=None):

            "ratio_means": np.nan,

def load_cmip6_clim(model, var):
    files = sorted(glob.glob(os.path.join(cmip6_root, model, var, "*.nc")))
    if len(files) == 0:
        return None, None

    ds = xr.open_mfdataset(
        files,
        combine="by_coords",
        use_cftime=True,
        decode_times=True,
    )

    vname = var if var in ds.data_vars else None
    if vname is None:
        for v in ds.data_vars:
            if v.lower() == var.lower():
                vname = v
                break
    if vname is None:
        vname = list(ds.data_vars)[0]

    da = ds[vname]
    units = da.attrs.get("units", "")

    time = ds["time"]
    mask_t = ((time.dt.year >= 1971) & (time.dt.year <= 2000)).values
    da = da.isel(time=mask_t)
    da = da.where(np.abs(da) < FILL_LIMIT)

    clim = da.groupby("time.month").mean("time")
    annual = clim.mean("month")

    factor = umol_kg_factor(units, RHO)
    annual = annual * factor
    annual.attrs["units"] = "micromoles_per_kilogram"
    return annual, ds


def load_cmip6_fixed(model, var):
    files = sorted(glob.glob(os.path.join(cmip6_root, model, var, "*.nc")))
    if len(files) == 0:
        return None, None

    ds = xr.open_mfdataset(
        files,
        combine="by_coords",
        use_cftime=True,
        decode_times=True,
    )

    vname = var if var in ds.data_vars else list(ds.data_vars)[0]
    da = ds[vname]

    if "time" in da.dims:
        time = ds["time"]
        mt = ((time.dt.year >= 1971) & (time.dt.year <= 2000)).values
        if np.any(mt):
            da = da.isel(time=mt).mean("time")
        else:
            da = da.isel(time=0)

    da = da.where(np.abs(da) < FILL_LIMIT)
    return da, ds

def regrid_to_regular(lat2d, lon2d, field):
    """Nearest/linear interpolation of a curvilinear field to 1 deg lon-lat."""
    lat = np.asarray(lat2d).ravel()
    lon = np.asarray(lon2d).ravel()
    vals = np.asarray(field).ravel()

    lon = ((lon + 180.0) % 360.0) - 180.0
    valid = np.isfinite(vals) & np.isfinite(lat) & np.isfinite(lon)

    pts = np.column_stack([lon[valid], lat[valid]])
    vals_v = vals[valid]

    # Duplicate points across the dateline for interpolation near +/-180
    pts_all = np.vstack(
        [pts, pts + np.array([360.0, 0.0]), pts - np.array([360.0, 0.0])]
    )
    vals_all = np.concatenate([vals_v, vals_v, vals_v])

    reg = griddata(
        pts_all,
        vals_all,
        (lon2d_reg, lat2d_reg),
        method="linear",
        fill_value=np.nan,
    )
    if np.any(np.isnan(reg)):
        reg_nn = griddata(
            pts_all,
            vals_all,
            (lon2d_reg, lat2d_reg),
            method="nearest",
        )
        reg = np.where(np.isnan(reg), reg_nn, reg)
    return reg
