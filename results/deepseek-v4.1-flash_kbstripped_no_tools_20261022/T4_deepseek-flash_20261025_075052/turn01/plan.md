# Plan

1. **Load the CMIP6 historical fields.** From `cmip6_historical`, open the monthly `thetao`, `so` and `mlotst` files for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, and each model's fixed `areacello`, reading the raw NetCDF with xarray. Restrict the monthly fields to 1971-01 … 2000-12 and split them into the 30 March and 30 September records per model. Keep every model on its own native grid and coordinate set (no common grid is imposed at this stage); report a variable a model does not publish as missing.
   - Note [cmip6-grid-labels]: GFDL-ESM4 is `gr`, so its values and mask are already remapped to 1° and its comparison is on that archive grid, not the native one; CESM2 (~100 km) and MPI (~250 km) differ in resolution by ~2.5×, so features narrower than the coarse grid are not comparable in amplitude and will be flagged where the disagreement concentrates.

2. **Compute the operator MLD from each monthly `thetao`/`so` profile.** For every cell and every one of the 30 monthly records of each month, build the vertical density profile from potential temperature (`thetao`) and practical salinity (`so`) converted to Absolute Salinity with the location-dependent gsw conversion, evaluate density with the potential-temperature route at the local pressure of each level, and find the shallowest level where the displaced-parcel buoyancy difference relative to the reference level exceeds the mlotst threshold. Linearly interpolate the crossing depth between the bracketing levels. Pass the model's shallowest level depth as the reference depth, since `mlotst` is referenced to the ocean surface, not 10 m. Where the threshold is never reached in the column (GFDL-ESM4 and MPI fill roughly the full column depth, CESM2 is often 0 m), drop the cell and record the excluded area.
   - Note [cmip6-mlotst-definition]: apply the criterion exactly as displaced-parcel δB > ΔB_crit = 0.0003 m s-2 (≈ Δρ_crit = 0.03 kg m-3) with linear interpolation between bracketing levels and `ref_depth` = the model's shallowest level; drop columns where the threshold is never reached and report the excluded area; state the three residual causes (reference level, displaced-parcel-at-local-pressure vs σ0 density form, and instantaneous-MLD averaging vs MLD of monthly means, with the offline MLD expected to be shallower) and do not report them as model error; note that GFDL-ESM4's native 75 hybrid layers were remapped to 35 z levels.
   - Note [cmip6-thetao-potential-temperature]: `thetao` is potential temperature and no `bigthetao` exists, so density must be taken through the potential-temperature route; the in-situ route biases density increasingly with depth.
   - Note [cmip6-so-practical-salinity]: `so` is practical salinity with units "0.001" and stored values ~35 — no ×0.001 or ×1000; convert S_P to S_A with the location-dependent gsw function before any TEOS-10 density call, otherwise the MLD bias varies by region.

3. **Form the March and September climatologies and the common mask.** For each model and each calendar month, compute the plain mean of the 30 monthly operator MLDs and the plain mean of the 30 monthly `mlotst` values (diagnostic first, average second). Build one mask per model and month from the cells where both the operator MLD and `mlotst` are defined in all 30 instances of that month, and apply it to both climatologies before any statistic.
   - Note [monthly-means-are-time-averages]: compute the MLD of each monthly profile first and then average the monthly MLDs; never average `thetao`/`so` first, and state that the diagnostic-first order was used because f(monthly mean) ≠ mean of f; each March/September climatology is the plain mean of that month's values across the 30 years, with no day weights.
   - Note [common-comparison-mask]: build one mask over the two fields and apply it to every field before scoring, and keep WOA23's land-sea mask out of this comparison (WOA23 is not part of it).
   - Note [missing-values-and-land-cells]: derive each field's valid mask from the field itself, reduce NaN-aware with the weights masked to the same cells, and keep land missing so it stays out of the area fractions; a value near 1e20 in the output is an unmasked fill value, not a result.

4. **Score the two fields on the common mask with the model's own cell areas.** Using `areacello` on the mask from step 3, compute for each of the six model-and-month cases: the area-weighted mean of (operator MLD − `mlotst`), the area-weighted RMS difference, the area-weighted spatial Pearson correlation of the two fields, the percentage of the common-mask area where |difference| > 20 m, and the common-mask area as a percentage of the model's total ocean area. Also report, per model, the area excluded in step 2 as a percentage of the same total ocean area.
   - Note [cmip6-static-grid-fields]: take cell areas from `areacello` (and any cell thickness from `volcello`/`areacello`, never from `lev_bnds` differences, since partial top and bottom cells fail and GFDL `volcello` is monthly); the total each percentage is taken over is each model's own `areacello` ocean total, since model totals differ by about a per cent and no model is rescaled to another.

5. **Rank the models and locate the disagreement.** Order CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR from most to least internally consistent using the area-weighted RMS difference, averaged over the March and September cases, as the primary criterion, with the mean difference, correlation and the >20 m area fraction as supporting evidence; state the criterion explicitly. Then summarise where the disagreement concentrates by tabulating the |difference| > 20 m cells by latitude band and basin, and by describing the patterns in Figure 1. The ranking compares scores computed on each model's own grid and its own `areacello` total, so it is not a cell-by-cell cross-model comparison.

6. **Figure 1 — maps of operator MLD minus mlotst.** A 3-by-2 panel, rows CESM2, GFDL-ESM4, MPI-ESM1-2-LR and columns March and September, on a common longitude-latitude frame (curvilinear model fields interpolated to a regular lon-lat grid for display only, coastlines drawn), one shared diverging colour scale centred on zero with symmetric limits taken from the largest |difference| across all six panels, land left white.

7. **Figure 2 — scatter plots.** A 3-by-2 panel in the same layout, operator MLD on x against `mlotst` on y, one point per common-mask ocean cell, logarithmic axes with identical range and identical ticks in all six panels, a dashed y = x line, and each panel annotated with its area-weighted bias, RMS difference and correlation from step 4. Cells with non-positive MLD cannot be placed on logarithmic axes and are omitted from the panel only, with their count stated; they remain in all statistics of step 4.

Other notes:
- The comparison is an internal-consistency test: both quantities come from the same simulation, so any disagreement is a property of the diagnostic, not of the model's physics.
- Ranking criterion: lowest area-weighted RMS difference, averaged over the two months, is most internally consistent; bias, correlation and the >20 m area fraction are reported alongside it.
- The six percentages in step 4 are each taken over that model's own `areacello` ocean total, not a rescaled common area.
- Resolution asymmetry between CESM2 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR must be stated wherever the disagreement concentrates in narrow features.
