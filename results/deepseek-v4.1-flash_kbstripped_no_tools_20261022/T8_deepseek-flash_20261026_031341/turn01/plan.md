# Plan

1. Open the GLODAPv2.2016b mapped climatology files and read TCO2, TAlk and pH together with the salinity and temperature fields and the lat/lon/depth axes (no time dimension, 33 standard levels). Read the pH field as `pHtsinsitutp`, i.e. total scale at in-situ temperature and pressure, and derive cell areas from its 1° lat/lon axes for later area weighting.
   - Note [cmip6-carbonate-system-definitions]: compare the model `ph` only against the in-situ, total-scale GLODAP field `pHtsinsitutp`, not `pHts25p0`; and remember `dissic` is C_T and `talk` is the total-alkalinity equivalent, so they are the counterparts of GLODAP TCO2 and TAlk.
2. Open the CMIP6 historical files for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR: monthly `dissic`, `talk`, `ph`, `thetao`, `so` for calendar year 2002, monthly `dissic`, `thetao`, `so` for 1971-2000, and each model's Ofx fixed fields `areacello` and `volcello`. Record per model which requested variable is reported missing rather than silently skipping it.
3. For each model, each month and each depth level, compute in-situ density from that month's `thetao`/`so` (`compute_potential_density_anomaly` with `ref_pressure=None`) and convert monthly `dissic` and `talk` from mol m-3 to µmol kg-1.
   - Note [cmip6-bgc-units-per-volume]: use µmol kg-1 = mmol m-3 × 1000 / ρ with the in-situ density field (not a constant density, which errs by about a per cent below the surface); never label mol m-3 × 1000 as µmol kg-1, and report the density assumption when the numbers are given.
4. Build the 2002 annual mean of every model field (and of the derived µmol kg-1 concentrations) by weighting the twelve months with their calendar lengths (`temporal_aggregate`, `freq='mean'`), and separately build the 1971-2000 mean of `dissic`, `thetao`, `so`. Order of operations: density and the per-volume-to-per-mass conversion are formed from each month's T/S first, and only then are the monthly concentrations averaged — so the nonlinear density step is not applied to an annual-mean T/S.
   - Note [monthly-means-are-time-averages]: weight months by calendar length rather than an equal-weight time mean, and state this order of operations explicitly; sub-monthly variance cannot be recovered from monthly means and must not be reported.
5. On each source's own grid, compute the global 0-50 m mean of DIC and of total alkalinity: one area-weighted mean over all level-cells whose depth lies in 0-50 m, weighting every model level-cell by that model's `areacello` and every GLODAP level-cell by its 1° cell area (`compute_basic_stat`, `op='area_weighted_mean'`). Report each model's value, GLODAP's value and model minus GLODAP in µmol kg-1 to the nearest whole unit.
   - Note [omip-weighted-means-and-integrals]: global means of `dissic`/`talk` are sums weighted by cell area (volume where depth is included), restricted NaN-aware to the same valid cells as the data; a 0-50 m mean is area-weighted with `areacello` and a plain `mean()` is wrong.
   - Note [omip-native-grid-vs-regridded]: compute every global/basin/region number on each source's own grid with its own weights (each model's `areacello`, GLODAP's own 1° areas), and regrid to the GLODAP 1° grid only for the maps, the pattern correlation and the RMSE; state in the answer which numbers come from which grid.
6. Repeat step 5 for `ph` against GLODAP `pHtsinsitutp` on the 0-50 m cells, reporting the total-scale means to 3 decimal places and each model's difference from GLODAP. [cmip6-carbonate-system-definitions]: the CESM2 documentation does not state its carbonate constants, so the CESM2 pH comparison rests on an unverified assumption and this must be said.
7. Map comparison of 0-50 m DIC: for each model reduce every horizontal cell to its own mean over that cell's 0-50 m levels, remap those 2-D fields and the correspondingly reduced GLODAP TCO2 field to the GLODAP 1° grid (bilinear interpolation onto the GLODAP lat/lon; GFDL-ESM4 is already on a 1° archive grid), then build one mask over all four sources with `build_comparison_mask(..., ocean_only=False)` and apply `.where(mask)` to every field before any metric. On the common masked cells compute each model's area-weighted mean bias, RMSE and spatial pattern correlation against GLODAP (`compute_difference_metrics`, which weights by area by default, and `analyze_correlation` with `weighting='area'`), reporting bias and RMSE in µmol kg-1 to the nearest unit and correlation to 3 decimals. The regridding method used for these maps (bilinear onto the GLODAP 1° grid) is stated with the results.
   - Note [common-comparison-mask]: put all fields on the GLODAP 1° grid, use a single mask built with `build_comparison_mask` and pass `ocean_only=False` because the reference is GLODAP, not WOA23 (the default would drop 1459 valid cells).
8. Fixed-depth DIC biases: interpolate each model's 2002 annual-mean DIC and GLODAP TCO2 vertically to 0, 100, 500, 1000 and 2000 m with `regrid_vertical` (`[omip-native-grid-vs-regridded]`), place them on the GLODAP 1° grid, and at each depth form the model-minus-GLODAP difference and its area-weighted global mean over exactly those cells where that model and GLODAP are both defined at that depth; report the bias in µmol kg-1 to the nearest unit and the cell count next to each value. `[common-comparison-mask]` the mask does not carry between levels, so the cells are taken from the fields at each depth rather than reused from the 0-50 m map.
9. Period change of DIC: for each model compute the global 0-50 m DIC mean for 1971-2000 the same way as in step 5 (on its own grid, area-weighted) and subtract it from the 2002 value (`[monthly-means-are-time-averages]`), reporting the change in µmol kg-1 to the nearest unit; then divide that change by the same model's 0-50 m DIC difference from GLODAP obtained in step 5 and report the ratio to 2 decimal places, stating what such a ratio implies for reading the GLODAP bias as a model error (how much of the apparent offset is a period/drift mismatch rather than a structural error).
10. Rank the three models using the step 5-8 metrics (0-50 m DIC bias and RMSE, alkalinity bias, pH difference), state which reproduces the observed carbon system best, and check whether the signs of the three models' DIC, alkalinity and pH errors are mutually consistent, flagging any model whose variables were reported missing in step 2.

Other notes:
- [cmip6-carbonate-system-definitions] The CESM2 model paper documents no carbonate-system constants, so wherever its `ph` enters the comparison this is an unverified assumption and must be stated as such.
- [cmip6-grid-labels] GFDL-ESM4 is `grid_label='gr'`: its values are already remapped to a 1° grid with a regridded land-sea mask, so its "native-grid" numbers are 1°-archive numbers and its budgets are only approximately conservative; CESM2 (~100 km) and MPI (~250 km) differ in resolution by about 2.5×, so features narrower than the coarse grid are not comparable in amplitude.
- [omip-native-grid-vs-regridded] State that global/basin means come from each source's own grid and weights, while bias, RMSE and pattern correlation come from the GLODAP 1° common mask, and name the vertical regridding used for the fixed depths.
- [monthly-means-are-time-averages] Sub-monthly variability cannot be reconstructed from monthly means and is not reported.
- [common-comparison-mask] Because the reference is GLODAP, the comparison mask is built with `ocean_only=False`; the default ocean-only mask would discard 1459 valid GLODAP cells.
- [omip-weighted-means-and-integrals] Every reported mean is area-weighted (volume-weighted where depth is integrated) and NaN-aware; unweighted lat/lon averages are not used anywhere.
- The GLODAP climatology (1972-2013, no time dimension) and the models' 2002 annual mean are not synchronous, which the step 9 ratio quantifies.
