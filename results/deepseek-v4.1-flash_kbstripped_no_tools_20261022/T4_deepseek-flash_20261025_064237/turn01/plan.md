# Plan

1. Open the raw `cmip6_historical` files for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR and load, on each model's native ocean grid and native vertical levels: `thetao` and `so` (Omon, monthly), `mlotst` (Omon, monthly) and the Ofx fixed field `areacello`. Convert the level coordinate to metres below the surface if the file stores another unit, and restrict the time axis to 1971-01 to 2000-12 (360 months). Keep every model on its own grid and levels (no regridding here).
   - Note [missing-values-and-land-cells]: read with xarray so the 1e20 fill decodes to NaN; build each field's valid mask from the field itself (`thetao`, `so` and `mlotst` masks can differ within a model and month) and use NaN-aware reductions with weights masked to the same cells.
2. For every monthly profile and every horizontal column of each model, compute the operator MLD with the density criterion that defines `mlotst` (a 0.03 kg/m3 potential-density increase relative to the reference depth): convert the profile's `thetao`/`so` to the TEOS-10 inputs needed for potential density, take the reference density at the model's shallowest level, scan downward for the first level whose density exceeds the reference by 0.03 kg/m3, and linearly interpolate the crossing depth between that level and the one above it. Where a column never reaches the threshold, leave the operator MLD as NaN and exclude that column. Output a (time, lat, lon) operator-MLD field on the model's native grid for all 360 months.
   - Note [cmip6-thetao-potential-temperature]: `thetao` is potential temperature and no `bigthetao` exists for these models, so derive any TEOS-10 density through the potential-temperature route (`temperature_kind()` reads this from metadata).
   - Note [cmip6-so-practical-salinity]: `so` is practical salinity (units "0.001", values of order 35), so convert S_P to S_A before calling any gsw routine that expects Absolute Salinity.
   - Note [cmip6-mlotst-definition]: pass `ref_depth` = the model's shallowest level (mlotst is referenced to the ocean surface, not 10 m) and exclude columns where the 0.03 kg/m3 threshold is never reached; expect the operator MLD to come out shallower than `mlotst`, and treat the three causes (reference level, displaced-parcel density at local pressure vs the σ0 difference, and averaging instantaneous MLDs vs MLD of averaged profiles) as definitional, not model error.
3. For each model and for March and September separately: collect the 30 corresponding monthly fields of operator MLD and of `mlotst` (30 Marches 1971-2000, 30 Septembers 1971-2000), build one shared validity mask on the native grid that is True only where both fields are finite in all 30 instances, apply it with `.where(mask)` to both fields, and then average the 30 operator-MLD values and the 30 `mlotst` values separately to obtain the two climatologies. Record the mask's cell count, its `areacello`-weighted area, and the model's total ocean area (sum of `areacello` over wet cells).
   - Note [monthly-means-are-time-averages]: compute the MLD of each monthly profile first and then average (never average T/S first, which would give a systematically shallower MLD); the March/September climatology is the plain mean of that month's 30 values over the years, with no day weights, and sub-monthly variance is not recoverable.
   - Note [common-comparison-mask]: build one shared mask per model and month with `build_comparison_mask`, apply it to both fields before any metric, and report its cell counts and the ocean-area fraction it represents.
4. For each of the six model-and-month combinations compute, on the shared-mask climatologies kept on each model's native grid: the mean difference (operator MLD minus `mlotst`), the root-mean-square difference, the spatial Pearson correlation between the two climatological fields, and the percentage of the shared-mask area where |difference| exceeds 20 m together with the shared-mask area as a percentage of the model's total ocean area.
   - Note [omip-weighted-means-and-integrals]: weight every map statistic with `areacello`; `compute_difference_metrics` weights by area by default and `analyze_correlation` needs `weighting='area'`; the >20 m exceedance is a share of area with missing cells out of the denominator, obtained from `compute_exceedance_fraction`, which also returns the area behind the percentage.
5. Rank the three models from most to least internally consistent using, as the stated criterion, the mean of the March and September RMS differences (smallest = most consistent), and report the corresponding mean differences and correlations alongside as supporting diagnostics. Identify from the Figure 1 difference maps the basins/latitude bands where the |difference| > 20 m exceedance concentrates for each model.
6. Produce Figure 1 as a 3-by-2 panel (rows CESM2, GFDL-ESM4, MPI-ESM1-2-LR; columns March, September) of global maps of operator MLD minus `mlotst`, each panel on a common regular longitude-latitude frame with a single diverging colour scale centred on zero. Produce Figure 2 as a 3-by-2 panel of scatter plots in the same layout, operator MLD on x against `mlotst` on y, one point per shared-mask ocean cell, logarithmic axes with identical range and ticks in every panel, a dashed y = x reference line, and each panel annotated with its bias, RMS difference and correlation from Step 4.
   - Note [omip-native-grid-vs-regridded]: regrid only for the Figure 1 maps and the cell-by-cell comparison, using method 'conservative' from the model grid to the coarser regular grid and stating the method used; keep the area means, counts and metrics of Step 4 on each model's own grid with its own `areacello`, since a regridded RMSE/correlation would depend on the method.

Other notes:
- [cmip6-mlotst-definition] The operator MLD uses the model's shallowest level as its reference depth rather than the 10 m often quoted for mlotst, so the operator field is expected to be shallower than `mlotst`; the residual is definitional (reference level, displaced-parcel density at local pressure vs σ0, averaging instantaneous MLDs vs MLD of averaged profiles), not a model error.
- [monthly-means-are-time-averages] MLD was computed per monthly profile and then averaged over the 30 Marches/Septembers; the monthly climatology is a plain 30-value mean with no day weights.
- The requested 1971-2000 window lies inside the CMIP6 historical run (1850-2014), so no scenario data are needed.
- A value near 1e20 or 9.97e36 anywhere in the outputs would be an unmasked fill value and must not be interpreted as a physical MLD.
