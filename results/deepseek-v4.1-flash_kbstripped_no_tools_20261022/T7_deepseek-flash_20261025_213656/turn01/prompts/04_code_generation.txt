Plan from the previous step:
1. Open the raw CMIP6 historical files for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR: monthly `no3` and `po4` (Omon) for 1971-01 to 2000-12, together with each model's fixed fields `volcello` and `areacello` (Ofx). A variable a model does not publish is carried as missing and excluded from that model's diagnostics.
   - Note [cmip6-bgc-units-per-volume]: convert `no3`/`po4` from mol m-3 to µmol kg-1 with µmol kg-1 = mol m-3 × 1000 / 1025 using the prescribed constant density, record it in `attrs['density_assumption']`, and note that N:P is scale-invariant so the conversion affects only the µmol kg-1 regression, not the ratio or the volume percentages.
2. Open the WOA23 raw files: `nitrate` and `phosphate` annual objectively analysed fields (the single all-period composite) and the `basinmask_01` basin mask.
3. Build each model's 1971-2000 climatology for `no3` and `po4` separately as a time-weighted mean over the 240 months of each grid cell and level; then form the cell-by-cell N:P field on each month's climatological field and average the monthly ratio fields over the 12 months (diagnostic first, then average), rather than averaging the concentrations and taking the ratio afterwards. WOA23 is already an annual climatology and is used as distributed.
   - Note [monthly-means-are-time-averages]: use the calendar-month-length time-weighted mean (freq='mean' semantics) for the 1971-2000 climatology, not an equal-weight mean over time.
4. Restrict all four sources to the 200-1000 m layer by keeping the model levels and WOA23 levels whose nominal depth lies within 200-1000 m, and stack those levels into one set of cells. Volume weights: each model's own `volcello` restricted to the same valid cells; WOA23 volume from the thickness between its level depth bounds times the 1° cell area. Report the volume source used with the numbers.
   - Note [cmip6-static-grid-fields]: take the model layer thickness from `volcello`/`areacello` (partial bottom cells respected), never from `lev_bnds` differences, and report `attrs['volume_source']` alongside the figures.
5. For each of the four sources compute, on its own native grid, the cell-by-cell N:P = no3/po4 over the 200-1000 m cells and then: (a) the volume-weighted mean of that cell-by-cell ratio (the headline mean N:P); (b) the ratio of the volume-weighted mean `no3` to the volume-weighted mean `po4`, labelled separately as the ratio of means; (c) the percentage of water volume with N:P strictly between 14 and 18, and the percentage with N:P below 12, globally, each reported with the m3 of volume behind the percentage.
   - Note [omip-weighted-means-and-integrals]: volume-weight every 200-1000 m mean with the weights restricted to the same valid cells, take the exceedance fractions from `compute_exceedance_fraction` and report the m3 behind each percentage, and label the mean of the cell-by-cell ratio and the ratio of the volume-weighted means as two different quantities.
6. Attach the WOA23 `basinmask_01` labels with `select_basin` using `include_marginal_seas=True` and `depth_dependent=False` so the surface labels apply through the whole 200-1000 m column; map those labels onto each model's native cells by their longitude/latitude (nearest cell of the 1° mask). Keep the Pacific, Atlantic and Indian Oceans and exclude the Southern and Arctic Oceans.
   - Note [basin-and-region-definitions]: this is a WOA basin labelling, not the OMIP 10-region one, and the Southern and Arctic Oceans are separate basins excluded from the three-basin results.
7. For each source, compute the headline mean N:P separately for the Pacific, Atlantic and Indian Oceans with the same volume weights and [omip-weighted-means-and-integrals] weighting, and the percentage of water volume with N:P below 12 for the Pacific and Indian Oceans (the global below-12 percentage already comes from step 5).
8. For each source, fit the ordinary least-squares regression NO3 = a * PO4 + b over all valid 200-1000 m cells with every cell weighted equally (unweighted, no volume weighting), with concentrations in µmol kg-1 from [cmip6-bgc-units-per-volume]; retain the unrounded slope a, intercept b and R-squared, to be rounded to 2, 2 and 3 decimal places only when reported.
9. Figure 1: build a 4-panel scatter of NO3 (y) against PO4 (x) in µmol kg-1 over 200-1000 m, panels ordered WOA23, CESM2, GFDL-ESM4, MPI-ESM1-2-LR, with identical axis ranges and ticks, each panel showing its own fitted line from step 8 and the Redfield 16:1 line through the origin as a visually distinct style, annotated with a, b and R-squared.
10. Figure 2: compute the 200-1000 m mean N:P minus 16 on each source's own native grid with its own areas/volumes (step 5 headline field), then regrid to a common regular longitude-latitude grid for display only, and draw the 2-by-2 map panel on one shared diverging colour scale centred on zero.
   - Note [omip-native-grid-vs-regridded]: keep every number on each source's own grid with its own areas and volumes and regrid for Figure 2 only, using 'conservative' for the model N:P fields and 'nearest_s2d' for categorical masks, and state the method.

Other notes:
- The WOA23 nutrients are a single all-period composite, not a 1971-2000 normal, while the models are averaged over 1971-2000; state this mismatch alongside the comparison.
- The mean N:P and the exceedance percentages are volume-weighted per [omip-weighted-means-and-integrals], whereas the regression is deliberately equal-weight per cell as the question prescribes; state that the two are differently weighted.
- The regression concentrations use the prescribed constant density 1025 kg/m3 rather than the in-situ density (see [cmip6-bgc-units-per-volume]); the ratio and volume percentages are unaffected by this choice.
- [bgc-model-structure-comparison] Interpret the cross-model N:P spread as partly structural — fixed N:P per plankton type in GFDL-ESM4, prescribed Redfield organic matter in MPI-ESM1-2-LR, variable C:P in CESM2 — and do not read "not detailed in paper" as the feature being absent.
- Intermediate values are carried unrounded; rounding is applied only at the point of reporting (means, slope and intercept to 2 dp, R-squared to 3 dp, percentages to 1 dp).

def cmip6_files(model, var, table):
    """Return CMIP6 files for a model/variable/table, filtering to 1971-2000."""
    d = os.path.join(CMIP6_ROOT, model, var)
    pattern = os.path.join(d, f"{var}_{table}_{model}_historical_*.nc")
    files = sorted(glob.glob(pattern))
    if not files:

    selected = []
    for f in files:
        m = re.search(r"_(\d{6})-(\d{6})\.nc$", os.path.basename(f))
        if m:
            start = int(m.group(1))
            end = int(m.group(2))
            if end < 197101 or start > 200012:
                continue
        selected.append(f)
    if not selected:
        selected = files
    return selected

def monthly_climatology(da, time_dim="time"):
    """
    Calendar-month-length weighted monthly climatology and annual time mean.
    Returns (monthly_clim[month, ...], annual_mean[...]).
    """
    w = da[time_dim].dt.days_in_month.astype(float)
    month = da[time_dim].dt.month

    num = (da * w).groupby(month).sum(dim=time_dim)
    den = w.groupby(month).sum(dim=time_dim)
    clim = (num / den).compute()
    ann = ((da * w).sum(dim=time_dim) / w.sum(dim=time_dim)).compute()
    return clim, ann

def get_woa_field(ds, prefix):
    """Find the objectively analysed field in a WOA file."""
    candidates = []
    for v in ds.data_vars:
        if v in ds.coords:
            continue
        dims = ds[v].dims
        if "depth" in dims and "lat" in dims and "lon" in dims:
            candidates.append(v)
    if not candidates:
        raise ValueError("No WOA 3-D field found")

    preferred = f"{prefix}_an"
    if preferred in candidates:
        return ds[preferred]
    for v in candidates:
        if v.lower().endswith("_an"):
            return ds[v]
    return ds[candidates[0]]


def woa_depth_bounds(ds, depth_name="depth"):
    """Return lower and upper depth bounds for WOA levels."""
    d = ds[depth_name].values
    n = len(d)

    if "depth_bnds" in ds.variables:
        b = ds["depth_bnds"].values
        if b.ndim == 2:
            if b.shape[0] == n and b.shape[1] == 2:
                lower, upper = b[:, 0], b[:, 1]
            elif b.shape[1] == n and b.shape[0] == 2:
                lower, upper = b[0, :], b[1, :]
            else:
                raise ValueError("Unexpected depth_bnds shape")
        elif b.ndim == 1 and len(b) == 2 * n:
            lower, upper = b[:n], b[n:]
        else:
            raise ValueError("Unexpected depth_bnds shape")
    else:
        lower = np.empty(n)
        upper = np.empty(n)
        lower[0] = 0.0
        for i in range(1, n):
            lower[i] = 0.5 * (d[i - 1] + d[i])
        for i in range(n - 1):
            upper[i] = 0.5 * (d[i] + d[i + 1])
        upper[-1] = d[-1] + 0.5 * (d[-1] - d[-2])
        if n == 1:
            upper[0] = d[0]

    return np.minimum(lower, upper), np.maximum(lower, upper)


def woa_area(lat, lon):
    """Approximate 1-degree cell area on a sphere."""
    R = 6371000.0
    dlon = np.deg2rad(1.0)
    area_lat = R**2 * dlon * (
        np.sin(np.deg2rad(lat + 0.5)) - np.sin(np.deg2rad(lat - 0.5))
    )
    return np.repeat(area_lat[:, None], len(lon), axis=1)

def read_basinmask_file(path, lat_woa, lon_woa):
    """
    Read the WOA sparse basin mask.
    The file has one row per ocean grid point, with latitude, longitude and the
    basin labels for the standard levels.  Because depth_dependent=False we only
    need the surface label (third column).
    """
    lats, lons, vals = [], [], []
    with open(path, "r") as f:
        for line in f:
            s = line.strip()
            if not s or s.startswith("#") or s.startswith("!"):
                continue
            parts = s.split()
            if len(parts) < 3:
                continue
            try:
                lat = float(parts[0])
                lon = float(parts[1])
                val = float(parts[2])
            except ValueError:
                continue
            lats.append(lat)
            lons.append(lon)
            vals.append(val)

    lats = np.asarray(lats)
    lons = np.asarray(lons)
    vals = np.asarray(vals)

    basin = np.full((len(lat_woa), len(lon_woa)), np.nan, dtype=float)
    lat_idx = np.abs(lat_woa[:, None] - lats[None, :]).argmin(axis=0)
    lon_idx = np.abs(lon_woa[:, None] - lons[None, :]).argmin(axis=0)
    basin[lat_idx, lon_idx] = vals
    return basin


def map_basin_codes(basin):
    """
    Map WOA basin numbers to parent basins:
      1 Atlantic, 2 Pacific, 3 Indian, 4 Arctic, 5 Southern.
    Marginal-sea codes >5 are assigned to the nearest open-ocean parent
    (include_marginal_seas=True).
    """
    parent = np.full_like(basin, np.nan, dtype=float)

    parent[np.isclose(basin, 1.0)] = 1.0
    parent[np.isclose(basin, 2.0)] = 2.0
    parent[np.isclose(basin, 3.0)] = 3.0
    parent[np.isclose(basin, 4.0)] = 4.0
    parent[np.isclose(basin, 5.0)] = 5.0

    finite = np.isfinite(basin)
    rounded = np.zeros_like(basin, dtype=int)
    rounded[finite] = np.round(basin[finite]).astype(int)
    unknown = finite & (basin > 0.0) & ~np.isin(rounded, [1, 2, 3, 4, 5])

    if np.any(unknown):
        distances = []
        for code in (1.0, 2.0, 3.0):
            distances.append(distance_transform_edt(~(parent == code)))
        distances = np.stack(distances, axis=0)
        nearest = np.argmin(distances, axis=0).astype(float) + 1.0
        parent[unknown] = nearest[unknown]

    return parent

def metrics_from_arrays(np_arr, no3_arr, po4_arr, vol_arr, basin_arr=None):
    """Compute volume-weighted metrics and unweighted OLS regression."""
    total_vol = float(np.sum(vol_arr))

    mean_np = float(np.sum(np_arr * vol_arr) / total_vol)
    ratio_means = float(np.sum(no3_arr * vol_arr) / np.sum(po4_arr * vol_arr))

    mask_14_18 = (np_arr > 14.0) & (np_arr < 18.0)
    vol_14_18 = float(np.sum(vol_arr[mask_14_18]))
    pct_14_18 = 100.0 * vol_14_18 / total_vol

    mask_below12 = np_arr < 12.0
    vol_below12 = float(np.sum(vol_arr[mask_below12]))
    pct_below12 = 100.0 * vol_below12 / total_vol

    slope, intercept = np.polyfit(po4_arr, no3_arr, 1)
    pred = slope * po4_arr + intercept
    ss_res = np.sum((no3_arr - pred) ** 2)
    ss_tot = np.sum((no3_arr - np.mean(no3_arr)) ** 2)
    r2 = float(1.0 - ss_res / ss_tot)

    out = {
        "mean_NP": mean_np,
        "ratio_of_means": ratio_means,
        "slope_a": float(slope),
        "intercept_b": float(intercept),
        "r2": r2,
        "pct_14_18": float(pct_14_18),
        "vol_14_18_m3": vol_14_18,
        "pct_below12": float(pct_below12),
        "vol_below12_m3": vol_below12,
        "total_volume_m3": total_vol,
    }

    if basin_arr is not None:
        for code, name in [(1.0, "Pacific"), (2.0, "Atlantic"), (3.0, "Indian")]:
            m = basin_arr == code
            if np.any(m):
                out[f"mean_NP_{name}"] = float(
                    np.sum(np_arr[m] * vol_arr[m]) / np.sum(vol_arr[m])
                )
                if name in ("Pacific", "Indian"):
                    out[f"pct_below12_{name}"] = float(
                        100.0
                        * np.sum(vol_arr[m & (np_arr < 12.0)])
                        / np.sum(vol_arr[m])
                    )
                else:
                    out[f"pct_below12_{name}"] = np.nan
            else:
                out[f"mean_NP_{name}"] = np.nan
                out[f"pct_below12_{name}"] = np.nan

    return out


def vertical_weighted_mean(field, vol, valid):
    """Volume-weighted vertical mean of a (lev, y, x) field."""
    num = np.nansum(np.where(valid, field * vol, np.nan), axis=0)
    den = np.nansum(np.where(valid, vol, np.nan), axis=0)
    out = np.full(num.shape, np.nan, dtype=float)
    mask = den > 0.0
    out[mask] = num[mask] / den[mask]
    return out


def conservative_regrid(values2d, lat2d, lon2d, area2d, common_lat, common_lon):
    """
    Area-weighted conservative binning to a regular 1-degree grid.
    This is used for the display map only.
    """
    nlat = len(common_lat)
    nlon = len(common_lon)

    lon360 = lon2d % 360.0
    j = np.floor(lon360).astype(int) % nlon
    i = np.floor(lat2d + 90.0).astype(int)
    i = np.clip(i, 0, nlat - 1)

    valid = (
        np.isfinite(values2d)
        & np.isfinite(area2d)
        & (area2d > 0.0)
    )

    sum_area = np.zeros((nlat, nlon), dtype=float)
    sum_val = np.zeros((nlat, nlon), dtype=float)

    np.add.at(sum_area, (i[valid], j[valid]), area2d[valid])
    np.add.at(sum_val, (i[valid], j[valid]), values2d[valid] * area2d[valid])

    out = np.full((nlat, nlon), np.nan, dtype=float)
    mask = sum_area > 0.0
    out[mask] = sum_val[mask] / sum_area[mask]
    return out

def process_woa():
    ds_n = open_woa_var("nitrate")
    ds_p = open_woa_var("phosphate")

    da_n = get_woa_field(ds_n, "n")
    da_p = get_woa_field(ds_p, "p")

    if "time" in da_n.dims:
        da_n = da_n.isel(time=0)
    if "time" in da_p.dims:
        da_p = da_p.isel(time=0)

    depth = ds_n["depth"].values
    lev_idx = np.where((depth >= 200.0) & (depth <= 1000.0))[0]
    if lev_idx.size == 0:
        raise ValueError("No WOA levels in 200-1000 m")

    da_n = da_n.isel(depth=lev_idx)
    da_p = da_p.isel(depth=lev_idx)

    lat = ds_n["lat"].values
    lon = ds_n["lon"].values

    lower, upper = woa_depth_bounds(ds_n, "depth")
    thick = np.abs(upper[lev_idx] - lower[lev_idx])
    area2d = woa_area(lat, lon)
    vol_woa = thick[:, None, None] * area2d[None, :, :]

    no3_all = da_n.values
    po4_all = da_p.values
    np_all = np.where(po4_all > 0.0, no3_all / po4_all, np.nan)

    valid = (
        np.isfinite(np_all)
        & np.isfinite(no3_all)
        & np.isfinite(po4_all)
        & (vol_woa > 0.0)
    )

    np_vals = np_all[valid]
    no3_vals = no3_all[valid]
    po4_vals = po4_all[valid]
    vol_vals = vol_woa[valid]

    basin_b = np.broadcast_to(basin_parent_2d[None, :, :], np_all.shape)
    basin_vals = basin_b[valid]

    metrics = metrics_from_arrays(
        np_vals, no3_vals, po4_vals, vol_vals, basin_vals
    )
    metrics["po4_vals"] = po4_vals
    metrics["no3_vals"] = no3_vals
    metrics["volume_source"] = "thickness from WOA depth bounds × 1° cell area"
    metrics["density_assumption"] = "not applicable (WOA mass basis)"
    metrics["map_np"] = vertical_weighted_mean(np_all, vol_woa, valid)

    lon2d, lat2d = np.meshgrid(lon, lat)
    metrics["map_lat2d"] = lat2d
    metrics["map_lon2d"] = lon2d
    metrics["map_area2d"] = area2d
    metrics["basin2d"] = basin_parent_2d
    return metrics

def process_model(model):
    info = MODELS[model]
    depth_name = info["depth_coord"]
    y_dim = info["y_dim"]
    x_dim = info["x_dim"]

    ds_no3 = open_cmip6_var(model, "no3", "Omon")
    da_no3 = ds_no3["no3"]

    lev = ds_no3[depth_name].values
    depth_m = lev * info["depth_scale"]
    lev_idx = np.where((depth_m >= 200.0) & (depth_m <= 1000.0))[0]
    if lev_idx.size == 0:
        raise ValueError(f"No {model} levels in 200-1000 m")

    da_no3 = da_no3.isel({depth_name: lev_idx})

    ds_po4 = open_cmip6_var(model, "po4", "Omon")
    da_po4 = ds_po4["po4"].isel({depth_name: lev_idx})

    no3_monthly, no3_ann = monthly_climatology(da_no3)
    po4_monthly, po4_ann = monthly_climatology(da_po4)

    no3_ann_umol = (no3_ann * CMIP6_TO_UMOL_KG).assign_attrs(
        units="umol kg-1", density_assumption="1025 kg m-3"
    )
    po4_ann_umol = (po4_ann * CMIP6_TO_UMOL_KG).assign_attrs(
        units="umol kg-1", density_assumption="1025 kg m-3"
    )

    np_monthly = xr.where(
        po4_monthly > 0.0,
        no3_monthly / po4_monthly,
        np.nan,
    )
    np_field = np_monthly.mean(dim="month").assign_attrs(units="1")

    vol = open_cmip6_fixed(model, "volcello", info, lev_idx=lev_idx)
    vol = vol.transpose(*np_field.dims)

    area = open_cmip6_fixed(model, "areacello", info, lev_idx=None)
    area = area.transpose(y_dim, x_dim)
    area2d = area.values

    lat2d, lon2d = get_latlon_2d(ds_no3, info)

    np_vals_all = np_field.values
    no3_vals_all = no3_ann_umol.values
    po4_vals_all = po4_ann_umol.values
    vol_vals_all = vol.values

    valid = (
        np.isfinite(np_vals_all)
        & np.isfinite(no3_vals_all)
        & np.isfinite(po4_vals_all)
        & (vol_vals_all > 0.0)
    )

    np_vals = np_vals_all[valid]
    no3_vals = no3_vals_all[valid]
    po4_vals = po4_vals_all[valid]
    vol_vals = vol_vals_all[valid]

    # Map WOA surface basin labels onto the model grid by nearest 1-degree cell.
    lon_mod = ((lon2d + 180.0) % 360.0) - 180.0
    lat_idx = np.floor(lat2d + 90.0).astype(int)
    lon_idx = np.floor(lon_mod + 180.0).astype(int) % len(lon_woa)
    lat_idx = np.clip(lat_idx, 0, len(lat_woa) - 1)
    basin2d = basin_parent_filled[lat_idx, lon_idx]

    basin_b = np.broadcast_to(basin2d[None, :, :], np_vals_all.shape)
    basin_vals = basin_b[valid]

    metrics = metrics_from_arrays(
        np_vals, no3_vals, po4_vals, vol_vals, basin_vals
    )
    metrics["po4_vals"] = po4_vals
    metrics["no3_vals"] = no3_vals
    metrics["volume_source"] = (
        "volcello (Omon 1971-2000 time mean)"
        if model == "GFDL-ESM4"
        else "volcello (Ofx)"
    )
    metrics["density_assumption"] = "1025 kg m-3"
    metrics["map_np"] = vertical_weighted_mean(
        np_vals_all, vol_vals_all, valid
    )
    metrics["map_lat2d"] = lat2d
    metrics["map_lon2d"] = lon2d
    metrics["map_area2d"] = area2d
    metrics["basin2d"] = basin2d
    return metrics
