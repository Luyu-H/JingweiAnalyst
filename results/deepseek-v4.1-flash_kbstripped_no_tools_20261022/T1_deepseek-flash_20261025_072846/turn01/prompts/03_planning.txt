[anomaly-baselines-and-climate-indices] (must)
- Each model index must be its anomaly from its **own** climatology (`compute_enso_nino_index` on model output; observed climatology on a model puts mean-state bias into the index); each calendar month is matched against that month's climatology, removing the seasonal cycle; state the climatology and baseline used (here 1950–2014 monthly, as the question prescribes).
- The exceedance counts asked for are months, not events: a single step beyond ±1.0 °C is not an event, so `detect_threshold_events`/`min_duration` do not apply; count months with |anomaly| > 1.0 °C as specified.

[monthly-means-are-time-averages] (must)
- Day weights do not enter a monthly climatology: each calendar month is the plain mean of that month's values over the 1950–2014 baseline, as `compute_anomaly_vs_own_climatology` and the index tools build it; day-weighted averaging (`temporal_aggregate`) is for averaging over a period, not for a climatology.
- Only interannual/inter-month variance is recoverable from monthly means; the 12 per-month standard deviations are variability across years, not sub-monthly.

[cmip6-tos-bulk-top-cell] (should)
- `tos` is the bulk temperature of the top model cell (including under ice), so take the top level (`depth_level=None`) for the Niño 3.4 index; do not apply an observed (WOA23) climatology to model `tos`.
- An index from a model is its anomaly from its own climatology; subtracting an observed climatology puts the model's mean-state bias into the index and it is then not an ENSO index.

[omip-weighted-means-and-integrals] (should)
- The 5S–5N, 170W–120W average must be area-weighted with each source's own cell areas; an unweighted `mean()` over (y, x) on a curvilinear grid, and over latitude on a regular grid, is wrong, and weights must be NaN-restricted to the same valid cells.
- An unweighted standard deviation of a map counts a polar cell as heavily as a tropical one; the question defines the population standard deviation (ddof=0) of the index series, over all 780 months, which is what is reported.

[cmip6-experiments-historical-ssp245] (should)
- Both experiments are concentration-driven and coupled: a given model year is not expected to match the observed year, so compare statistics (standard deviations, seasonal cycle, exceedance rates), never event timing.
- One member per model cannot separate forced change from internal variability; the amplitude bias/ratio must carry that caveat.

[cmip6-grid-labels] (should)
- GFDL-ESM4 `tos` is `gr` (regridded to 1x1 degree, conserve_order1), CESM2 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR are `gn`; resolution differs ~2.5x (100 km vs 250 km), so features narrower than the coarse grid are not comparable in amplitude and amplitude differences between models partly reflect resolution.

[cmip6-static-grid-fields] (should)
- On the curvilinear native grids the model's own `areacello` is the only correct area weight (zero/missing over land); `load_areas` records its origin in `attrs['area_source']`, worth reporting.
- Total ocean area differs between models by about a per cent because the coastline is resolved differently; state the area each average was taken over rather than rescaling one model to match another.
