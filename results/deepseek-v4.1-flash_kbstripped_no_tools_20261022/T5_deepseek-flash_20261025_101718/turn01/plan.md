# Plan

1. Using WOA23, open `salinity`, `landsea_01` and `basinmask_01`. Select the 1971-2000 climate normal and the annual field; if salinity is stored monthly, average the twelve months after selecting 1971-2000. On the WOA 1° grid, compute cell areas from longitude/latitude and full-level volumes for the 0, 5 and 10 m levels from their standard level bounds, without clipping to the 0-10 m interval. Compute WOA SSS = sum(salinity_k × V_k) / sum(V_k) over levels whose depth lies in 0-10 m. Keep landsea values >1 as ocean and retain the basin labels.

2. Using CMIP6 historical and ssp245, open monthly `so` for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR; slice historical 1950-01 to 2014-12 and ssp245 2071-01 to 2100-12. Open each model’s own `areacello` and `volcello` fixed fields once and use them for both experiments. Verify and record the member labels: CESM2 historical r1 and ssp245 r10; GFDL-ESM4 r1/r1; MPI-ESM1-2-LR r1/r1. Keep the two experiments as separate time series.
   - Note [historical-ssp245-member-continuity]: CESM2 historical r1 and ssp245 r10 are different realisations, so its delta SSS contains forced change plus the internal-variability difference between members and its ssp245/historical slope ratio compares different members; GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR are r1/r1 and continuous. Do not fit a trend across the 2015 join; fit historical and ssp245 slopes separately.

3. For each model and experiment, compute monthly SSS on the native model grid as SSS = sum(so_k × volcello_k) / sum(volcello_k) over model levels whose depths lie within 0-10 m (one or two levels), using the full `volcello` cell volume as each level’s weight and without clipping to the 0-10 m overlap. Apply exactly the same 0-10 m level selection and full-volume weighting definition used for WOA23.
   - Note [cmip6-so-practical-salinity]: model `so` and WOA23 salinity are both practical salinity; difference them directly and do not multiply `so` by 0.001, because the `0.001` units string already denotes psu-scale numbers.

4. Regrid CESM2 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR native `gn` SSS and areacello to the WOA23 1° longitude-latitude target grid; treat GFDL-ESM4 as already `gr` and already regridded to 1° with its regridded land-sea mask. Preserve valid-cell information. Compute each model’s 1971-2000 SSS climatology from historical monthly fields by first forming annual cell means over 1971-2000 and then the period mean; also compute historical 1995-2014 and ssp245 2071-2100 period means where needed later.
   - Note [cmip6-grid-labels]: CESM2 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR are native `gn` and must be regridded to the WOA23 1° grid for the metrics, while GFDL-ESM4 is `gr` and already regridded. Resolution differs (~100 km versus ~250 km), so features narrower than the coarse grid are not comparable in amplitude.

5. Build one single common comparison mask on the WOA23 1° grid: WOA `landsea_01` > 1, valid WOA SSS, and valid regridded 1971-2000 SSS for all three models. Apply `.where(mask)` to every model and WOA field before bias, RMSE, pattern correlation and the comparison global means. Record and report the mask cell count.
   - Note [common-comparison-mask]: build one common mask with `ocean_only=True`, valid here because WOA23 is in the comparison, apply `.where(mask)` to every field before bias, RMSE and pattern correlation, and report the mask’s cell counts alongside the metrics. Do not score or rank models on separate pairwise cells.

6. On the common mask, compute the 1971-2000 global mean SSS for each model and for WOA23 using area weights: regridded model `areacello` for the models and WOA analytic cell areas for WOA, with weights restricted to the same masked cells. Compute each model minus WOA23 difference and the SSS bias map. Compute area-weighted RMSE and area-weighted spatial Pearson correlation against WOA23 on the same cells. Report global means and differences to 2 decimal places, RMSE to 2 decimal places, correlation to 3 decimal places, and include the common-mask cell count.
   - Note [omip-weighted-means-and-integrals]: use `compute_basic_stat` with `op='area_weighted_mean'` and `areacello` for global and regional SSS means, with weights restricted to the same valid masked cells; `compute_difference_metrics` weights by area by default; `analyze_correlation` needs `weighting='area'` for the map correlation.
   - Note [serial-correlation-and-significance]: for the spatial pattern correlation read the coefficient, not its p-value; for trend fits use the linear method with effective sample size along time by default, quote the corrected 95% CI, R² and whether the interval excludes zero, and draw the slope everywhere while stippling significant cells.

7. For each model, form annual cell-mean SSS over 1950-2014 and 2071-2100, then annual global mean SSS using area weights [omip-weighted-means-and-integrals]. Fit separate linear trends for historical 1950-2014 and ssp245 2071-2100 using the effective-sample-size correction [serial-correlation-and-significance]; report each slope in psu per decade to 3 significant figures with its corrected 95% CI, R² and whether the interval excludes zero. Compute the ssp245/historical slope ratio to 1 decimal place and state its meaningfulness, noting the separate periods and, for CESM2, the member mismatch [historical-ssp245-member-continuity]. Also compute cell-by-cell annual SSS trends over 1950-2014 with p-values for Figure 2 using the same [serial-correlation-and-significance] method.

8. For each model, compute cell-wise delta SSS = ssp245 2071-2100 mean minus historical 1995-2014 mean, keeping only cells valid in both periods. Compute area-weighted global and basin means using the model’s `areacello` [omip-weighted-means-and-integrals] restricted to those valid cells. For the Atlantic, Indian and Pacific basins use the WOA23 basin mask with marginal seas folded into the parent ocean and exclude the Southern and Arctic Oceans. Report delta SSS to 2 decimal places and state the CESM2 member caveat [historical-ssp245-member-continuity].
   - Note [basin-and-region-definitions]: basin means use the WOA23 basin mask via `select_basin` with `include_marginal_seas=True` and `depth_dependent=False` (surface labels) for the 0-10 m SSS; state that WOA labelling, not the OMIP 10-region mask, was used. The Ofx `basin` variable is not in the local archive and each mask choice changes basin means.

9. Produce the figures: Figure 1 with three global bias maps in one row, model 1971-2000 SSS minus WOA23 on the common comparison cells, one shared diverging scale centred on zero, colour bar labelled psu. Figure 2 with three maps of cell-by-cell 1950-2014 annual SSS trend in psu per decade on a common longitude-latitude frame, one shared diverging scale centred on zero, and p < 0.05 cells stippled; for CESM2 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR put the native/curvilinear trend and significance fields on a regular longitude-latitude grid for display only [cmip6-grid-labels]. Figure 3 with a single panel of annual global mean SSS for all three models, historical 1950-2014 and ssp245 2071-2100 drawn as separate segments with the 2015-2070 gap left empty, each segment overlaid with its own fitted line, y axis labelled psu, and consistent model colours across all three figures.

Other notes:
- The ssp245/historical slope ratio is a comparison of two separate-period slopes, not a continuous 1950-2100 trend, a projection or a direct acceleration estimate; the 2015-2070 interval is not bridged.
- The mean-state comparison uses the WOA23 1971-2000 normal and model historical 1971-2000 climatology, while delta SSS uses historical 1995-2014 and ssp245 2071-2100 exactly as requested.
- All global and basin means are area-weighted with the models’ own `areacello` and WOA analytic cell areas for WOA; no plain latitude-longitude averages are used.
- Metrics are conditional on the common-mask ocean cells; the mask cell counts must accompany the reported global means, RMSE and correlation.
