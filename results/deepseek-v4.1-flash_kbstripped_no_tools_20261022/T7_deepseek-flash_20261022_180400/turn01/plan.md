# Plan

1. Open the WOA23 annual objectively analysed nutrient climatology and read `nitrate` and `phosphate` on the native 1° grid. Also open the WOA23 `basinmask_01` basin mask. Restrict the nutrient fields to the standard depth levels lying within 200–1000 m without vertical interpolation, and form WOA23 layer thicknesses from the depth-level bounds and cell areas from the 1° spherical grid for later volume weighting. Keep the native WOA23 nutrient units (µmol kg-1; convert only if the file metadata show otherwise) and compute the cell-by-cell N:P ratio where both nutrients are valid.

2. Open the CMIP6 historical `no3` and `po4` fields for CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR for monthly 1971-01 to 2000-12, together with each model’s `volcello` and `areacello` (GFDL-ESM4 `volcello` is monthly). Read each model on its own native grid; for GFDL-ESM4 this is the archive `gr` grid.
   - Note [cmip6-bgc-units-per-volume]: `no3`/`po4` are archived in mol m-3; convert to µmol kg-1 as µmol kg-1 = mmol m-3 × 1000 / ρ with ρ = 1025 kg m-3, and record the density assumption (`attrs['density_assumption']`). Compute N:P from the native per-volume fields because the ratio and its thresholds are invariant under scaling.

3. Average the monthly CMIP6 `no3`, `po4` and (for GFDL-ESM4) monthly `volcello` over 1971–2000 using day weights in each model’s calendar, producing 1971–2000 monthly climatological means for each model.
   - Note [monthly-means-are-time-averages]: use day-weighted time averaging over the model calendar, not equal weighting of every month; state that the order used is “average monthly fields first, then compute the N:P diagnostic”, the like-for-like choice against the WOA23 climatology.

4. For each CMIP6 model, construct the 200–1000 m layer thickness as `volcello` / `areacello` (`cell_thickness_like`) and use the model’s own `volcello` for volume weights, restricted to the same valid nutrient cells; do not interpolate to additional vertical levels.
   - Note [cmip6-static-grid-fields]: take layer thickness for 200–1000 m from `volcello`/`areacello`, not from `lev_bnds` differences, because those fail at partial bottom cells; GFDL-ESM4’s monthly `volcello` must be used as monthly data. Record the volume/area origin (`attrs['volume_source']`).

5. For each of the four sources (WOA23 and the three CMIP6 models), compute the 200–1000 m cell-by-cell N:P field on that source’s own grid, from nitrate and phosphate where both are valid, using the native per-volume concentrations for the ratio. For the regression only, convert each model cell’s nitrate and phosphate to µmol kg-1 with ρ = 1025 kg/m3; WOA23 is already treated in its native µmol kg-1 units. Do not regrid before computing any statistic.
   - Note [omip-native-grid-vs-regridded]: compute every number (means, ratios, regressions, volume percentages, basin statistics) on each source’s own grid with its own weights; any regridding is only for the Figure 2 display maps and must use the ‘conservative’ method with the method stated.

6. For each source, compute over the 200–1000 m layer: the headline mean N:P as the volume-weighted mean of the cell-by-cell N:P; the ratio of the volume-weighted mean nitrate to the volume-weighted mean phosphate; and the ordinary least-squares regression NO3 = a·PO4 + b over all valid cells with every cell weighted equally (not volume-weighted), reporting slope a, intercept b in µmol kg-1, and R². Also compute the percentage of water volume with N:P strictly between 14 and 18, and the percentage with N:P below 12, globally.
   - Note [omip-weighted-means-and-integrals]: use volume-weighted means over the layer restricted to the same valid cells; keep the mean of the cell-by-cell N:P and the ratio of the two volume-weighted means separate and clearly labelled. Volume percentages are volume shares, computed with missing cells excluded from the denominator, with the corresponding water volume (m3) available behind the percentage.

7. Map the WOA23 `basinmask_01` labels to each source’s grid (nearest WOA23 mask cell for curvilinear model grids) and compute, for each source, the headline volume-weighted mean N:P in the Pacific, Atlantic and Indian Oceans, plus the percentage of water volume with N:P below 12 in the Pacific and Indian Oceans. Use the surface basin label through the whole 200–1000 m column.
   - Note [basin-and-region-definitions]: use the WOA23 basin mask (`select_basin`/`load_basin_mask` equivalent) with marginal seas folded into their parent ocean (`include_marginal_seas=True`), Southern and Arctic treated as separate basins and excluded from the Pacific/Atlantic/Indian means, and the surface label applied through the column (`depth_dependent=False`).

8. Draw Figure 1 as a 4-panel scatter of nitrate (y) against phosphate (x) in µmol kg-1 over 200–1000 m, one panel per source in the order WOA23, CESM2, GFDL-ESM4, MPI-ESM1-2-LR, using all valid cells equally weighted. Use identical axis ranges and ticks in every panel; show each panel’s own fitted regression line and the Redfield 16:1 line through the origin, made visually distinguishable, and annotate a, b and R² from step 6.

9. Draw Figure 2 as a 2-by-2 panel of global maps of the 200–1000 m mean N:P minus 16 for the four sources. Compute each column’s layer mean N:P on the source’s own grid and subtract 16 first; then, for display only, put the curvilinear model fields on a common regular longitude–latitude grid using conservative regridding. Use one shared diverging colour scale centred on zero and make each panel a true geographic map, following the display-only treatment required by [omip-native-grid-vs-regridded].
