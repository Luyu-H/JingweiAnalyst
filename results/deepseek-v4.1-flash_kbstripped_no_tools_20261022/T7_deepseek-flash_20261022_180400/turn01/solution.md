**Method summary (from the executed code).** The reference is the WOA23 annual objectively analysed nitrate and phosphate climatology on its native 1° grid, together with the WOA23 `basinmask_01` basin mask. WOA23 standard depth levels within 200–1000 m were selected without vertical interpolation (23 levels, 200–1000 m), with layer thicknesses from the depth-level bounds and cell areas from the 1° spherical grid. CMIP6 historical `no3` and `po4` from CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR were read on each model’s native/archive grid for monthly 1971–2000, together with `volcello` and `areacello`; GFDL-ESM4 `volcello` is monthly. Monthly fields were first averaged over 1971–2000 using day weights in each model calendar, then the N:P diagnostic was formed. CMIP6 concentrations were converted from mol m⁻³ to µmol kg⁻¹ using the prescribed constant ρ = 1025 kg m⁻³ for the regression and reporting; N:P ratios and thresholds were computed from the native per-volume fields. No vertical interpolation or regridding was used for any statistic. Volume weights came from WOA23 spherical cell area × depth-level bounds, CESM2 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR `volcello` fixed fields, and GFDL-ESM4 monthly `volcello` day-weighted over 1971–2000; all were restricted to valid nutrient cells. Basin means used the WOA23 basin mask with marginal seas folded into their parent ocean, the surface label applied through the whole 200–1000 m column, and Southern and Arctic basins treated separately and excluded from Pacific/Atlantic/Indian means. Figure 2 used display-only area-weighted binning of the curvilinear CESM2/MPI fields to a common 1° longitude–latitude grid; WOA23 and GFDL-ESM4 were already regular.

**Key numerical results.** The headline mean N:P is the volume-weighted mean of the cell-by-cell N:P ratio; the second N:P is the ratio of the volume-weighted mean nitrate to the volume-weighted mean phosphate. The OLS regression NO3 = a·PO4 + b used all valid cells equally weighted, not volume-weighted.

**Interpretation.** All four sources have mean intermediate-water N:P below Redfield 16:1. WOA23 is only modestly below Redfield globally (14.15), with the expected basin structure: lowest N:P in the Pacific and Indian and higher N:P in the Atlantic. The low-N:P imprint in WOA23 is relatively limited globally (6.2% of volume with N:P <12), concentrated in the Pacific (8.1%) and Indian (10.4%). Among the models, GFDL-ESM4 is closest to WOA23 in global mean N:P (14.13 vs 14.15) and global low-N:P volume (7.3% vs 6.2%), and its Pacific and Atlantic means are reasonably close. However, GFDL-ESM4 almost entirely misses the observed Indian Ocean low-N:P volume (0.5% vs 10.4%) and has a higher Indian mean N:P (14.72 vs 13.72). CESM2 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR are too low in mean N:P (11.83 and 12.28) and strongly over-produce low-N:P water, especially CESM2. CESM2 has 36.7% of global volume below N:P 12, with 59.4% in the Pacific and 35.8% in the Indian Ocean; MPI-ESM1-2-LR has 22.5% globally, 39.6% in the Pacific and 15.0% in the Indian. Thus, all models sit below Redfield, but none reproduces simultaneously the observed low-N:P volume and its basin distribution: GFDL-ESM4 captures the global low-N:P fraction but not the Indian signal, while CESM2 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR exaggerate the low-N:P volume and Pacific/Indian imprint. The OLS slopes also differ from WOA23: WOA23 has a = 13.04 and high R² = 0.908, whereas the models have lower slopes and intercepts offset from zero, with CESM2 especially scattered (a = 4.89, b = 10.84, R² = 0.238). GFDL-ESM4 has the highest model R² (0.817) and slope closest to observed, but still with a positive intercept. Overall, the models qualitatively produce below-Redfield intermediate-water stoichiometry, but they do not faithfully reproduce the magnitude and basin pattern of the observed low-N:P denitrification imprint.

**Visualizations.** Figure 1 is a four-panel scatter of nitrate versus phosphate (µmol kg⁻¹) over 200–1000 m, ordered WOA23, CESM2, GFDL-ESM4, MPI-ESM1-2-LR, with identical axis limits and ticks. Each panel shows the individual OLS fit and the Redfield 16:1 line through the origin, annotated with the fitted a, b and R². Figure 2 is a 2×2 global map of the 200–1000 m mean N:P minus 16 for the same four sources, using a common diverging colour scale centred on zero. Curvilinear model fields were placed on a regular longitude–latitude grid for display only; no statistic was computed from the regridded display fields.

**Caveats.** GFDL-ESM4 is the archive `gr` grid (1°), already remapped with a regridded land–sea mask, so its “native” numbers are on that archive grid and only approximately conservative in budget terms. CESM2 (~100 km) versus MPI-ESM1-2-LR (~250 km) resolution limits direct comparison of narrow features. The prescribed density is 1025 kg m⁻³ for the CMIP6 µmol kg⁻¹ conversion and regression; WOA23 was used in its native µmol kg⁻¹ units.
