- If a shared mask is built, `ocean_only=True` is right only when WOA23 is part of the comparison; here the reference is ERSSTv5, so use `ocean_only=False`.

[monthly-means-are-time-averages] (must)
- Day weights do not enter a monthly climatology: each calendar month's climatology is the plain mean of that month's values over the 1950-2014 years, as the index tools build it.

[eof-modes-and-pdo-index] (should)
- Form anomalies by removing the climatological annual cycle AND the global-mean SST anomaly at each grid point, and weight cells by sqrt(cos(latitude)) before the EOF; the sign convention is positive index = negative SST anomalies in the central/western North Pacific, applied to both pattern and index.
- Amplitude = area-weighted RMS of the regression pattern (anomalies regressed on the standardised index), in °C per standard deviation — not the dimensionless EOF; compare patterns across sources with `analyze_correlation(weighting='area')`, since the domain reaches high latitudes.

[cmip6-tos-bulk-top-cell] (should)
