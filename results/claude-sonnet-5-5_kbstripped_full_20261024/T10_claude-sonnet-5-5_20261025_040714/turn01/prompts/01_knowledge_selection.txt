===== USER =====
User question: Assess the global oxygen minimum zones (OMZ) simulated by CMIP6 models against the WOA23 observed climatology, and quantify how they change under the ssp245 scenario.

Data to use:
- CMIP6 historical `o2`, `thetao` and `so` from GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, monthly, 1971-01 to 2000-12. CESM2 publishes no `o2` for the historical experiment.
- CMIP6 ssp245 `o2`, `thetao` and `so` from CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, monthly, 2071-01 to 2100-12.
- Each model's own volcello, areacello and deptho.
- WOA23 oxygen, percent oxygen saturation and apparent oxygen utilization, on the 1971-2000 climate normal.
- The WOA23 land-sea mask (landsea_01) and basin mask (basinmask_01).

Oxygen concentrations and OMZ thresholds are in micromoles per kilogram.

Expected numerical results, for the six source-and-experiment combinations (WOA23; GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR historical 1971-2000; CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR ssp245 2071-2100) where applicable:
- The total volume of water with oxygen strictly below 20 micromoles per kilogram, and separately strictly below 60, in units of 1e15 cubic metres to 3 significant figures.
- At the 60 threshold, the maximum OMZ thickness (summed thickness of the water below the threshold in a column) in metres to 1 decimal place, with its latitude and longitude to 2 decimal places.
- For GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, the percentage change in OMZ volume from historical to ssp245 at both thresholds, to 2 decimal places, positive for an expanding OMZ.
- The total dissolved oxygen inventory of the global ocean in moles, to 3 significant figures, for GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR historical and all three models for ssp245.
- At the 60 threshold, the OMZ volume in the Pacific, Indian and Atlantic Oceans (basins as defined by the WOA23 basin mask, marginal seas folded into their parent ocean; the Southern and Arctic Oceans are basins of their own and excluded) for WOA23 and the two historical models, in units of 1e15 cubic metres to 3 significant figures.
- For WOA23, the mean apparent oxygen utilization (micromoles per kilogram) and percent oxygen saturation within the OMZ at the 60 threshold, each to 1 decimal place, and what they imply about whether the zone is set by respiration or by poor ventilation.
- Which model reproduces the observed OMZ volume better, and whether the two models agree on the sign and size of the scenario change.

Expected visualizations:
- Figure 1, three global maps in one row of the historical OMZ thickness at the 60 threshold for GFDL-ESM4, MPI-ESM1-2-LR and WOA23, on one shared sequential colour scale in metres.
- Figure 2, three global maps in one row: the change in OMZ thickness at the 60 threshold (ssp245 2071-2100 minus historical 1971-2000) for GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR on one shared diverging colour scale centred on zero, and CESM2's ssp245 OMZ thickness on its own sequential scale, labelled to show that no change can be formed for CESM2.
