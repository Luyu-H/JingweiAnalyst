The user's question:
Check whether a mixed layer depth (MLD) computed from each CMIP6 model's own temperature and salinity reproduces the mlotst field that the same model publishes. This is an internal consistency test: both quantities come from the same simulation, so any disagreement is a property of the diagnostic, not of the model's physics.

Data to use:
- CMIP6 historical `thetao`, `so` and `mlotst` from CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, monthly, 1971-01 to 2000-12.
- Each model's own published cell areas (areacello).

Expected numerical results, comparing the 1971-2000 March and September climatologies of the operator MLD with those of the published mlotst, for each of the six model-and-month combinations. The operator MLD is the mixed layer depth computed offline from the model's own thetao and so with the density criterion that defines mlotst, the crossing depth interpolated between the bracketing levels. For each model and calendar month, both climatologies cover only the cells where both fields are defined in all 30 instances of that month (the 30 Marches, or the 30 Septembers):
- The mean difference, operator MLD minus mlotst, in metres to 1 decimal place.
- The root-mean-square difference, in metres to 1 decimal place.
- The spatial Pearson correlation between the two fields, to 3 decimal places.
- The percentage of the area where both fields are defined on which the absolute difference exceeds 20 m, to 1 decimal place, and the area where both fields are defined as a percentage of the model's total ocean area, to 1 decimal place.
- The ranking of the three models from most to least internally consistent, with the criterion used, the regions where the disagreement concentrates, and why the operator and mlotst are not expected to agree exactly.

Expected visualizations:
- Figure 1, a 3-by-2 panel of global maps of operator MLD minus mlotst, rows CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, columns March and September, all six panels drawn as maps on a common longitude-latitude frame (each panel a true geographic map, with curvilinear model fields put on a regular longitude-latitude grid for display only) with one shared diverging colour scale centred on zero.
- Figure 2, a 3-by-2 panel of scatter plots in the same layout, operator MLD on the x axis against mlotst on the y axis, one point per ocean cell, with logarithmic axes of identical range and ticks in every panel, a dashed y = x reference line, and each panel annotated with its bias, root-mean-square difference and correlation.

ds = "cmip6_historical"
models = ["CESM2", "GFDL-ESM4", "MPI-ESM1-2-LR"]
month_names = {3: "March", 9: "September"}
time_range = ["1971-01", "2000-12"]
basins = ["Atlantic", "Pacific", "Indian", "Southern", "Arctic"]
BIG = 1e10  # anything above this is an unmasked fill value (about 1e20)

results = {}        # (model, month name) -> dict of reported numbers
diff_native = {}    # native-grid difference fields (shared mask applied)
diff_map = {}       # difference regridded to a regular grid, for display only
scatter = {}        # (model, month name) -> (x, y) arrays, one point per shared cell
model_info = {}     # per-model reference depth and total ocean area
basin_tables = {}

for model in models:
    levels, bounds = load_depth_levels(dataset=ds, model=model, variable="thetao")
    ref_depth = float(np.asarray(levels)[0])  # shallowest level of this model

    thetao = load_regular_data(dataset=ds, variable="thetao", model=model, time_range=time_range)
    so = load_regular_data(dataset=ds, variable="so", model=model, time_range=time_range)
    mlotst = load_regular_data(dataset=ds, variable="mlotst", model=model, time_range=time_range)
    if "depth" in mlotst.dims:
        mlotst = mlotst.isel(depth=0, drop=True)

    # put so on thetao's vertical grid only if the two differ
    if (so.sizes["depth"] != thetao.sizes["depth"]) or (
            not np.allclose(so["depth"].values, thetao["depth"].values)):
        so = so.interp(depth=thetao["depth"])

    # the model's own areacello, on the model's own grid
    area = load_areas(dataset=ds, model=model, variable="thetao")
    total_area = float(area.sum().compute())
    model_info[model] = {"ref_depth_m": ref_depth, "total_ocean_area_m2": total_area,
                         "area_source": area.attrs.get("area_source")}

    for mnum, mname in month_names.items():
        idx = np.flatnonzero(thetao["time"].dt.month.values == mnum)
        t_m = thetao.isel(time=idx)
        s_m = so.isel(time=idx)
        idx2 = np.flatnonzero(mlotst["time"].dt.month.values == mnum)
        mlot_m = mlotst.isel(time=idx2)
        assert len(idx) == 30 and len(idx2) == 30

        # MLD of each monthly-mean profile (density criterion 0.03 kg m-3, reference depth = shallowest level)
        mld = compute_mixed_layer_depth(t_m, s_m, ref_depth=ref_depth, method="density",
                                        density_threshold=0.03, temperature_type="auto").compute()
        mld = mld.where(mld < BIG)
        mlot_m = mlot_m.where(mlot_m < BIG).compute()
        mlot_m = mlot_m.assign_coords(time=mld["time"])
        mld = mld.assign_attrs({**mlot_m.attrs, "units": "m", "long_name": "operator MLD (sigma0 +0.03)"})

        # area removed by excluding no-crossing columns (ocean at the surface in all 30 months, but no crossing found)
        ocean_surf = t_m.isel(depth=0).notnull().all("time").compute()
        nocross = ocean_surf & mld.isnull().any("time")
        nocross_area = float(area.where(nocross).sum().compute())

        # climatologies: MLD of each monthly profile first, then the mean over the 30 instances
        mld_clim = temporal_aggregate(mld, freq="mean").compute()
        mlot_clim = temporal_aggregate(mlot_m, freq="mean").compute()

        # shared mask: both fields valid in all 30 instances (same grid per model, so no regridding needed)
        mask = (mld.notnull() & mlot_m.notnull()).all("time")
        a = mld_clim.where(mask)
        b = mlot_clim.where(mask)
        mask_cells = int(mask.sum().compute())
        mask_area = float(area.where(mask).sum().compute())

        met = compute_difference_metrics(a, b)  # operator MLD minus mlotst, area-weighted by own areacello
        diff = met["difference"]
        corr = analyze_correlation(a, b, dim=["lat", "lon"], weighting="area")
        exc = compute_exceedance_fraction(diff, threshold=20.0, comparison="abs_greater", dim=["lat", "lon"])
        ext = locate_extreme(diff, op="abs_max")

        # basin breakdown of the disagreement
        rows = []
        for bname in basins:
            dB = select_basin(diff, basin=bname)
            if int(dB.notnull().sum().compute()) == 0:
                continue
            mB = float(compute_basic_stat(dB, op="area_weighted_mean", dim=["lat", "lon"]).compute())
            r2 = float(compute_basic_stat(dB ** 2, op="area_weighted_mean", dim=["lat", "lon"]).compute())
            eB = compute_exceedance_fraction(dB, threshold=20.0, comparison="abs_greater", dim=["lat", "lon"])
            rows.append({"basin": bname, "mean_diff_m": mB, "rmse_m": float(np.sqrt(r2)),
                         "pct_area_abs_diff_gt_20m": float(eB["fraction_percent"]),
                         "area_Mkm2": float(eB["total_amount"]) / 1e12})
        basin_tables[(model, mname)] = pd.DataFrame(rows)

        key = (model, mname)
        results[key] = {
            "mean_diff_m": float(met["bias"]),
            "rmse_m": float(met["rmse"]),
            "centered_rmse_m": float(met["centered_rmse"]),
            "mean_operator_m": float(met["mean_data"]),
            "mean_mlotst_m": float(met["mean_reference"]),
            "corr": float(corr["correlation"]),
            "pct_abs_diff_gt_20m": float(exc["fraction_percent"]),
            "mask_cells": mask_cells,
            "mask_area_m2": mask_area,
            "mask_pct_of_ocean_area": 100.0 * mask_area / total_area,
            "nocross_area_removed_m2": nocross_area,
            "nocross_area_removed_pct_of_ocean": 100.0 * nocross_area / total_area,
            "std_ratio": float(met["std_ratio"]),
            "max_abs_diff_m": ext["value"],
            "max_abs_diff_where": ext["coordinates"],
            "n_valid": met["n_valid"],
        }
        diff_native[key] = diff

        # regrid for display only (all numbers above remain on the native grid)
        diff_map[key] = regrid_horizontal(diff, target="woa23", method="conservative").compute()

        # scatter data: one point per shared-mask cell; log axes need positive values
        xv = a.values.ravel()
        yv = b.values.ravel()
        ok = np.isfinite(xv) & np.isfinite(yv)
        pos = ok & (xv > 0) & (yv > 0)
        scatter[key] = (xv[pos], yv[pos])
        results[key]["n_points_scatter"] = int(pos.sum())
        results[key]["n_points_nonpositive_dropped_from_scatter"] = int(ok.sum() - pos.sum())

    del thetao, so, mlotst

summary = pd.DataFrame(results).T
summary.index.names = ["model", "month"]

# ranking: mean RMSE over the two months (criterion), correlation and >20 m share as supporting criteria
rank_tbl = summary.reset_index().groupby("model")[["rmse_m", "corr", "pct_abs_diff_gt_20m", "mean_diff_m"]].mean()
rank_tbl = rank_tbl.sort_values("rmse_m")
rank_tbl["rank"] = np.arange(1, len(rank_tbl) + 1)
ranking_criterion = ("Lowest mean (March, September) area-weighted RMSE of operator MLD minus mlotst on the shared mask; "
                     "higher correlation and a smaller >20 m area share are supporting criteria.")

caveats = ("Operator MLD is computed from monthly-mean thetao and so, whereas mlotst is the time mean of instantaneous "
           "diagnostics; the MLD criterion is nonlinear, so exact agreement is not expected. Other causes: vertical "
           "interpolation and reference depth, equation-of-state differences, and differing encodings of no-crossing columns.")

print(pd.DataFrame(model_info).T)
print(summary.round(3).to_string())
print(rank_tbl.round(3))

## 1. Direct answer

For all three models, the MLD computed offline from each model's own monthly-mean `thetao` and `so` reproduces the published `mlotst` closely in pattern. Spatial correlations are 0.945–0.998. ## 2. What was actually done

- **Data:** `load_regular_data` from `cmip6_historical` for `thetao`, `so` and `mlotst`, monthly, 1971-01 to 2000-12, global. Each model was kept on its own grid, and metrics were never computed on a regridded field. If `mlotst` had a depth dimension, its first level was taken. If `so` was on a different vertical grid from `thetao`, it was interpolated to the `thetao` depths.
- **Reference depth:** each model's shallowest `thetao` level from `load_depth_levels`.
  - CESM2: 5.0 m
  - GFDL-ESM4: 2.5 m
  - MPI-ESM1-2-LR: 6.0 m
- **Areas:** `load_areas`, which returns each model's own published `areacello`. Total ocean areas are 3.605×10¹⁴ m² (CESM2), 3.611×10¹⁴ m² (GFDL-ESM4) and 3.613×10¹⁴ m² (MPI-ESM1-2-LR).
- **Operator MLD:** for each model, the 30 Marches and 30 Septembers were selected. `compute_mixed_layer_depth` was called with `method="density"`, `density_threshold=0.03` (kg m⁻³), `ref_depth` set to the model's shallowest level, and `temperature_type="auto"`. The density and salinity handling is done inside that tool; the script does not convert salinity itself. This was applied to each monthly-mean profile, so **monthly-mean T and S were used and the MLD was computed first**. The 30 MLDs were then averaged with `temporal_aggregate(freq="mean")`, a plain mean over years. `mlotst`, the time mean of instantaneous diagnostics, was averaged over the same 30 months in the same way. Values ≥1e10 (unmasked fill) were set to NaN in both fields.
- **No-crossing columns:** I take them to be NaN in the tool's output, so they drop out of the mask. The code defines the "area removed" as cells that are ocean at the surface in all 30 months but have a NaN operator MLD in at least one month. The script has no per-model handling of no-crossing encodings (such as 0 m in CESM2). The scatter step found no non-positive values among shared-mask cells (0 dropped).
- **Shared mask:** a cell is kept only if both the operator MLD and `mlotst` are non-NaN in all 30 instances of that month. This was built directly from the two fields and applied with `.where(mask)`; `build_comparison_mask` was not called. Because both fields are on the same grid within a model, no regridding was needed.
- **Metrics:**
  - `compute_difference_metrics(operator, mlotst)` gives bias and RMSE.
  - `analyze_correlation(..., weighting="area")` gives the spatial correlation.
  - `compute_exceedance_fraction(diff, threshold=20, comparison="abs_greater")` gives the >20 m share.
  - Area weights were not passed explicitly in these calls, so the weighting is handled inside the tools.
  - `locate_extreme(op="abs_max")` finds the largest difference.

| Rank | Model | Mean RMSE (m) | Mean correlation | Mean >20 m share (%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CESM2 | 6.3 | 0.998 | 1.4 |
| 2 | MPI-ESM1-2-LR | 10.6 | 0.989 | 2.5 |
| 3 | GFDL-ESM4 | 17.9 | 0.966 | 6.9 |

All three criteria give the same order. In March alone, MPI-ESM1-2-LR (6.8 m) is marginally lower than CESM2 (7.5 m). MPI's September RMSE (14.3 m) is much larger, which is why it ranks second overall.

**Where the differences concentrate** (from `locate_extreme` and the basin tables):
- **North Atlantic and Labrador Sea in March** (deep convection):
  - CESM2 has its largest difference (−373 m) at about 59.7°N, 58.6°W. The Atlantic basin RMSE is 12.3 m, with 11% of the area above 20 m.
  - MPI's March maximum (−434 m) is at about 54°N, 51.8°W.
  - GFDL-ESM4's March maximum (+814 m) is at 54.5°N, 10.5°W. Its Atlantic RMSE is 34.3 m, with 15.6% of the area above 20 m.
- **Arctic in March:** GFDL-ESM4 has an RMSE of 25.9 m with 10.9% of the area above 20 m. MPI has an RMSE of 13.9 m with 5.4% above 20 m.
- **Southern Ocean in September:**
  - MPI has an RMSE of 37.4 m and a bias of −14.0 m, with 13.7% of the area above 20 m. Its largest difference (−698 m) is at about 72°S, 139°W.
  - GFDL-ESM4 has an RMSE of 20.0 m, with 12.1% of the area above 20 m.
  - GFDL-ESM4's largest September difference (+432 m) is at about 37.5°S, 139.5°E.
  - CESM2's Southern Ocean September RMSE is only 1.9 m.
- **GFDL-ESM4 Indian Ocean in September:** RMSE of 18.1 m, with 12.5% of the area above 20 m.

**Why exact agreement is not expected:**
- The operator MLD was computed from monthly-mean `thetao` and `so`, whereas `mlotst` is the time mean of instantaneous diagnostics. The threshold criterion is nonlinear, so the MLD of the mean profile is not the mean of the MLDs.
- Vertical interpolation and the reference depth differ.
- The equation of state may differ.
- No-crossing columns are encoded differently across models.

The consistently negative bias is plausibly linked to the monthly-mean averaging, but that was not tested separately.

## 6. Caveats

- The metrics are area-weighted means on the native grid of each model. Cell counts are not comparable across models because the grids differ.
- Figure 1 uses the regridded field for display only. GFDL-ESM4 is already on a regular 1° grid, so its displayed field is effectively the native one.
- No-crossing columns are excluded only to the extent that the tool returns NaN for them. The script has no model-specific treatment of 0 m or full-column-depth encodings, so any such values that fall inside the shared mask would still be counted.
