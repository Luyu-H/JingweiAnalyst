### 3. Assessment against WOA23 and the denitrification low-N:P signal

The observed WOA23 intermediate-water stoichiometry is clearly sub-Redfield: headline mean N:P is 14.22 and the ratio of mean nitrate to mean phosphate is 14.00. Its OLS slope is 12.42 with an intercept of 2.97 and R² = 0.890, so the NO₃–PO₄ relationship is fairly tight but systematically below the 16:1 Redfield line. Only 1.9% of the global 200–1000 m water volume has N:P < 12, with a somewhat larger low-N:P fraction in the Indian Ocean (4.4%) than in the Pacific (1.7%), consistent with localized denitrification imprints.

All three models are also below Redfield, but they do not reproduce the observed low-N:P volume and basin distribution well:

- **CESM2** is far too low in N:P, with a headline mean of 11.75 and basin means of 11.27 in the Pacific and 11.04 in the Indian Ocean. It has a very poor NO₃–PO₄ linear relationship (slope 2.39, R² = 0.061) and produces a very large low-N:P volume: 38.1% globally, 47.0% in the Pacific, and 40.4% in the Indian Ocean. The low-N:P signal is therefore much too widespread and too strong.

- **GFDL-ESM4** is the closest to WOA23 in overall mean N:P (14.05) and regression structure (slope 11.10, intercept 4.40, R² = 0.809). However, it still fails the observed low-N:P basin distribution: it has 8.0% global N:P < 12 and 13.3% in the Pacific, much higher than observed, while almost no low-N:P volume in the Indian Ocean (0.3% vs 4.4% observed). Its Indian basin mean N:P (14.69) is also higher than observed.

- **MPI-ESM1-2-LR** has a headline mean N:P of 12.35 and basin means of 11.93 in the Pacific and 12.63 in the Indian Ocean, all below WOA23. It produces 21.7% global N:P < 12, 30.9% in the Pacific, and 11.5% in the Indian Ocean. Thus it greatly overproduces low-N:P water, particularly in the Pacific, even though the Indian Ocean also has more low-N:P volume than observed. Its regression is intermediate (slope 7.98, intercept 6.60, R² = 0.653) but clearly below the observed slope.

Overall, none of the three models reproduces the observed WOA23 low-N:P volume and its basin pattern. CESM2 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR generate far too much sub-12 N:P water, especially in the Pacific, while GFDL-ESM4 is closest in bulk mean and regression but misplaces the low-N:P signal, overproducing it in the Pacific and almost eliminating it in the Indian Ocean.
