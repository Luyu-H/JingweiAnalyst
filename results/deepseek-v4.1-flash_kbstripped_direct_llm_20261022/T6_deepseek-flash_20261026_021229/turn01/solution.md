Using the WOA23 annual objectively analysed nitrate climatology and 1971–2000 climatological means from the three CMIP6 historical simulations, the nitracline depth was defined as the depth of the maximum vertical nitrate gradient within the top 1000 m, with the gradient at 1000 m allowed to use the next deeper level.

**Global mean nitracline depth**

| Source | Global mean depth (m) | Difference vs WOA23 (m) |
|---|---:|---:|
| WOA23 | 166 | — |
| CESM2 | 147 | −19 |
| GFDL-ESM4 | 134 | −32 |
| MPI-ESM1-2-LR | 135 | −31 |

All three models place the global nitracline too shallow: CESM2 by about 19 m, GFDL-ESM4 by about 32 m, and MPI-ESM1-2-LR by about 31 m. CESM2 has the smallest global mean bias.

**Basin mean nitracline depth**

| Source | Atlantic (m) | Pacific (m) | Indian (m) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| WOA23 | 147 | 182 | 156 |
| CESM2 | 108 | 159 | 169 |
| GFDL-ESM4 | 92 | 149 | 157 |
| MPI-ESM1-2-LR | 100 | 165 | 109 |

**Model minus WOA23 differences**

| Model | Atlantic (m) | Pacific (m) | Indian (m) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| CESM2 | −39 | −23 | +14 |
| GFDL-ESM4 | −55 | −34 | +2 |
| MPI-ESM1-2-LR | −47 | −17 | −47 |

All models strongly underestimate the Atlantic nitracline depth, by 39–55 m. All three also underestimate the Pacific nitracline depth, by 17–34 m. For the Indian Ocean, CESM2 is 14 m too deep, GFDL-ESM4 is almost unbiased (+2 m), while MPI-ESM1-2-LR is much too shallow (−47 m).

**Spatial comparison on the WOA23 1° grid**

| Model | Pearson correlation | RMSE (m) |
|---|---:|---:|
| CESM2 | 0.393 | 151.4 |
| GFDL-ESM4 | 0.509 | 145.4 |
| MPI-ESM1-2-LR | 0.608 | 139.5 |

**Best model and basin ordering**

MPI-ESM1-2-LR performs best in terms of spatial correlation (0.608) and RMSE (139.5 m), and it is the only model that reproduces the observed basin ordering: Pacific > Indian > Atlantic. However, it still has a large shallow bias globally and a very large shallow bias in the Indian Ocean.

CESM2 has the closest global mean nitracline depth to WOA23, but its spatial correlation is lowest and its basin ordering is Indian > Pacific > Atlantic, which does not match the observed ordering. GFDL-ESM4 also reverses the Pacific and Indian ordering, and it has the largest global shallow bias.

Thus, **MPI-ESM1-2-LR is the best overall model for the spatial pattern and basin ordering**, despite substantial regional biases.
