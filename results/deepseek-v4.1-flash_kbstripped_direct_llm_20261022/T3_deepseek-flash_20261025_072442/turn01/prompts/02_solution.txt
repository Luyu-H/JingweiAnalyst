The user's question:
Evaluate the 1971-2000 sea surface temperature (SST) climatology of three CMIP6 historical simulations against the World Ocean Atlas 2023 (WOA23) observational climatology, and describe the spatial structure of the bias.

Data to use:
- CMIP6 historical variable `tos` from CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR, monthly, from 1971-01 to 2000-12, each on its own native grid with its own published cell areas (areacello).
- WOA23 temperature, the 1971-2000 climate normal, annual time slice, objectively analysed field, at 0 m depth, on the 1-degree grid.
- The WOA23 1-degree land-sea mask (landsea_01), in which a cell is ocean when its value is greater than 1.

Expected numerical results:
- For each model, the global mean of its 1971-2000 SST climatology over its own native ocean grid, in degrees Celsius to 2 decimal places.
- The global mean of the WOA23 0 m annual field over the whole 1-degree grid, in degrees Celsius to 2 decimal places.
- On the WOA23 1-degree grid, over the cells on which the models are compared with WOA23: the mean SST of each model and of WOA23, in degrees Celsius to 2 decimal places.
- On the same cells, for each model: the mean bias delta_SST (model minus WOA23) in degrees Celsius to 2 decimal places, the RMSE against WOA23 in degrees Celsius to 3 decimal places, and the spatial Pearson correlation with WOA23 to 3 decimal places.
- The ranking of the three models from smallest to largest absolute delta_SST.

- Figure 1, a 2-by-2 panel of global maps: the bias fields (model minus WOA23) of CESM2, GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR on the cells of that comparison with one shared diverging colour scale centred on zero, and the WOA23 0 m reference SST on the same cells with a sequential colour scale. def files_in(model, name):
    d = os.path.join(CMIP_ROOT, model, name)
    fs = sorted(glob.glob(os.path.join(d, name + "*.nc")))
    if not fs:
        fs = sorted(glob.glob(os.path.join(d, "*.nc")))

def open_var(model, name):
    fs = files_in(model, name)
    if not fs:
        return None, None
    if len(fs) == 1:
        ds = xr.open_dataset(fs[0], use_cftime=True)
    else:

    tos = ds["tos"]
    if "height" in tos.dims:

    years = tos.time.dt.year
    keep  = np.where(((years >= 1971) & (years <= 2000)).values)[0]
    tos   = tos.isel(time=keep)

    # climatological mean (all months 1971-2000)
    tos_clim = tos.mean("time").compute()

    # native cell areas
    ads, _ = open_var(model, "areacello")
    area = ads["areacello"].squeeze().compute()

    # ocean/land mask: CESM2 needs sftof, the others publish pre-masked areas
    sds, _ = open_var(model, "sftof")

    valid = np.isfinite(tvals) & np.isfinite(avals) & (avals > 0) & sea

    native_global_mean[model] = float(np.nansum(tvals * avals * valid) /

    # 2-D lat/lon of the native grid
    latname = ds["tos"].dims[1]

    lat2  = np.asarray(ds[lat_c].values, dtype=float)

# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
# 4) Regrid each model climatology onto the WOA23 1-degree grid
# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
def regrid_to_target(lat2, lon2, data, tlat, tlon):
    lon2 = np.mod(lon2, 360.0)
    m = np.isfinite(data) & np.isfinite(lat2) & np.isfinite(lon2)
    x, y, z = lon2[m], lat2[m], data[m]
    # longitude halo so the Delaunay triangulation is periodic
    xs = np.concatenate([x - 360.0, x, x + 360.0])
    ys = np.concatenate([y, y, y])
    zs = np.concatenate([z, z, z])
    TLON, TLAT = np.meshgrid(np.mod(tlon, 360.0), tlat)
    out = griddata((xs, ys), zs, (TLON, TLAT), method="linear")
    need = ~np.isfinite(out)
    if need.any():
        out[need] = griddata((xs, ys), zs, (TLON[need], TLAT[need]), method="nearest")
    return out

for model in MODELS:

    # a target cell only counts if the nearest native ocean point is reasonably close
