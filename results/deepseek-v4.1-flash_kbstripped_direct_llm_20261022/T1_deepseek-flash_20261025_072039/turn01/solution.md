The Nino 3.4 index was defined as the area-weighted SST anomaly over 5°S–5°N, 170°W–120°W, relative to the 1950–2014 monthly climatology of each source. Standard deviations use the population definition (ddof = 0) over all 780 months.

## Model amplitude bias and ratio against ERSSTv5

Bias = model SD minus ERSSTv5 SD; ratio = model SD divided by ERSSTv5 SD.

| Model | SD (°C) | Bias (°C) | Ratio | Absolute-bias rank |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| MPI-ESM1-2-LR | 0.85 | 0.01 | 1.01 | 1 |
| GFDL-ESM4 | 1.00 | 0.15 | 1.18 | 2 |
| CESM2 | 1.29 | 0.44 | 1.52 | 3 |

Ranking from smallest to largest absolute amplitude bias: **MPI-ESM1-2-LR < GFDL-ESM4 < CESM2**. MPI-ESM1-2-LR reproduces the observed overall amplitude almost exactly; GFDL-ESM4 is moderately stronger; CESM2 is substantially too strong, with about 1.52× the observed Nino 3.4 SD.

## Peak/minimum seasonal amplitude and phase locking

| Source | Peak month | Peak SD (°C) | Minimum month | Minimum SD (°C) | Peak/min ratio |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| ERSSTv5 | Dec | 1.12 | Apr | 0.60 | 1.88 |
| CESM2 | Jan | 1.62 | Jun | 0.94 | 1.72 |
| GFDL-ESM4 | Dec | 1.25 | Jul | 0.75 | 1.67 |
| MPI-ESM1-2-LR | Dec | 1.03 | Jul | 0.71 | 1.45 |

The observed seasonal phase locking peaks in **December** with a peak-to-minimum amplitude ratio of **1.88**.

- **CESM2:** Does **not** reproduce the observed peak month; its maximum occurs in **January** rather than December. Its peak-to-minimum ratio is **1.72**, weaker than the observed **1.88** by 0.16.
- **GFDL-ESM4:** Reproduces the observed peak month, **December**, but has weaker seasonal phase locking, with a ratio of **1.67** versus the observed **1.88** (weaker by 0.21).
- **MPI-ESM1-2-LR:** Also reproduces the observed peak month, **December**, but its seasonal phase locking is substantially weaker, with a ratio of **1.45** versus **1.88** (weaker by 0.43).

Overall, MPI-ESM1-2-LR has the closest total ENSO amplitude to ERSSTv5, followed by GFDL-ESM4, while CESM2 strongly overestimates ENSO amplitude. However, all three models somewhat underrepresent the observed seasonal peak-to-minimum amplitude contrast; only GFDL-ESM4 and MPI-ESM1-2-LR place the maximum in the observed December, whereas CESM2 peaks in January.
